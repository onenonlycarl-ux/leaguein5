New Orleans Saints

3-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Saints beat Browns in overtime, eye first place

Tyler Shough led an 11-play, 74-yard overtime drive, and New Orleans beat Cleveland 23-22 for its first home win of 2026. The defense stopped a two-point try on the final play. The Saints, now 3-5, share the NFC South lead with Carolina and have won two of their last three. They also traded defensive tackle Davon Godchaux to Jacksonville.

Source: New Orleans Saints ↗

2
Injury Report

Center Erik McCoy out, Etienne closer to return

McCoy (biceps) will not play, so Cesar Ruiz slides to center and rookie Jeremiah Wright starts at right guard. Running back Travis Etienne Jr. (hamstring), on injured reserve, practiced on a limited basis and could play for the first time in six games. Alvin Kamara was a full participant Thursday. Tackle Kelvin Banks Jr. (ankle) and cornerback Martin Emerson Jr. (shin) were limited.

Source: The Ticket (Associated Press) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Carl Granderson keeps piling up multi-sack games

Granderson had two sacks against Cleveland, his fourth straight game with multiple sacks. The team says he is the first NFL player to do that since 2013. He has 7.5 sacks this season, one behind teammate Chase Young, who has eight.

Source: New Orleans Saints ↗

4
Stat of the Day

2020: last Saints overtime win before the Browns game

The 23-22 win over Cleveland was New Orleans' first overtime victory since 2020 and moved its all-time overtime record to 16-16. The Saints outrushed the Browns 160-95 and sacked them five times.

Source: New Orleans Saints ↗

5
What's Next

Panthers visit the Superdome Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on FOX

The winner takes sole possession of first place in the NFC South. The Saints won both meetings last season, 17-7 in Carolina and 20-17 at home. Watch the interior line without McCoy and whether Etienne returns to the backfield.
