New Orleans Saints

3-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Shough lifts Saints past Cleveland in OT

Tyler Shough hit Juwan Johnson for a 15-yard touchdown on the first overtime drive, and New Orleans stopped Cleveland's two-point try to win 23-22. It was the Saints' first home win of the season and puts them at 3-5, level with every other team in the NFC South.

Source: WLOX ↗

2
Injury Report

Kamara, McCoy and Donaldson sit Wednesday

Running back Alvin Kamara (rest, back), center Erik McCoy (biceps) and running back CJ Donaldson (shoulder) did not practice Wednesday. Travis Etienne (hamstring) and tight end Noah Fant (back) were limited.

Source: Saints.com injury report ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Shough wins it with his arm and legs

Shough went 21-of-35 for 265 yards and a touchdown with no interceptions, and he ran 10 times for 51 yards. He led an 11-play, 74-yard drive in overtime for the winning score.

Source: Saints.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

4: straight games with multiple sacks for Granderson

Carl Granderson is the only Saint to record multiple sacks in four consecutive games, and the first NFL player to do it since 2013. New Orleans sacked Cleveland five times in all.

Source: Saints.com ↗

5
What's Next

Panthers come to the Superdome

New Orleans hosts Carolina on Sunday, Nov. 15 at noon CT (1 p.m. ET) on Fox. New kicker Charlie Smyth made three of four field goals in his debut after the Saints released Daniel Carlson, and the winner of this game takes sole possession of first place.
