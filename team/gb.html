Biggest Story

Packers carry a broken run game into Foxborough

Green Bay ranks 32nd in EPA per rush and has used 11 different offensive linemen this season. The 5-3 Packers hold the seventh seed in the NFC. They have lost at Detroit 20-17 and at Minnesota 39-22 this year and now face New England on Sunday.

Injury Report

Packers rule out Micah Parsons and four others vs. Patriots

Parsons (knee), linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin), lineman Travis Glover (knee), lineman Sean Rhyan (ankle) and tight end Josh Whyle (concussion) are out. Cornerback Keisean Nixon (groin) and running backs MarShawn Lloyd (knee) and Chris Brooks (ankle) are questionable.

Player Spotlight

Jordan Love keeps protecting the ball and the pocket

Love is 192 of 312 (61.5 percent) for 2,246 yards, 16 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. His sack rate of 4.3 percent is sixth best in the league. Christian Watson leads his targets with 42 catches for 708 yards and 6 touchdowns.

Green Bay's kicker has made 11 of 16 field goal tries this season. That is the lowest success rate among the league's regular kickers.
