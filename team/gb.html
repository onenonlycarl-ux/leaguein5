Green Bay Packers

6-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Rams run past Packers 26-13 and take the tiebreaker

Green Bay erased a 10-point deficit to tie the game at halftime, then got shut out 13-0 in the second half. Both teams entered at 6-4, and the Rams now hold the head-to-head edge in the wild-card race. Matt LaFleur called the rest of the schedule a one-game season.

Source: Packers.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Christian Watson in concussion protocol after sideline hit

Watson had four catches for 65 yards and a touchdown before a hit to the head late in the first half. LaFleur said Watson seems to be in a good place but did not know where he stands in the protocol. He could miss Week 13 if he does not clear it.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Micah Parsons returns from knee injury with a sack

Parsons played on a snap count in his first game back from the knee injury that sidelined him in December. He recorded a sack late in the first half and added several pressures. Green Bay needs that pass rush to hold up with six games left.

Source: Packers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

55: total yards the Packers gathered in the second half

The offense produced two first downs after halftime, and Jordan Love threw two interceptions that he called bad reads. The Rams ran 71 plays to Green Bay's 44 and held the ball for 40:40. Blake Corum ran 32 times for 184 yards.

Source: Packers.com ↗

5
What's Next

At Saints, Dec. 6, 1 p.m. ET on FOX

Green Bay plays at the Caesars Superdome in Week 13. The Packers lead the all-time series 19-10. Watch Watson's protocol status and whether the offense fixes its second-half problems.
