Green Bay Packers

6-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Packers lose 26-13 to the Rams, their second straight

Green Bay fell to 6-5 in the first Thanksgiving Eve game in league history. The Packers managed 244 yards, committed three turnovers and held the ball for just 19:20. They now sit ninth in the NFC, one spot out of the final playoff seed.

Source: Forbes ↗

2
Injury Report

Watson in concussion protocol, Zeitler hurts ankle

Wide receiver Christian Watson left in the first half after a helmet-to-helmet hit and was evaluated for a concussion. Guard Kevin Zeitler also left with an ankle injury and did not return. Neither had a confirmed return date after the game.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Parsons makes his season debut with a sack

Micah Parsons returned from his knee injury on a snap count and sacked Matthew Stafford in the first half. Forbes credits him with one sack, two quarterback hits and a tackle for loss.

Source: Forbes ↗

4
Stat of the Day

241: rushing yards the Packers allowed to the Rams

Blake Corum ran for a career-high 184 yards and a touchdown as Los Angeles held the ball for more than 40 minutes. Green Bay ran 44 plays to the Rams' 71 and was outscored 13-0 in the second half.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Packers visit the Saints on Dec. 6

Green Bay plays at New Orleans on Sunday, Dec. 6. Kickoff time and network were not in the reports I found. Watch the run defense and whether Watson clears the protocol.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗
