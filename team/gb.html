NFC North · Wednesday, November 18 · Week 11

Green Bay Packers

6-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

LaFleur says protection was bad, but no staff changes

Green Bay lost 30-10 to Minnesota at Lambeau Field and gave up eight sacks. Matt LaFleur called the pass protection pretty bad and said some of his play calls should have been different. Offensive coordinator Adam Stenavich and line coach Luke Butkus stay.

Source: Packers.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Parsons has a few hurdles left before Rams game

Micah Parsons, out since a left knee ACL and meniscus tear last December, has had his 21-day practice window opened. LaFleur says the plan is a return in Week 12, but the team reassesses at week's end. Guard Kevin Zeitler (triceps) lost his first start to a practice injury, and Skyy Moore's ankle needs more evaluation.

Source: TSN ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Love absorbs eight sacks in Minnesota loss

Jordan Love went 17 of 27 for 220 yards, one touchdown and one interception. He took a dozen hits, and Minnesota pressured him on 21 of 35 dropbacks.

Source: Vikings.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

57: first-half yards for the Packers offense

Green Bay finished with 220 total yards and went 3 of 10 on third down. It did not score until trailing 20-0 late in the third quarter. Four turnovers fed the Vikings' lead.

Source: Packers.com ↗

5
What's Next

Bye Sunday, then at the Rams on Nov. 25

Green Bay is off in Week 11 and plays at the Los Angeles Rams on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 7 p.m. on Netflix. Both teams are 6-4. Watch whether Parsons makes his season debut.

Source: SI.com ↗
