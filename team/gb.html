Biggest Story

Packers head into bye after 30-10 loss to Minnesota

Green Bay trailed 17-0 at halftime and fell to 6-4, last place in the NFC North. Matt LaFleur called the offensive line performance bad and said the game plan should have gotten the ball out faster. The Vikings swept the season series.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Jacobs has a broken finger, Rhyan an ankle injury

Running back Josh Jacobs is sidelined with a broken finger. Center Sean Rhyan missed the Minnesota game with an ankle injury and may not be ready for the Rams. The bye week gives both extra time.

Source: Packers Talk ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jordan Love took a beating against Minnesota

Love went down eight times and took 12 hits against the Vikings. Green Bay had 57 yards of offense at halftime and four turnovers for the game. The week before, the offense scored 45 points at New England.

Source: Packers Talk ↗

4
Stat of the Day

8: sacks the Vikings recorded at Lambeau

Seven Vikings shared the eight sacks, the most the franchise has recorded at Lambeau Field. Minnesota also took the ball away four times and outscored Green Bay 69-32 across the two meetings.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

What's Next

Bye, then the Rams on Thanksgiving Eve

Green Bay is on its bye this week. It visits the Los Angeles Rams (6-4) on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 8:00 p.m. ET on Netflix. The Rams are also coming off a bye.
