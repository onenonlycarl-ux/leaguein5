NFC North · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Green Bay Packers

6-3Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Packers rout Patriots 45-27 behind Jordan Love

Green Bay beat New England 45-27 at Gillette Stadium and has won five of its last six. The defense forced four turnovers that turned into 21 points. The Patriots gave up a franchise-record 45 points at home. Green Bay gained 478 yards in all.

Source: AP via Your Valley ↗

2
Injury Report

Josh Jacobs needs surgery on fractured finger

Running back Josh Jacobs fractured the ring finger on his right hand in the first quarter and needs surgery. Matt LaFleur said Jacobs had no grip strength after trying tape and then a cast. Injured reserve is not ruled out, which would mean at least four games. Linebacker Ty'Ron Hopper left with a chest injury but LaFleur does not expect him to miss time.

Source: TSN ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jordan Love throws for 372 and accounts for four scores

Love threw for 372 yards and three touchdowns and added a rushing touchdown. Christian Watson caught seven passes for 171 yards and a score, and Matthew Golden had 10 for 164 and a touchdown. They are the first Packers teammates since 1942 to each top 150 receiving yards and score in the same game.

Source: AP via Your Valley ↗

4
Stat of the Day

1962: last time Green Bay scored 45 with 450 yards

The Packers had not scored 45 or more points while gaining 450 or more yards since 1962. They did it Sunday despite entering the day last in the NFL in rushing offense. They ran for 129 yards, their first game this season above 86.

Source: AP via Your Valley ↗

What's Next

Vikings visit Lambeau before the Week 11 bye

Green Bay hosts Minnesota on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 1 p.m. ET on FOX at Lambeau Field, then takes its bye. Watch the running back room without Jacobs, and whether Kaleb Johnson, who ran for 68 yards on 14 carries Sunday, keeps the job.
