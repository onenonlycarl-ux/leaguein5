AFC West · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Denver Broncos

7-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Broncos hold off Panthers 26-21 for a fifth straight win

Denver went to Charlotte and won a game that turned on two fourth-quarter plays. A Sean Payton challenge flipped a Carolina goal-line sequence into an interception, and Nik Bonitto's strip-sack with 1:24 left sealed it. The Broncos sit alone atop the AFC West at 7-2.

Source: Live 5 News (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Waddle and Moss leave Carolina game with concussions

Wide receiver Jaylen Waddle took a hit to the helmet on a catch attempt and went to the locker room, where doctors diagnosed a concussion. Cornerback Riley Moss also suffered one in the first half. Both were ruled out, and the bye week buys each of them extra time to clear the protocol.

Source: ClutchPoints ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bonitto's strip-sack ends it for Denver

Nik Bonitto forced and recovered a fumble from Bryce Young in the final two minutes, his first strip-sack of the season. Bo Nix then converted a third-down sneak to run out the clock. Nix finished with two touchdown passes, one of them to Pat Bryant in his first game back from injured reserve.

Source: DenverBroncos.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

16: straight one-score wins, an NFL record

Denver has now won 16 consecutive regular-season games decided by one score, a league record. The Panthers game fit the pattern: the Broncos outscored Carolina 13-7 in the fourth quarter. Denver is 3-2 on the road this season.

Source: DenverBroncos.com ↗

5
What's Next

Bye in Week 10, then the Raiders at home

Denver has no game this weekend. The Broncos return on Sunday, Nov. 22 to host Las Vegas, and they will get a full two weeks to heal. Watch whether Waddle and Moss clear concussion protocol before that game.
