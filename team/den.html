Denver Broncos

8-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Payton suspends Dobbins for Black Friday trip to Pittsburgh

Sean Payton announced Tuesday that running back J.K. Dobbins will miss the game at Pittsburgh for conduct detrimental to the team. Dobbins left the facility after showing up that day, and Payton declined to explain further. ESPN notes he is believed to be the first Broncos player suspended midseason for that reason since Brandon Marshall in 2009.

Source: ESPN ↗

2
Injury Report

Three Broncos ruled out for the Steelers game

Denver's final report lists defensive lineman Sai'Vion Jones (hamstring), safety Taylor Rapp (quadriceps) and tight end Caleb Lohner (foot) as out. Running back Tyler Badie (thumb) is questionable and still on injured reserve. Denver also lost Dobbins to suspension, which thins the backfield further.

Source: Steelers Depot ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie Jonah Coleman earns AFC Offensive Player of the Week

Coleman ran 24 times for 111 yards and three touchdowns in the win over Las Vegas, plus four catches for 18 yards. The fourth-round pick is the first Broncos offensive player honored this season. Payton is watching his workload on a short week.

Source: Heavy ↗

4
Stat of the Day

38: points Denver put on the Raiders last Sunday

The Broncos beat Las Vegas 38-24 in their last game and sit 8-2 overall, 4-1 in the AFC and 3-2 on the road. They now head to a Steelers team that is 4-0 at home.

Source: CBS Sports ↗

What's Next

Broncos at Steelers today, 3 p.m. ET on Prime Video

Denver visits Acrisure Stadium on Black Friday against a 6-4 Pittsburgh team that waived Aaron Rodgers on Wednesday and will start Mason Rudolph. Rain and wind are in the forecast. Watch how Denver splits carries between Coleman and Badie.
