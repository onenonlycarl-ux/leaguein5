Denver Broncos

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Broncos rally past Chiefs to take sole possession of first place

Denver erased an 11-point fourth-quarter deficit to beat Kansas City 28-24 and improve to 6-2. Bo Nix threw two touchdowns, including a fourth-down score to Nate Adkins. The win dropped the Chiefs out of first place in the AFC West.

Source: NFL.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Three Broncos out at Carolina, Hufanga questionable

Cornerback Kris Abrams-Draine, safety Brandon Jones and lineman Eyioma Uwazurike are out after three straight days without practice. Safety Talanoa Hufanga is questionable after limited work, his first practices since a Week 5 ankle injury. Receiver Pat Bryant practiced fully and can be activated from injured reserve.

Source: DenverBroncos.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bo Nix keeps winning the fourth quarter

Nix went 20 of 32 for 222 yards and two touchdowns against Kansas City. Since he entered the league in 2024 he leads the NFL with 15 game-winning drives and 12 fourth-quarter comebacks. He has not thrown an interception in five straight games.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

164: Nix's pass attempts without an interception

Nix's current streak of 164 straight attempts without a pick ranks sixth in Broncos history. His five straight games without an interception tie for the second-longest run in team history.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

5
What's Next

At Carolina Sunday, then the bye

Denver visits the Panthers on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS. Watch the receiver room, where Bryant could return to join Jaylen Waddle and Courtland Sutton. The Broncos then take their Week 10 bye before hosting the Raiders.
