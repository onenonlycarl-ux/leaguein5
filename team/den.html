Denver Broncos

7-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Broncos beat Panthers 26-21 for a fifth straight win

Denver went to Charlotte and won 26-21 to reach 7-2, with three straight road wins. A Sean Payton challenge turned a Carolina catch at the goal line into a Devon Key interception, and the Broncos drove 80 yards for a touchdown after it. Nik Bonitto's late strip-sack closed the game before Denver heads into its bye.

Source: Denver7 (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Waddle and Moss leave Carolina game with concussions

Wide receiver Jaylen Waddle took a hit from safety Zakee Wheatley on a downfield pass and left in the first half with a concussion. Cornerback Riley Moss also suffered a concussion and did not return. Pat Bryant, back from injured reserve, led the Denver receivers with 56 yards. The bye gives both concussed players extra time before the Nov. 22 game.

Source: TSN (Canadian Press) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bonitto's strip-sack seals Denver's win in Charlotte

Nik Bonitto stripped Bryce Young with under 90 seconds left and Denver recovered at its own 44. It was Bonitto's first strip-sack of the season. Bo Nix then scored on a one-yard sneak three plays later to put the game away.

Source: Denver7 (AP) ↗

4
Stat of the Day

16: straight one-score wins, an NFL record

Denver has won 16 consecutive regular-season games decided by one score, the longest such streak in NFL history. The Panthers game fit the pattern, with the Broncos taking the lead in the fourth quarter. They also converted 6 of 10 third downs, their best mark of the year.

Source: DenverBroncos.com ↗

5
What's Next

Broncos rest in Week 10, then host the Raiders Nov. 22

Denver is on its bye this weekend. The Broncos return home on Sunday, Nov. 22 at 4:25 p.m. ET on CBS against the Las Vegas Raiders. Kansas City sits half a game back in the AFC West, so the division race stays tight through the break.
