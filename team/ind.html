Indianapolis Colts

3-8Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Colts lose 26-3 and fail to reach the end zone

Indianapolis scored on its opening drive, then did not score again until the fourth quarter. It was the first game this season without a touchdown. The Colts are 3-8.

Source: Colts.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Both starting tackles sit, McGowan leaves with hamstring

Bernhard Raimann (concussion) and Jalen Travis (knee) were inactive, as were cornerbacks Sauce Gardner (calf) and Charvarius Ward (knee). Running back Seth McGowan hurt a hamstring late in the second quarter and did not return.

Source: Colts.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Daniel Jones struggles behind a patched line

Jones completed 12 of 27 passes for 144 yards with one interception, a 45.9 passer rating. Anthony Richardson entered midway through the fourth quarter and went 0 for 2 as a passer.

Source: Colts.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

1.9: Jonathan Taylor's yards per carry against Houston

Taylor's average was the second lowest of his career. The Colts have also allowed 608 rushing yards over their last four games, 151.8 per game.

Source: ESPN ↗

5
What's Next

Giants visit Indianapolis Sunday, Nov. 29 at 1 p.m. ET

The Colts host New York on Fox after a ten-day break, with time to get tackles Raimann and Travis and corners Gardner and Ward healthy. Watch whether the run defense tightens up against a Giants offense that leans on Cam Skattebo.
