Biggest Story

Colts dump two receivers at the deadline, keep Taylor

Indianapolis sent Josh Downs to Kansas City and Keenan Allen to San Francisco before Tuesday's deadline. The Colts also turned down offers for running back Jonathan Taylor, reportedly including a second-round pick, and held onto quarterback Daniel Jones.

Source: WISH-TV 21Alive ↗

2
Injury Report

No timetable for Pierce's return from heel injury

Receiver Alec Pierce re-injured the same left heel that had surgery in the offseason, went on injured reserve after Week 2, and coach Shane Steichen would not set a return date. With Downs and Allen gone, Pierce is the receiver the Colts are waiting on.

Source: Yardbarker ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Taylor carries the Colts' ground game almost alone

Jonathan Taylor has 656 of the team's 1,037 rushing yards and 8 of its 11 rushing touchdowns. Against Dallas he managed 13 carries for 46 yards in the 27-23 loss.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

500: yards the Colts defense allowed Dallas, again

Indianapolis gave up more than 500 yards to the Cowboys, the third time this season it has done that. The Colts have allowed more than six yards per play in five games.

Source: Football Nation ↗

What's Next

Vs. Dolphins, Sunday 1 p.m. ET on CBS

The Colts, losers of five straight, host a Miami team that is 1-7 at Lucas Oil Stadium. Jones is listed as the starter. Watch whether the defense stops giving up big plays.
