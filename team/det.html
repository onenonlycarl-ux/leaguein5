Detroit Lions

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Lions win 19-9 in Miami without a big offensive day

Detroit beat the Dolphins 19-9 and never trailed, its fourth straight win. The league's top-ranked offense, which came in averaging 395.4 yards, finished with 271. Jake Bates kicked four field goals and the defense held Miami without a touchdown. Dan Campbell picked up his 54th win as Lions coach.

Source: AP via Chicago Sun-Times ↗

2
Injury Report

Jack Campbell on injured reserve with neck injury

The Lions placed All-Pro linebacker Jack Campbell on injured reserve on Saturday, less than a week after he was hospitalized with a neck injury against Minnesota. Dan Campbell said Friday that Jack continues to improve. Linebacker Jimmy Rolder is out for the season and needs surgery, per the coach's update last week. Detroit's players wore No. 46 jerseys on Sunday in tribute.

Source: AP via Your Valley ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jahmyr Gibbs scores on fourth down in Miami

Gibbs carried 20 times for 74 yards and ran in an 8-yard touchdown on fourth down, his 11th touchdown from scrimmage this season. Jameson Williams set it up with a 31-yard catch, Detroit's longest play of the day. Amon-Ra St. Brown added 86 receiving yards.

Source: AP via Chicago Sun-Times ↗

4
Stat of the Day

54: Dan Campbell's wins, second most in Lions history

Campbell's 54th regular-season win moved him into sole possession of second place on the franchise list in his sixth season. Only Wayne Fontes is ahead with 66 wins, from 1988 to 1996.

Source: AP via Chicago Sun-Times ↗

What's Next

Lions meet the Patriots in Munich

Detroit faces New England on Sunday, Nov. 15 at Allianz Arena in Munich. Kickoff is 9:30 a.m. ET on FOX. The Patriots arrive off a 45-27 loss to Green Bay, so watch how Detroit's offense bounces back and who steps up at linebacker.
