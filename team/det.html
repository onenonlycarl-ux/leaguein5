Biggest Story

Lions pull away from Buccaneers 31-21

Detroit won its sixth straight to reach 8-2. Jared Goff threw for 279 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions, and the Lions scored 14 points in the fourth quarter to break it open. Jameson Williams caught four passes for 93 yards and a score.

Injury Report

Hutchinson out for the season with right knee injury

Aidan Hutchinson hurt his right knee celebrating a sack of Drake Maye on Nov. 15, and a source told the AP the injury ends his season. He had six sacks in nine games. Cornerbacks D.J. Reed (hip) and Ennis Rakestraw (shoulder) were inactive Sunday, and Khalil Dorsey returned after a first-half wrist injury.

Source: Michigan Public ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jahmyr Gibbs runs for 98 yards against the league's top run defense

Gibbs scored on a 1-yard run and a 4-yard run in the fourth quarter. Tampa Bay entered allowing a league-low 79 rushing yards per game. His touchdowns turned a close game into a 10-point win.

Source: CBS Detroit ↗

Stat of the Day

7 of 11: Lions third-down conversions against Tampa Bay

That is a 63.6 percent rate, far above Detroit's season mark of 39.4 percent. The Lions also went 3 for 3 in the red zone. Goff added a season-high 10-yard scramble to convert a third down.

What's Next

Lions host Bears on Thanksgiving

Detroit hosts Chicago on Thursday, Nov. 26, at 1 p.m. ET on CBS at Ford Field. Both teams are 8-2. The Lions play their first Thanksgiving game without Hutchinson, so the pass rush is the thing to watch.
