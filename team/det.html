NFC North · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

Detroit Lions

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Lions grind out a 19-9 win in Miami to reach 6-2

Detroit won its fourth straight on Sunday behind Jahmyr Gibbs' 8-yard touchdown and four field goals from Jake Bates. The offense was quiet, but the defense held Miami to nine points. The Lions now travel to Munich for a Week 10 game with New England.

Source: CBS12 (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Mays, Reed and Joseph out for the Germany game

Center Cade Mays (ankle), cornerback D.J. Reed (hip) and safety Kerby Joseph (knee) are ruled out against New England. Quarterback Jared Goff (chest) and edge DJ Wonnum (concussion) were full participants, and receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown (rib) was limited. Running back Sione Vaki (ankle) did not practice but is traveling with the team.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jake Bates goes 4 for 4 as the offense stalls

Bates hit field goals from 28, 33, 40 and 43 yards and supplied most of Detroit's points in Miami. Gibbs added 20 carries for 74 yards and his 11th touchdown from scrimmage this season. Goff finished 17 of 25 for 170 yards with three sacks and a tipped interception.

Source: CBS12 (AP) ↗

4
Stat of the Day

30th: Where Detroit's defense ranks in opponent EPA per play

Despite the win in Miami, the Lions rank 30th in opponent EPA per play and 21st in opponent yards per play at 5.4. Detroit also lost All-Pro linebacker Jack Campbell to injured reserve with a neck injury suffered against Minnesota on Nov. 1. Drake Maye, who ranks fifth in adjusted EPA per play, is next.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

5
What's Next

Patriots in Munich, Sunday at 9:30 a.m. ET on Fox

Detroit (6-2) plays New England (5-4) at the home of FC Bayern Munich in the NFL's Germany game. The Patriots lost cornerback Carlton Davis III and guard Ben Brown to ruled-out designations, so watch how the Lions' short-handed secondary handles Maye.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗
