AFC North · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Baltimore Ravens

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Jaguars rout Ravens 33-10 for Baltimore's fourth straight loss

Baltimore fell to 4-5 on Thursday night, the first time in team history it has lost three straight games by 20 or more points. Jesse Minter pointed to an explosive play before halftime that broke the game open and called the pre-snap penalties unacceptable. The Ravens had four more false starts and now lead the league with 24.

Source: Baltimore Ravens ↗

2
Injury Report

Lamar Jackson on IR as Hamilton's hip check awaits

Lamar Jackson is on injured reserve with a right thumb injury, and Minter will not say whether he needs surgery. Safety Kyle Hamilton left Thursday's game with a hip injury, and Minter said things look fairly good, with more clarity coming once players return Tuesday. Safety Jaylinn Hawkins (knee) has missed three straight games, and cornerback Nate Wiggins (illness) sat out against Jacksonville.

Source: Baltimore Ravens ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Derrick Henry moves up to seventh on the career rushing list

Henry carried 17 times for 57 yards against Jacksonville, enough to pass another back and take seventh place on the all-time rushing list. The Ravens still ran the Wildcat 10 times and attempted just one pass of 10 or more yards downfield, so the ground game carried a stalled offense.

Source: Baltimore Ravens ↗

4
Stat of the Day

24: false starts for Baltimore, the most in the league

Four more flags before the snap against Jacksonville pushed the Ravens to a league-high 24 false starts this season. Rookie Joe Fagnano, making his first career start on a heavily wrapped ankle, went 10 for 18 for 60 yards with two interceptions, and Baltimore's longest completion was 14 yards.

Source: Baltimore Ravens ↗

5
What's Next

Ravens host Chargers on Monday night with Brissett at QB

Baltimore plays the Chargers at home on Monday, Nov. 16, at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. Jacoby Brissett, acquired in a trade with Arizona, is expected to start, and Minter said he will get the ball out on time. Watch whether the offense stretches the field after the Jaguars game and whether Hamilton is back.
