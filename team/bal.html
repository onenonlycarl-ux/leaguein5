Baltimore Ravens

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Ravens turn to Jacoby Brissett as fourth starting QB in four weeks

Baltimore acquired Brissett from Arizona on Nov. 6, and he first practiced with the team Wednesday. He will start Monday night against the winless Chargers. The Ravens have lost four straight and need a spark to keep their season alive.

Source: National Football Post ↗

2
Injury Report

Jackson and Huntley stay out as Bateman, Hawkins, Ioane work limited

Lamar Jackson hurt his right thumb on the first offensive play against the Bills on Nov. 1 and is out long term. Tyler Huntley is also out after toe surgery. Rashod Bateman (calf), Jaylinn Hawkins (knee) and Olaivavega Ioane (knee/shoulder) were limited Thursday.

Source: National Football Post ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jacoby Brissett arrives in Baltimore with a 3-5 mark this season

The 33-year-old has 73 career starts over 11 seasons. He is 3-5 as a starter this season, and Zay Flowers praised his communication with receivers. Flowers has just three catches for 6 yards over the past two games, so Brissett has work to do.

Source: The Baltimore Banner ↗

4
Stat of the Day

117-43: the margin the Ravens have been outscored during a four-game skid

Baltimore lost 36-10 to the Bills and 33-10 to the Jaguars in the last two games of the slide. The Ravens sit at 4-5. Their offense has to find points with a new quarterback.

Source: National Football Post ↗

5
What's Next

Ravens host winless Chargers on Monday Night Football

Baltimore hosts Los Angeles (0-8) Monday, Nov. 16 at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. First-year coach Jesse Minter faces his former boss, Jim Harbaugh, for the first time. Watch whether Brissett and Flowers connect early.
