AFC North · Monday, November 16 · Week 10

Baltimore Ravens

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Ravens turn to Brissett with Jackson out 6 to 10 weeks

Lamar Jackson fractured and dislocated his right thumb on his first snap back from a high ankle sprain, then had surgery. Coach Jesse Minter says that kind of recovery usually takes six to ten weeks. Baltimore has lost four straight and sits one game out of the last AFC playoff spot, so veteran Jacoby Brissett starts tonight.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Hawkins out, Randall questionable for Monday night

Safety Jaylinn Hawkins is out with a knee injury and will miss his fourth straight game. Rookie running back Adam Randall (neck and hamstring) is questionable, and Minter said there is potential he gets activated for his first regular-season game. Receiver Rashod Bateman (calf) and guard Vega Ioane (knee and shoulder) were limited Thursday.

Source: Baltimore Ravens ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Brissett takes a crash course in the Ravens offense

Jacoby Brissett landed in Baltimore after a trade, a week after Arizona made him its No. 3 quarterback. He threw for 215 yards a game in eight games with the Cardinals. He has stayed after practice to throw with receivers and work with coordinator Declan Doyle. Calais Campbell, his old Arizona teammate, says Brissett is ripping the ball.

Source: Baltimore Ravens ↗

4
Stat of the Day

13: most points Baltimore has scored in its four straight losses

The Ravens have not topped 13 points during the skid. The passing game managed 68 yards in Buffalo and 55 against Jacksonville with undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano under center. Brissett has to lift that floor right away.

Source: Baltimore Ravens ↗

5
What's Next

Monday night at home against the winless Chargers

Baltimore hosts Los Angeles tonight on Monday Night Football (8:15 p.m. ET, ESPN per the league schedule). The Chargers are 0-8 and led by a coaching staff that faces its former defensive coordinator, Jesse Minter. The teams carry a combined 15 straight losses, a Monday night record per Sportradar. The Ravens visit Carolina in Week 11.
