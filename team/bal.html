Baltimore Ravens

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Ravens trade for Jacoby Brissett after Lamar Jackson goes down

Baltimore finished a deal with Arizona on Friday for veteran quarterback Jacoby Brissett, sending a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 seventh-round pick and getting a 2027 fifth back. The move follows a four-game losing streak and the loss of both Jackson and Tyler Huntley. The team has not said whether Brissett will start.

Source: The Sports Encounter ↗

2
Injury Report

Jackson on IR after thumb surgery, Huntley done for the year

Lamar Jackson hurt his right thumb after one offensive play in his return against Buffalo on Nov. 1 and had surgery. He is on injured reserve with no return date. Backup Tyler Huntley has a toe injury that is reported to end his season.

Source: The Sports Encounter ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Justice Hill supplies the lone bright spot with a 65-yard score

Hill broke a 65-yard rushing touchdown in the fourth quarter of Thursday's 33-10 loss to Jacksonville. It was Baltimore's only trip to the end zone in a game where undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano made his first NFL start and threw two interceptions. Hill gave the offense its only real chunk play.

Source: The Sports Encounter ↗

4
Stat of the Day

4: straight losses for a Ravens team now 4-5

Baltimore has dropped four in a row, the latest a 33-10 home loss to the Jaguars in the rain. The Ravens managed just over 200 net yards and Jacksonville forced three turnovers. The previous week, a 36-10 loss to Buffalo, produced fewer than 100 combined passing yards.

Source: Yahoo Sports (SB Nation) ↗

5
What's Next

Chargers visit Baltimore on Nov. 16

The Ravens host the Los Angeles Chargers on Nov. 16, the first game after the Brissett trade. Watch who takes the snaps and whether the offense can move the ball with a new quarterback learning the system on a short week. The source did not list kickoff time or network.
