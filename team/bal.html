Baltimore Ravens

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Jaguars run over the Ravens 33-10 on Thursday night

Jacksonville beat a depleted Baltimore team 33-10 in Week 9 and pushed the Ravens to 4-5. It was their fourth straight loss, and it left them last in the AFC North. Coach Jesse Minter declined to discuss Lamar Jackson's status after the game.

Source: ClutchPoints ↗

2
Injury Report

Jackson's thumb injury leaves the Ravens thin at quarterback

Lamar Jackson lasted one play against Buffalo in Week 8 before hurting his right thumb, one game after he came back from a high ankle sprain. Minter said Jackson would have surgery and go on injured reserve, which means at least four missed games. Backup Tyler Huntley also got hurt in that game, and SI reported a turf toe injury that ends his season.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Ryan Neuzil arrives to steady the middle of the line

Baltimore got the center from Atlanta at the Tuesday deadline for guard Andrew Vorhees and a reported conditional 2028 sixth-round pick. Neuzil started all 17 games for the Falcons last year, and his PFF grade ranks 15th among centers with at least half their team's snaps. He played for Dwayne Ledford in Atlanta, and Ledford now coaches the Ravens' line.

Source: Ravens.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

4: straight losses since a 4-1 start

The Ravens opened 4-1 and have lost four in a row since, falling to 4-5. The pass defense has finished above minus 1 percent DVOA only once this season, in Week 1, according to the BetMGM preview.

Source: BetMGM ↗

5
What's Next

Ravens host the Chargers on Monday night

Baltimore plays Los Angeles at M&T Bank Stadium on Monday, Nov. 16 at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. The Chargers were 0-8 entering the week, and this is a chance to stop the slide. Watch the quarterback decision and whether safety Kyle Hamilton and cornerback Nate Wiggins can play.
