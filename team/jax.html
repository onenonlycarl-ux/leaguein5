Jacksonville Jaguars

7-3Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Giants beat Jaguars 23-18, snapping a three-game streak

Jacksonville (7-3) kept the AFC South lead but scored one touchdown on six red zone trips. Coach Liam Coen said his team was outplayed and outcoached in all three phases and held an accountability meeting on Monday.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Tuten limited after ankle gets stepped on twice

Running back Bhayshul Tuten aggravated his ankle against the Giants. Coen said Monday's recheck was not too bad and expects Tuten to be limited again. The team will watch him Wednesday and Thursday. Tackle Walker Little also left in the fourth quarter.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Josh Hines-Allen sacks and strips in a loss

Hines-Allen finished with five tackles, a sack, a tackle for loss and a quarterback hit. He also forced the Cam Skattebo fumble that gave Jacksonville its best scoring chance of the third quarter.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

13: Penalties the Jaguars took for 107 yards

The Giants were flagged six times for 46 yards. Jacksonville also scored no rushing touchdown for the first time since its loss to Houston in Week 6, and Trevor Lawrence threw two interceptions.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

What's Next

Titans visit EverBank Stadium on Sunday

Jacksonville hosts Tennessee (1-9) on Sunday, Nov. 29 at 1 p.m. ET on FOX in the Salute to Service game. The Jaguars beat the Titans 40-23 on Nov. 15, and the Tennessee defense has allowed 40 or more points in three straight games.
