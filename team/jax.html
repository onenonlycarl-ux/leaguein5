Jacksonville Jaguars

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Jaguars rest new defenders Bates and Davis against Titans

Coach Liam Coen said safety Jessie Bates and tackle Jordan Davis, both deadline acquisitions, will not play at Tennessee. Neither has had a bye yet this season. Jacksonville is a double-digit favorite and leads the AFC South by three games.

Source: A to Z Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Tuten sat Thursday with ankle and foot issue; Green nears return

Running back Bhayshul Tuten did not practice Thursday with an ankle and foot injury. Guard Ezra Cleveland, safety Antonio Johnson, running back Chris Rodriguez Jr., center Robert Hainsey and linebacker Branson Combs were limited. Defensive end BJ Green practiced fully again and is set to return from injured reserve.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Trevor Lawrence owns the NFL's second-best passer rating

Lawrence has 18 touchdown passes, four interceptions and a 111.7 passer rating through eight games. That rating ranks second in the league. He threw no interceptions in the last two games.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

+7: Jaguars lead the NFL in turnover differential

Jacksonville has 15 takeaways this season after three against Baltimore in the 33-10 win. Under defensive coordinator Anthony Campanile, the Jaguars have 46 takeaways in 25 games, up from nine in all of 2024. They did it without nose tackle DaVon Hamilton and safety Eric Murray.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

5
What's Next

Jaguars visit Titans Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on FOX

Jacksonville (6-2) plays at Tennessee (1-7) at Nissan Stadium on Nov. 15. The teams meet again Nov. 29 in Jacksonville. Lawrence threw for 484 yards and five touchdowns in two games against the Titans last season.
