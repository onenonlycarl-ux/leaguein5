Biggest Story

Jaguars beat Ravens 33-10, lead South by three games

Jacksonville moved to 6-2 with a Thursday night win in Baltimore. The Jaguars outgained the Ravens 319 to 227 and forced three turnovers. They have scored 31 or more in each of their last three games.

Injury Report

Bates and Davis rest, Green out, Hainsey questionable

Safety Jessie Bates III and tackle Jordan Davis are out for rest, not injury. Edge BJ Green II (forearm) is out. Lineman Robert Hainsey (concussion) is questionable.

Player Spotlight

Lawrence posts the NFL's second best passer rating

Trevor Lawrence has 18 touchdown passes and four interceptions, with a 111.7 passer rating that ranks second in the league. Last week he went 16 of 26 for 242 yards and a 50-yard touchdown to Parker Washington.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

+108: Jaguars scoring margin leads the NFL

Jacksonville is the only team with a top-five scoring offense and a top-five scoring defense. The Jaguars score 28.6 points a game and allow 15.1. They are only the fourth team since 2000 to win four of their first eight games by 23 or more points.
