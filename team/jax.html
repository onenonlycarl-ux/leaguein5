Biggest Story

Lawrence lifts Jaguars to 40-23 win in Nashville

Trevor Lawrence threw for 352 yards and three touchdowns as Jacksonville moved to 7-2. The Jaguars have scored 30 or more in four straight games and lead the AFC South by four games. They also set a franchise record with a fifth straight win against division opponents.

Danny Striggow was evaluated for a concussion and did not return. Robert Hainsey hurt his left knee and also left the game. Neither has a Week 11 status yet.
