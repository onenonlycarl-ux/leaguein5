AFC South · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Jacksonville Jaguars

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Jaguars add Jordan Davis, Jessie Bates and Davon Godchaux in three trades

Jacksonville acquired defensive tackle Jordan Davis from Philadelphia for a 2027 first and 2029 second. Safety Jessie Bates III cost a 2027 second and 2028 fifth from Atlanta, and Godchaux came from New Orleans for a conditional sixth. General manager James Gladstone rejected the all-in label and called the deals team building.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Tuten, Meyers and Walker miss Wednesday practice

Running back Bhayshul Tuten (ankle and foot), receiver Jakobi Meyers (ankle) and end Travon Walker (rib) did not practice. Davis and Bates sat out as veteran rest days. Center Robert Hainsey (concussion), guard Ezra Cleveland and safety Antonio Johnson were limited. Liam Coen has not said whether the new defenders play Sunday.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Trevor Lawrence stays sharp in a 33-10 win at Baltimore

Lawrence completed 16 of 26 passes for 242 yards and a touchdown, a 50-yard strike to Parker Washington before halftime. He has 18 touchdown passes against four interceptions this season. Coen called his performance extremely sharp and accurate.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

17: points the Jaguars defense has allowed in two games since the bye

Jacksonville held Baltimore to 10 points on Thursday night and forced three turnovers. Coen says his team leads the NFL in run defense, and the new tackles should help. The Jaguars lead the AFC South by 3.5 games.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

5
What's Next

Jaguars visit the Titans Sunday in Nashville

Jacksonville plays at Tennessee on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 1 p.m. ET on Fox at Nissan Stadium. Watch how quickly Bates and the new tackles work into the defense. The Jaguars play at the Giants the following Sunday.
