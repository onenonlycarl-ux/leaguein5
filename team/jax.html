AFC South · Saturday, November 7 · Week 9

Jacksonville Jaguars

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Jaguars beat Ravens 33-10 to build a 3.5-game division lead

Jacksonville never trailed in Baltimore on Thursday night and improved to 6-2, its fourth win in five games. The Jaguars outgained the Ravens 319 to 227 and forced three turnovers against a Baltimore team starting rookie Joe Fagnano at quarterback. The Texans, at 2-5, trail by 3.5 games.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Murray and Hamilton out for the season, line help sought

Coach Liam Coen confirmed that safety Eric Murray and defensive tackle DaVon Hamilton are finished for 2026, and both went on injured reserve. Hamilton tore his quad in Week 8. Edge Travon Walker (rib) and running back Bhayshul Tuten (foot and ankle) were questionable Thursday and played through it.

Source: Pro Football Rumors ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Trevor Lawrence is sharp, Parker Washington hits a 50-yard TD

Lawrence went 16 of 26 for 242 yards and a touchdown with no interceptions, lifting his season line to 18 touchdown passes and four picks. Washington caught a 50-yard score that made it 17-3 at halftime. Coen called Lawrence extremely sharp and accurate when the game mattered.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

31: Points or more in each of the Jaguars' last three games

Jacksonville has scored at least 31 points in three straight games, and all three were played in wet weather. Coen said the rain tests a team's physical and mental toughness. The Jaguars also held Derrick Henry to 57 yards on 17 carries, with 51 of those yards coming in the first half.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

5
What's Next

At Titans, Sunday Nov. 15, 1 p.m. ET on FOX

Jacksonville travels to Nissan Stadium to face a Tennessee team that is 1-7 and coming off its bye. The Jaguars have no game this weekend and use the extra days to heal. Watch the defensive line, which lost Hamilton, and whether the team adds help before the Nov. 10 trade deadline.
