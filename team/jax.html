AFC South · Monday, November 23 · Week 11

Jacksonville Jaguars

7-3Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Giants beat Jaguars 23-18 after a 20-6 halftime lead

Jacksonville lost for the first time since Week 6 and fell to 7-3, though it still leads the AFC South by three games. Trevor Lawrence threw two interceptions and the Jaguars turned a replay ruling on a Parker Washington catch into a field goal instead of a touchdown. Coach Liam Coen said his team was outplayed.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Left tackle Walker Little leaves with a knee injury

Little, the starting left tackle, left the loss to New York with a knee injury and Wyatt Milum replaced him. Cornerback Jarrian Jones also left early with a chest injury, according to the team's game report. Running back Bhayshul Tuten went into the game hurt and was injured again late. Center Robert Hainsey was out.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Parker Washington posts 8 catches, 103 yards in the loss

Washington led Jacksonville's receivers with eight catches for 103 yards. Officials also ruled a fourth-quarter third-and-9 grab at the Giants' 12 incomplete, the replay held, and Jacksonville settled for a field goal. Brian Thomas Jr. later caught an 18-yard score.

Source: Jaguars.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

1 of 6: Jaguars' touchdown rate in the red zone

Jacksonville threw 12 passes inside the 20 and ran twice for one yard. Lawrence completed four of those 12. The team also took 13 penalties for 107 yards, eight of them before the snap.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

What's Next

Jaguars host the Titans on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on Fox

Jacksonville faces 1-9 Tennessee at EverBank Stadium on Nov. 29, a team it beat 40-23 in Nashville a week before the Giants game. Watch the red zone play calling and the status of the left tackle spot.
