Arizona Cardinals

3-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Minshew likely out for the year, Beck next in line

Gardner Minshew may have torn his right UCL in the 17-3 loss at Seattle and is seeking more opinions before surgery. The Cardinals drafted Carson Beck in the third round this year, and he is expected to make his first career start against the Rams. Arizona traded Jacoby Brissett to Baltimore after benching him for Minshew in Week 8.

Source: Bleacher Report ↗

2
Injury Report

Minshew on IR, Wilson out with neck fracture

The Cardinals placed Minshew on injured reserve Tuesday. Coach Mike LaFleur said receiver Michael Wilson will miss the rest of the season with a neck fracture and also went on IR. Arizona signed Josh Johnson and Joey Aguilar from practice squads to restock the quarterback room.

Source: The Fantasy Footballers ↗

3
Player Spotlight

McBride caught 7 of 8 targets in a rough day

Tight end Trey McBride was the steadiest piece of an offense that managed only a field goal. He caught seven of eight targets for 54 yards, including a fourth-down grab late in the fourth quarter.

Source: Yardbarker ↗

4
Stat of the Day

10: points in two games against Seattle this year

Arizona has scored a combined 10 points in its two meetings with Seattle, tying for the fewest by any team in a division season series in the last five seasons. The Seahawks have now beaten the Cardinals 11 straight times, the longest streak against one opponent in franchise history.

Source: Seahawks.com ↗

What's Next

Rams visit State Farm Stadium Sunday

Arizona hosts the Los Angeles Rams on Sunday, Nov. 15, at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS. Watch the quarterback spot, where Beck is expected to take over, and whether the Cardinals can protect him after allowing three sacks to Seattle.
