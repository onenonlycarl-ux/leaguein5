1
Biggest Story

Minshew replaces Brissett as Arizona's starting quarterback

Coach Mike LaFleur named Gardner Minshew the starter for Sunday at Seattle after he rallied the Cardinals from a 17-3 deficit in Dallas. Minshew went 17 of 23 for 188 yards and three touchdowns after Jacoby Brissett came out in the second half. Carson Beck moves up to the No. 2 job and Brissett drops to the emergency role.

Injury Report

Three Cardinals ruled out, three more questionable vs. Seattle

Guard Jon Gaines (ankle), running back Bam Knight (concussion) and left tackle Jayden Williams (knee) are out on Friday's report. Guard Isaiah Adams (ankle), cornerback Max Melton (knee) and defensive lineman Walter Nolen (ankle) are questionable. The offensive line takes the biggest hit with two starters sidelined.

Source: Arizona Cardinals ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Allgeier posts a career day when Arizona's backfield runs thin

Tyler Allgeier ran 11 times for 147 yards, a career high, and scored on a 63-yard run, his longest ever. He added five catches for 44 yards and a touchdown. Allgeier was the only healthy back left once Bam Knight went into concussion protocol and Jeremiyah Love left with an ankle injury.

Source: Deseret News ↗

4
Stat of the Day

5-0: Cardinals keep winning at AT&T Stadium

Arizona beat Dallas 33-30 in overtime and is now 5-0 at AT&T Stadium. The Cardinals have won five straight against the Cowboys overall. Chad Ryland kicked the 42-yard winner after he missed an extra point with six seconds left in regulation.

Source: AZFamily ↗

5
What's Next

Cardinals visit Seahawks Sunday at 4:25 PM ET on FOX

Arizona plays at Lumen Field on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 4:25 PM ET on FOX. Seattle will start Drew Lock because Sam Darnold is out. Lock threw for 235 yards and three touchdowns when the Seahawks beat Arizona 31-7 in Week 2.
