Arizona Cardinals

1
Biggest Story

Cardinals stick with rookie Carson Beck for a trip to Arrowhead

Carson Beck made his first NFL start in the 34-6 loss to the Rams. He went 11 of 18 for 108 yards with two interceptions and took five sacks. Coach Mike LaFleur still came away encouraged by his command of the huddle and cadence. Beck now gets a second start against the 7-2 Chiefs.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Five Cardinals sit out Wednesday, led by Baker and Sweat

Safety Budda Baker, guard Isaac Seumalo, linebacker Mack Wilson and edge rusher Josh Sweat did not practice Wednesday, mostly for rest. Defensive lineman Walter Nolen missed with an illness. Center Hjalte Froholdt (concussion), guard Jon Gaines (ankle) and left tackle Jayden Williams (knee) were limited.

Source: Arizona Cardinals ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Trey McBride keeps the Cardinals offense moving

McBride has 78 catches for 635 yards and five touchdowns on 99 targets. That is about 27.5 percent of Arizona's 360 targets. He caught all five passes thrown his way against the Rams and ranks second among NFL tight ends in receiving yards, behind Brock Bowers.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

649: Penalty yards Arizona has given away in 10 games

The Cardinals have been flagged 72 times for 649 yards, more than 64 yards a game. That total is about two-thirds of their 95.8 rushing yards per game. They have also allowed 28 sacks and score touchdowns on 42.9 percent of red-zone trips.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Cardinals visit the Chiefs Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on CBS

Arizona (3-7) plays at Arrowhead Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 22, with CBS carrying the game. The Cardinals have not scored a touchdown since their 33-30 win in Dallas to open the month. Watch whether Beck can keep Patrick Mahomes and the Chiefs' offense off the field.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗
