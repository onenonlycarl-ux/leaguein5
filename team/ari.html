Arizona Cardinals

3-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Rookie Carson Beck will make his first NFL start against the Rams

Coach Mike LaFleur confirmed the third-round pick takes over after Gardner Minshew went on injured reserve with a season-ending elbow injury. Beck threw 15 for 19 in the Hall of Fame Game before a rib injury ended his preseason. Arizona already traded Jacoby Brissett to Baltimore.

Source: Arizona Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Cardinals list center Hjalte Froholdt out with a concussion

Froholdt, who had not missed a game since 2023, is out Sunday along with guard Jon Gaines II and lineman Zach Carter. Cornerback Max Melton and safety Andrew Wingard are questionable. Receivers Michael Wilson, Kendrick Bourne and Jalen Brooks are already done for the season.

Source: NFL.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Trey McBride gives Beck a safe target

McBride has 73 catches for 598 yards and five touchdowns this season. He caught seven passes for 54 yards in the loss at Seattle. With the receiver room gutted, he will be the first option for a rookie quarterback.

Source: TheRams.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

28.3: Points Arizona allows per game, 31st in the NFL

The Cardinals own the league's second-worst scoring defense at 28.3 points per game. Their offense scores 19.8, which ranks 26th. The Rams enter as 9.5-point favorites.

Source: Sharp Football Analysis ↗

5
What's Next

Cardinals host the Rams Sunday at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS

The game is at State Farm Stadium. Watch how Beck handles the Rams' pass defense, which ranks fifth in completion rate allowed. Arizona has lost 11 straight to Seattle and needs a division win.
