NFC West · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

Arizona Cardinals

3-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Minshew lands on IR, and Arizona rebuilds its quarterback room

The Cardinals put Gardner Minshew on injured reserve Tuesday after Dante Fowler Jr. hit his throwing arm on the last play of the first half in Seattle. Minshew finished that game anyway, but he is not expected back this season. Arizona signed 40-year-old Josh Johnson off the Bengals practice squad, his 15th NFL team, a week after shipping Jacoby Brissett to Baltimore.

Source: FOX Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Froholdt in concussion protocol as five starters sit Wednesday

Lineman Hjalte Froholdt (concussion), lineman Jon Gaines II (ankle) and defensive lineman Roy Lopez (toe, ankle) missed Wednesday's practice. Budda Baker, Isaac Seumalo, Josh Sweat and Mack Wilson Sr. also sat, mostly for rest. Running back Bam Knight (concussion) and cornerback Max Melton (knee, hand) were limited, and the team has Zach Carter and Hunter Long practicing in their IR return windows.

Source: Arizona Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Carson Beck goes from emergency QB to Sunday starter

Beck, a third-round rookie, spent the first eight weeks as the emergency third quarterback and sat inactive on game days. He played 55 college games at Georgia and Miami, and his last snap came in the national title game loss to Indiana in January. Coach Mike LaFleur says Beck will not shy away from throwing deep, though Arizona's active receivers have just eight catches combined this season.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

11: straight losses to Seattle after Sunday's 17-3 defeat

Arizona has dropped 11 in a row to the Seahawks after managing just 198 total yards at Lumen Field. The offense went 4 of 13 on third down and put together eight straight scoreless drives after a first-quarter Chad Ryland field goal. The loss dropped the Cardinals to 3-6.

Source: ClutchPoints ↗

5
What's Next

Rams visit State Farm Stadium Sunday at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS

Beck makes his first career start against a Rams team that has won three straight. Watch how he handles a pass rush led by Myles Garrett, who had three sacks in his return last week. Trey McBride led Arizona with seven catches in Seattle and should see plenty of targets again.

Source: Rams.com ↗
