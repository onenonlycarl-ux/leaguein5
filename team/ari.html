Arizona Cardinals

3-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Cardinals draw a Mahomes who has not thrown an interception in three games

Patrick Mahomes threw for 348 yards and five touchdowns in Kansas City's 35-13 win at Atlanta. He has 10 touchdown passes and no interceptions over his last three games. Arizona (3-7) has not scored a touchdown since its 33-30 win over Dallas and now faces that passing game on the road.

Source: SI ↗

2
Injury Report

Cardinals line goes limited as Baker and Sweat rest

Safety Budda Baker, guard Isaac Seumalo, edge rusher Josh Sweat and linebacker Mack Wilson sat out Wednesday for rest, and lineman Walter Nolen missed with an illness. The bigger concern is up front. Center Hjalte Froholdt (concussion), guard Jon Gaines (ankle) and tackle Jayden Williams (knee) all practiced in a limited role. Defensive lineman Jordan Burch hurt his knee against the Rams and did not return.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Carson Beck inherits the job after two quarterback moves

Beck, a third-round pick out of Miami, started against the Rams after Arizona traded Jacoby Brissett to Baltimore on Nov. 6 and Gardner Minshew went down with a season-ending elbow injury. The rookie finished 11 of 18 for 108 yards with two interceptions. Kansas City is his second NFL start.

Source: KOLD ↗

4
Stat of the Day

123: Total yards the Cardinals gathered against the Rams

Arizona held a 6-3 lead late in the second quarter, then gained only 123 yards in the 34-6 loss, most of them after the game was decided. The Cardinals have also lost 11 straight home games, a streak of 427 days. The league record is 14.

Source: KOLD ↗

5
What's Next

Cardinals at Chiefs Sunday, 1 p.m. ET on CBS

Arizona (3-7) travels to Arrowhead Stadium to face Kansas City (7-2) on Sunday, Nov. 22. CBS has the game and Paramount+ streams it. Watch whether the Cardinals protect Beck with a patchwork line and finally find the end zone.
