Arizona Cardinals

3-8Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Josh Johnson set for another start as Carson Beck's back flares

Coach Mike LaFleur said Wednesday that Josh Johnson is preparing to start Sunday against Washington. Rookie Carson Beck missed the loss in Kansas City and sat out practice again with back spasms. Friday's injury report will show whether Beck has a chance to return.

Source: CBS Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Gaines setback, Beck out of practice, rest days for veterans

Guard Jon Gaines II suffered an ankle setback and will likely miss his sixth straight game. Beck did not practice Wednesday with his back issue. Budda Baker, Isaac Seumalo, Hjalte Froholdt and Josh Sweat sat out as rest or injury management, and linebacker Cody Simon (calf) was limited.

Source: AZCardinals.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Johnson, 40, throws for 155 yards in Kansas City

Josh Johnson went 18 of 31 for 155 yards and one touchdown in the 34-7 loss to the Chiefs. Kansas City sacked him three times, and he finished with an 82.1 passer rating. He had joined the roster less than two weeks earlier.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

4: starting quarterbacks the Cardinals used in four weeks

Johnson became the fourth different starter in four weeks, after Jacoby Brissett, Gardner Minshew and Beck. Minshew is lost for the season with a right UCL injury. Arizona now sits at 3-8.

Source: AZCardinals.com ↗

5
What's Next

Commanders visit Sunday, 4:25 PM ET on Fox

Arizona hosts Washington (4-5-1) at State Farm Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 29, with kickoff at 4:25 PM ET on Fox. Watch the quarterback situation first: Johnson is preparing to start unless Beck clears his back issue. Gaines and the offensive line depth also bear watching.

Source: FOX Sports ↗
