NFC South · Wednesday, November 25 · Week 12

Atlanta Falcons

3-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Falcons come off the bye at 3-7 with a defense to rebuild

Atlanta has lost three straight and five of its last six, and it traded safety Jessie Bates III, its best defender, before the bye. Clutch Points argues the defense has to improve by a few percent per player to cover that loss. Linebacker Kendal Daniels leads the team with 69 tackles.

Source: Clutch Points ↗

2
Injury Report

Branch and Deablo still unsettled before Minnesota

Wide receiver Zachariah Branch (hamstring) missed Weeks 9 and 10, and his coach has said he could miss more games. Linebacker Divine Deablo (forearm) sat out Week 10. Neither had an official designation as of Tuesday. Running back Brian Robinson Jr. is on injured reserve and cannot return before Dec. 13.

Source: DraftKings Network ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bijan Robinson leads the league in touches since 2023

Robinson has 6,855 yards from scrimmage and 1,223 offensive touches over the last four years, the most in the NFL in both categories, according to The Athletic's note in the Falcons' power rankings roundup. He was the eighth pick in 2023. He remains the steadiest piece of an offense that has struggled with turnovers.

Source: AtlantaFalcons.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

19: Falcons giveaways, tied for second most in the NFL

Atlanta also ranks 28th in third-down conversion rate at 34.4 percent and is tied for 30th on fourth down at 28.6 percent, per FOX Sports. Those numbers explain why an offense with strong runners keeps stalling.

Source: AtlantaFalcons.com ↗

5
What's Next

At Minnesota on Sunday, 1 p.m. ET on Fox

The Falcons visit the Vikings on Nov. 29 at U.S. Bank Stadium, with Atlanta after the bye and Minnesota coming off a 35-10 loss to San Francisco. Watch how Atlanta protects Michael Penix Jr. under pressure, an area SI flagged as a weakness, and how the run game fares against Brian Flores' defense.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗
