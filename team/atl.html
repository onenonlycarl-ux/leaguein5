NFC South · Saturday, November 7 · Week 9

Atlanta Falcons

3-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Falcons try to rebound in Madrid after a 16-0 loss

Atlanta landed in Spain on Wednesday and practiced at Real Madrid's training complex before Sunday's game against Cincinnati. The Falcons are coming off a shutout loss at Tampa Bay in heavy rain. Coach Kevin Stefanski says the trip can build team bonding, and receiver Jahan Dotson sees a chance to reset a season that has slipped to 3-5.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

2
Injury Report

Branch and Ebukam hurt, Pearce Jr. returns

Receiver Zachariah Branch and defensive end Samson Ebukam both injured hamstrings against Tampa Bay, and the AJC reports both are out for Madrid. Stefanski would not rule out either missing the Chiefs game after that. Edge rusher James Pearce Jr. returns from an eight-game suspension, and his role may grow with Ebukam sidelined. Divine Deablo (forearm), Brian Robinson Jr. and Billy Bowman Jr. (hamstrings) also missed the Tampa game.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jahan Dotson: 95 yards on two catches in the loss

Dotson caught two passes for 95 yards in Atlanta's 16-0 loss at Tampa Bay, one of the few bright spots on the day. He also takes over punt return duties with Branch hurt. The AJC says Dotson views the Madrid trip as a chance to reset the season.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

32nd: Penix's rank among 35 QBs in completion over expected

Michael Penix Jr. ranks 32nd of 35 starting quarterbacks in completion percentage over expected, according to Sports Illustrated's Week 9 preview. He went 8 of 24 for 125 yards with four interceptions against Tampa Bay. Cincinnati's Joe Burrow leads the league in passing yards per game at 302.1.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

5
What's Next

Falcons vs. Bengals in Madrid, Sunday 9:30 AM ET

Atlanta (3-5) faces Cincinnati (5-2) at Santiago Bernabeu on Sunday at 9:30 AM ET on NFL Network. Watch how the Falcons' secondary handles Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins, who both topped 100 yards in the Bengals' 43-36 overtime win over Tennessee. The Falcons also need their run game and run defense to recover after Tampa Bay ran for 324 yards.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗
