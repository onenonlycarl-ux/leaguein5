Atlanta Falcons

3-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Falcons enter the bye looking for answers on offense

Atlanta lost 35-13 to Kansas City on Nov. 15, its third straight loss and fifth in six games. Michael Penix Jr. has one touchdown and six interceptions in the three November losses, and the offense scored 30 points total in that stretch. Kevin Stefanski called the bye a time for a self scout, and Tommy Rees keeps the play calling.

Source: Atlanta News First ↗

2
Injury Report

Branch and Deablo were the last names on the Falcons list

Receiver Zachariah Branch was ruled out with a hamstring injury for the second straight game before the bye. Linebacker Divine Deablo (forearm) was doubtful, and edge James Pearce Jr. (back) was questionable. Atlanta had not released a post-bye status for any of them as of Tuesday.

Source: AtlantaFalcons.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bijan Robinson says the boos were deserved

Robinson ran 16 times for 71 yards against the Chiefs. He has not reached 20 carries since the Week 7 win over San Francisco, and he missed that mark in all five losses in the last six games. After the game he said the fans were right to boo the team.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

4: straight three-and-outs to open the Chiefs game

Atlanta started the Kansas City game with four consecutive three-and-outs, its first start like that since 2000. The Falcons also went 5 for 16 on third down and 1 for 5 in the red zone. They averaged a league-high 31 points per game from Weeks 3 to 7.

Source: ESPN ↗

5
What's Next

Falcons visit the Vikings after the bye

Atlanta plays at Minnesota on Sunday, Nov. 29 at 1:00 p.m. ET on FOX. Then comes a home game against Detroit on Dec. 6. Stefanski wants the offense to stay on schedule against Brian Flores' defense.
