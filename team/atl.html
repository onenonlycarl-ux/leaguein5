Atlanta Falcons

3-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Burrow carves up Atlanta in Madrid

Joe Burrow went 24-for-29 for 271 yards and three touchdowns as Cincinnati beat the Falcons 34-17 on Sunday. Atlanta gave away ground with 12 penalties for 88 yards and has lost four of its last five. The Falcons sit 3-6, only a half game behind first-place Carolina.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

2
Injury Report

Hamstring trouble hangs over the Falcons

Defensive end Samson Ebukam and receiver Zachariah Branch both sat out the Madrid game with hamstring injuries, and coach Kevin Stefanski did not rule out Week 10 for either. Running back Brian Robinson Jr., linebacker Divine Deablo and defensive back Billy Bowman Jr. were also being monitored as of the Nov. 2 report. Watch this week's practice sheets for updates.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bijan Robinson stays productive in defeat

Bijan Robinson ran 19 times for 100 yards and a touchdown against Cincinnati and added six catches for 47 yards. Quarterback Michael Penix Jr. finished 20-of-31 for 162 yards, one touchdown and one interception, plus 50 rushing yards.

Source: Atlanta News First ↗

4
Stat of the Day

13-for-18: Nick Folk's field goal line

Folk missed a 38-yard field goal in Madrid, his first miss inside 40 yards since Oct. 5, 2017. He has missed at least one kick in four games this season, and Stefanski sidestepped questions about a change.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

5
What's Next

Chiefs visit Atlanta before the bye

Atlanta hosts Kansas City on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS, then has its Week 11 bye. The Falcons lost the last meeting 22-17 in 2024. Watch whether the penalties and the kicking improve with the break coming.
