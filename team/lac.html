Biggest Story

Chargers sink to 0-8 with a 44-22 loss to Houston

The Chargers are the NFL's only winless team, and the Titans and Dolphins sit at 1-7. Houston posted 485 yards and 44 points, both season highs against Los Angeles. The Chargers have not started 0-8 since the 2020 Jets.

2
Injury Report

McConkey and nine others sat out Thursday practice

Receiver Ladd McConkey (back) did not practice after sitting out the Houston game with back spasms. Defensive lineman Justin Eboigbe, receiver Brenen Thompson and safety Kendall Williamson also missed practice. Tight end David Njoku and linebacker Denzel Perryman were limited as they ramp up.

Source: Chargers.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Justin Herbert takes a career-high eight sacks

Herbert went 13 of 23 for 193 yards, one touchdown and one interception behind a line that gave up eight sacks. X-rays on his hand came back negative, and Jim Harbaugh said he is good to go. Herbert has thrown an interception in all eight games.

Source: Chargers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

37: drives in a row without a touchdown ended in Houston

Herbert's 58-yard touchdown pass to Quentin Johnston finally snapped the streak early in the fourth quarter. Los Angeles scored three field goals on its first four drives against the Texans, then went scoreless on its next six.

What's Next

Chargers visit Ravens on Monday night

Los Angeles plays at Baltimore on Monday, Nov. 16 at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN at M&T Bank Stadium. It is the Harbaugh brothers matchup, and the Ravens have won the last three meetings. Watch Herbert's protection and McConkey's back.
