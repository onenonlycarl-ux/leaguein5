Los Angeles Chargers

1-8Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Chargers become the second team to win on MNF after an 0-8 start

Los Angeles beat Baltimore 23-20 on Monday to end an eight-game winless start. ESPN Insights says only the 2013 Buccaneers had won a Monday night game after starting 0-8 or worse. The Chargers led 17-10 at halftime and held on after a late Zay Flowers fumble.

Source: ClutchPoints ↗

2
Injury Report

McConkey limited with a back issue on Thursday

Wide receiver Ladd McConkey was a limited participant Thursday because of a back problem. Jets quarterback Geno Smith (ankle) was limited and wide receiver Omar Cooper Jr. (ankle and hip) did not practice.

Source: FOX Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Hampton posts a season-high 105 yards in the first win

Omarion Hampton ran for a season-high 105 yards and a touchdown against Baltimore and averages 4.8 yards per carry this season. He has four rushing touchdowns and 474 yards through nine games. Jets coach Aaron Glenn said the Chargers are feeling good about themselves.

Source: Jets.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

2: sacks Herbert took, against nearly four per game

Justin Herbert went down only twice against Baltimore, well below his average of nearly four sacks a game over the first eight weeks. The Chargers also outrushed the Ravens 160-16, and Tarheeb Still forced the Flowers fumble that Elijah Molden recovered with 2:03 left.

Source: ESPN ↗

What's Next

Jets visit SoFi Stadium on Sunday at 4:05 PM ET on Fox

Los Angeles (1-8) hosts New York (2-8) and looks for its first back-to-back wins. Watch whether Hampton keeps the run game going and how McConkey's back holds up.
