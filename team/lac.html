Biggest Story

Chargers fall to 0-7 after 24-9 loss to Rams

The Rams scored 21 straight points after a slow start to beat Los Angeles. Justin Herbert threw two interceptions, and the Chargers settled for three field goals on fourth-down attempts. It is their first 0-7 start since 2000.

Source: NFL.com ↗

Injury Report

Slater and James on IR, Alt back at left tackle

Tackle Rashawn Slater and safety Derwin James Jr. are both on injured reserve. Joe Alt returned in Week 8 after missing two games and moved to left tackle, where he allowed no pressures against the Rams.

Source: Chargers.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Joe Alt anchors a patchwork line

Alt has allowed four pressures and no sacks over 169 pass-blocking snaps this season. Jim Harbaugh said Alt did a heck of a job sliding over to left tackle against the Rams.

Source: Chargers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

27: straight Chargers drives without a touchdown

It is the longest such stretch since the 2024 Dolphins. Herbert has thrown 10 interceptions, tied for most in the league, and Los Angeles has just nine touchdowns all season, the fewest in the NFL.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

What's Next

Texans visit SoFi on Sunday

Los Angeles hosts the 2-5 Houston Texans on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS. The Chargers' line must handle Will Anderson Jr., who shares the league lead with 7.5 sacks.
