AFC West · Sunday, November 22 · Week 11

Los Angeles Chargers

1-8Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Chargers finally win, 23-20 over the Ravens

Los Angeles beat Baltimore on Monday night for its first win of the season after eight straight losses. The Chargers scored on their first drive, never trailed and played their first turnover-free game of the year. Jim Harbaugh said he was thrilled and wants to win every week.

Source: ESPN ↗

2
Injury Report

McConkey questionable, Henley out against the Jets

Wide receiver Ladd McConkey (back) is questionable after missing Week 9 and sitting out the Baltimore game. Linebacker Daiyan Henley (knee), cornerback Rodney Shelley (ankle) and safety Kendall Williamson (calf) are out. Receiver Brenen Thompson (hamstring) is doubtful.

Source: Chargers.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Omarion Hampton runs for 105 yards in the win

Hampton carried 22 times for 105 yards and a touchdown against Baltimore, his first 100-yard game of the season. He also scored on a one-yard run. Justin Herbert added a 22-yard touchdown run to open the scoring.

Source: DAZN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

160: Chargers rushing yards, a season high

The Chargers ran for 160 yards against Baltimore behind a line that started three rookies. Herbert threw only 18 passes, a season low. The defense held the Ravens to 16 rushing yards.

Source: ESPN ↗

5
What's Next

Jets at Chargers, today at 4:05 p.m. ET on FOX

Los Angeles hosts New York at SoFi Stadium. Watch McConkey's status and whether the run game keeps working without Henley on defense.
