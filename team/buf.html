Buffalo Bills

1
Biggest Story

Bills reach 7-1 for the first time since 1993

Buffalo beat Minnesota 23-6 on Monday night and has won four straight. The defense has allowed 16 points over its last two games, after giving up 28.5 a game before the Week 7 bye. The Bills now lead the AFC East, though New England holds the head-to-head tiebreaker.

Source: NFL.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Spencer Brown sits out Thursday with an ankle issue

The starting right tackle missed practice Thursday after a limited Wednesday. Cornerback Christian Benford (wrist), tight end Dalton Kincaid (ankle) and linemen Alec Anderson, Dion Dawkins and Connor McGovern all practiced on a limited basis. Safety Sam Franklin Jr. (shoulder) also popped up late. Game designations come after Friday's practice.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

James Cook carries it 31 times for 107 yards

Cook ran for 107 yards and a late touchdown against Minnesota, after just 33 yards on 15 carries at halftime. He enters Week 10 with 814 rushing yards and eight touchdowns in eight games. His 169 carries rank third in the league.

Source: KTTC / AP ↗

4
Stat of the Day

322: Josh Allen ties Peyton Manning for touchdowns

Allen has 322 regular-season touchdowns, 232 passing, 88 rushing and two receiving. That ties Manning for the most by any player in his first 10 NFL years. Tyler Bass also pushed his franchise-record field goal streak to 24.

Source: KTTC / AP ↗

5
What's Next

Bills visit the Jets on Sunday at 1 p.m. on CBS

Buffalo goes to MetLife Stadium on a short week after Monday night. The Bills lead the league in yards per game (386.4) and points per game (32). Watch whether the banged-up offensive line holds up against a Jets pass rush led by rookie David Bailey.
