Buffalo Bills

7-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bills smother Vikings 23-6 on Monday night for a fourth straight win

Buffalo improved to 7-1 at U.S. Bank Stadium by controlling the ball for 42:32 and holding Minnesota to 17:28 of possession. The defense forced three turnovers, including interceptions by Dorian Williams and Terrel Bernard, and the Vikings went 0-for-7 on third down.

Source: Buffalo Bills ↗

2
Injury Report

Dawkins and Anderson sat out Monday night, replacements held up

Left tackle Dion Dawkins (ribs) and guard Alec Anderson (ankle) missed the Vikings game after sitting out practice Thursday and Friday. Tylan Grable and Austin Corbett filled in. Their status for the Jets game is not yet known.

Source: Buffalo Bills ↗

3
Player Spotlight

James Cook carries 31 times for 107 yards and a score

Cook ran for a 5-yard touchdown and powered a 15-play, 8:32 fourth-quarter drive that was Buffalo's longest of the season. He has a rushing touchdown and at least 75 yards in seven straight games, a team record, and he passed 5,000 career rushing yards on the same drive as Josh Allen.

Source: Buffalo Bills (SI) ↗

4
Stat of the Day

0-for-7: Minnesota's third-down mark against Buffalo

The Vikings never moved the chains on third down, and Carson Wentz finished 8 of 25 for 81 yards with two interceptions. Josh Allen was a modest 21 of 31 for 234 yards, one touchdown and one pick, because the run game and defense did the heavy work.

Source: Buffalo Bills ↗

5
What's Next

Bills visit the Jets on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on CBS

Buffalo plays at MetLife Stadium in Week 10, then returns home to face Miami in Week 11. Watch the status of Dawkins and Anderson up front against a Jets pass rush led by rookie David Bailey.
