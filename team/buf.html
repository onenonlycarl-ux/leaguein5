Buffalo Bills

1
Biggest Story

Bills run past the Jets to improve to 8-1

Buffalo beat the Jets 31-12 at MetLife Stadium in Week 10. Josh Allen threw for 196 yards and a touchdown and ran for two more scores, and James Cook closed the game with 74 of his 157 rushing yards in the final five minutes. The Bills now share the league's best record with San Francisco.

Source: amNY ↗

2
Injury Report

Benford and Williams out, Kincaid doubtful vs. Miami

Cornerback Christian Benford (groin) and linebacker Dorian Williams (calf) are out for Sunday. Tight end Dalton Kincaid (thumb and ankle) is doubtful, and safety Damar Hamlin (toe), tight end Jackson Hawes (shoulder) and lineman T.J. Sanders (shoulder) are questionable. Safety Cole Bishop is also working through a shoulder issue.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

James Cook keeps stacking 75-yard games

Cook ran for 157 yards against the Jets, his eighth straight game with at least 75 rushing yards and a rushing touchdown. Only four other players in NFL history have matched that run. He enters Sunday with 971 rushing yards, second in the league and close to 1,000.

Source: amNY ↗

4
Stat of the Day

11: Josh Allen's rushing touchdowns lead the NFL

Allen has 11 rushing touchdowns, the most in the league. Against Miami in his career he has 4,256 passing yards, 42 touchdown passes and 5 rushing scores in 16 games. Buffalo has won 14 of the last 16 meetings in the series.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

What's Next

Sunday vs. Miami, 1 p.m. ET on CBS

Buffalo hosts the 1-8 Dolphins at Highmark Stadium in Week 11, and the game also streams on Paramount+. Miami has lost 10 straight games in Buffalo, and the Bills' second-ranked run game faces a defense that has allowed 100 rushing yards to six straight opponents.
