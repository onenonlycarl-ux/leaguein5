AFC North · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Cincinnati Bengals

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bengals beat Falcons 34-17 in Madrid for a fourth straight win

Cincinnati improved to 6-2 with its first victory outside the United States. Joe Burrow went 24 of 29 for 271 yards and three touchdowns, with no interceptions, and added a 1-yard sneak for a score. The Bengals now host Pittsburgh with a share of the AFC North lead.

Source: Local 12 ↗

2
Injury Report

Dugger out week to week with a quad injury

At the Madrid practice on Nov. 5, safety Kyle Dugger (quad) did not practice and is week to week. Cornerback DJ Turner II (heel) went from out Wednesday to full Thursday, defensive end Cedric Johnson (ankle and foot) was limited, and safety Jordan Battle (hamstring) was limited both days. The Bengals have not published a post-trip report for the Steelers game in what I found.

Source: A to Z Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Ja'Marr Chase puts up 155 yards in Spain

Chase caught 10 passes for 155 yards and a 47-yard touchdown against Atlanta, and Tee Higgins added five catches and two scores. Burrow said afterward he would have a hard time putting anybody above Chase.

Source: Local 12 ↗

4
Stat of the Day

3: straight touchdown drives to open the Madrid game

Cincinnati scored on its first three possessions after two games of slow starts, finishing with 34 points and 123 rushing yards. Chase had 8 catches for 142 yards in the first half alone, the best first half by a Bengal since 1988, and Chase Brown ran nine times for 57.

Source: A to Z Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Bengals host Steelers on Sunday night with the division on the line

Cincinnati hosts Pittsburgh on Sunday, Nov. 15, at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. Both teams are 6-2 with four-game win streaks, and the Steelers won the first meeting 30-27. Watch the travel angle: teams coming off an international game are 0-5 the next week against opponents coming off a bye.
