AFC North · Tuesday, November 24 · Week 12

Cincinnati Bengals

7-3Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Commanders beat Bengals 22-20 on a walk-off field goal

Evan McPherson's 56-yard field goal put Cincinnati up 20-19 with 46 seconds left. Washington then covered 64 yards in three plays, and Drew Stevens kicked a 26-yard winner as time expired. The loss ended the Bengals' five-game winning streak and dropped them to 7-3. A clock operator error left two seconds on the board that never ran, though it likely did not change the result.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Risner out with knee injury, Cook shakes off ankle scare

Right guard Dalton Risner missed the game with a knee injury, and Jalen Rivers started in his place. Safety Bryan Cook had an ankle scare but returned. Safety Dax Hill, who battled illness during the week, spent the final two drives on the sideline.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Burrow throws for 300 yards and no turnovers in the loss

Joe Burrow went 28 of 40 for 300 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions. Tee Higgins caught two passes for 43 yards and a score, but Ja'Marr Chase managed five catches for 31 yards on nine targets. Burrow also missed Chase deep in the third quarter.

Source: Bleacher Report ↗

4
Stat of the Day

31: Rushing yards for the Bengals against Washington

Cincinnati's run game produced just 31 yards, and a fourth-and-1 quarterback sneak near midfield early in the game got stuffed. The offense still put up 300 passing yards but could not control the clock late. Boye Mafe had three first-half sacks and now has 9.5 on the season.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Bengals host New Orleans on Sunday

Cincinnati hosts the Saints on Sunday, Nov. 29 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS at Paycor Stadium. Watch the right guard spot with Risner's knee in question and whether the defense tightens up in the closing minutes after Monday's collapse.
