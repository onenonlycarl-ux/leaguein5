Cincinnati Bengals

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bengals and Steelers meet at 6-2 with the AFC North lead on the line

Cincinnati rides a four-game winning streak and its best start since 2015 into Sunday night. The Bengals beat Atlanta 31-17 in Madrid last week. A win would put them alone atop the division.

Source: Associated Press via Info News ↗

2
Injury Report

Dugger ruled out as three Bengals return from injured reserve

Safety Kyle Dugger (quadriceps) is out. Jalen Davis, Andrei Iosivas and Brian Parker II were cleared to practice and are listed doubtful, with roster activation decisions due Saturday. Guard Dylan Fairchild (pectoral) was a full participant Friday.

Source: Yahoo Sports (Steelers Wire) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Joe Burrow leads the NFL in three passing categories

Burrow tops the league in completion rate, passing yards per game and touchdown passes. He has thrown at least three touchdowns in each of the last four games. He threw for 282 yards and three scores in the Week 3 loss at Pittsburgh.

Source: Associated Press via Info News ↗

4
Stat of the Day

4: straight games of 30 or more points for Cincinnati

The Bengals have scored at least 30 in each of their four wins, the first time they have done that in a four-game winning streak. Chase, Higgins and Mike Gesicki each have at least six receiving touchdowns through eight games. Only the 2013 Broncos had another trio like it.

Source: Associated Press via Info News ↗

5
What's Next

Bengals host Steelers on Sunday Night Football

Cincinnati hosts Pittsburgh Sunday, Nov. 15 at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. The Bengals rank 25th against the run and allow 5.04 yards per carry, the most in the NFL. Watch how they handle Jaylen Warren.

Source: NFL.com ↗
