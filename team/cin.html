Cincinnati Bengals

5-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bengals head to Madrid with a first-place chase on the line

Cincinnati arrives at the Bernabeu at 5-2 after a 43-36 overtime win over Tennessee. The Bengals are trying to reach 6-2 before they host first-place Pittsburgh. The offense, led by Joe Burrow, drives the whole plan.

Source: BVM Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Howell, Johnson and Turner all questionable for Atlanta

Rookie edge rusher Cashius Howell had wrist surgery, and no timetable has been given. Edge Cedric Johnson and cornerback DJ Turner II are also questionable. Wide receiver Andrei Iosivas remains on injured reserve.

Source: SI ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Tee Higgins sits second in the league with 726 receiving yards

Higgins has piled up 726 yards through seven games, second in the NFL. He caught 10 passes for 155 yards and two touchdowns in the overtime win over the Titans. He pairs with Ja'Marr Chase, who added 11 catches for 141 yards in that game.

Source: BVM Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

462: Joe Burrow's passing yards against Tennessee

Burrow completed 38 of 48 passes for 462 yards and five touchdowns, with three interceptions, in the 43-36 overtime win. He leads the league in completion percentage at 73%. Sources differ on his touchdown total (26 per BVM Sports, 20 per SI), so check before citing it.

Source: Yahoo Sports (SB Nation) ↗

5
What's Next

Falcons in Madrid, Sunday at 9:30 a.m. ET on NFL Network

Cincinnati faces Atlanta (3-5) at the Santiago Bernabeu in Madrid. The Falcons are coming off a 16-0 loss to Tampa Bay. After the trip the Bengals host Pittsburgh on Nov. 15.

Source: SI ↗
