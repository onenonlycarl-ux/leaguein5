1
Biggest Story

Bengals rout Steelers 38-18, own the division

Cincinnati led 24-3 at the half and outgained Pittsburgh 337 yards to 40 in the first two quarters. The win is the Bengals' fifth in a row and puts them alone in first place in the AFC North at 7-2.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Risner leaves with a knee injury

Starting guard Dalton Risner left Sunday night's game with a knee injury. Tee Higgins looked to be limping late but still caught five passes for 66 yards. Watch the practice report this week for both.

Source: NFL.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Chase catches 11 for 231 against Pittsburgh

Ja'Marr Chase had his biggest game of the season with 11 receptions and 231 yards. Joe Burrow went 35 of 44 for 409 yards and four touchdowns, tying Peyton Manning's record of five straight games with at least four offensive touchdowns.

Source: NFL.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

5: straight 30 point games, a Bengals first

Cincinnati has scored at least 30 points in five consecutive games, a franchise record. Burrow also became the third passer in league history with 20 touchdown passes over a five-game span, joining Tom Brady and Peyton Manning.

Source: Ravens.com ↗

5
What's Next

Bengals visit Commanders on Monday night

Cincinnati travels to Northwest Stadium in Landover to face Washington on Monday, Nov. 23 at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. The Commanders are 3-5-1 and rank near the bottom of the league against the pass.

Source: FOX Sports ↗
