Biggest Story

Commanders kick winner as time expires, 22-20

Evan McPherson's 56-yard field goal put Cincinnati up 20-19 with 46 seconds left, but Jayden Daniels hit two deep throws and Drew Stevens won it from 26 yards. The loss snapped the Bengals' win streak and leaves them 7-3 on a short week.

Injury Report

Dugger and Risner questionable, Burks lost to IR

Safety Kyle Dugger (left quadriceps) and guard Dalton Risner (knees) head the Week 12 report. Linebacker Oren Burks went on injured reserve with a groin injury, and Jalen Rivers made his first start of the year at right guard Monday.

Player Spotlight

Boye Mafe piles up three first-half sacks

Mafe set a career high with three sacks, all before halftime, and finished with five tackles. His 9.5 sacks lead the Bengals this season.

Stat of the Day

3,095: Burrow leads the NFL in passing yards

Joe Burrow went 28 of 40 for 300 yards and two touchdowns against Washington, his fourth 300-yard game of the year. He has 29 touchdowns and six interceptions on the season.
