Dallas Cowboys

5-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Cowboys hang 51 on Titans and get back to 5-6

Dallas scored touchdowns on its first six drives and rolled Tennessee 51-30 at home. The win ended a loss to San Francisco and keeps the Cowboys in the NFC East race at 5-6. They now turn to a short week against Philadelphia.

Source: ESPN ↗

2
Injury Report

Dak Prescott wraps his left hand but expects to play

Prescott left the Titans game with his left hand wrapped and said he will be fine. No other Cowboys injuries came out of Sunday, so the short week looks clean so far. Watch Prescott's practice reps before Thursday.

Source: ESPN ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Dak Prescott throws four TDs without a pick

Prescott went 28 of 33 for 325 yards and four touchdowns, and ran one in. CeeDee Lamb caught 11 passes for 132 yards, and George Pickens added seven for 102. Prescott has 11 touchdown passes and one interception over his last three games.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

6 for 6: Cowboys score a TD on every opening drive

Dallas scored touchdowns on its first six possessions against Tennessee. The Cowboys say that has not happened since the stat was first recorded in 1993. It was also their first 50-point game since 2022.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Eagles visit AT&T Stadium on Thanksgiving

Dallas hosts Philadelphia on Thursday, Nov. 26, at 4:30 p.m. ET on Fox. The Cowboys finished with 11 penalties for 128 yards against Tennessee, and the secondary gave up a 59-yard touchdown. Those are the fixes to watch on a short week.
