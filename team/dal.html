NFC East · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Dallas Cowboys

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Cowboys steal one from Indianapolis, 27-23

Javonte Williams plunged in from the 1-yard line with 1:08 left to put Dallas ahead of the Colts, and Caleb Downs sealed it with an interception of Daniel Jones with 35 seconds to play. It was the Cowboys' first win of the year when they scored fewer than 30 points. Dallas moved to 4-5 and now opens a three-game homestand.

Source: NBC DFW ↗

2
Injury Report

Ferguson, Goodson and Locke face multiple missed weeks

Tight end Jake Ferguson hurt his left knee against the Colts, and coach Brian Schottenheimer says he will miss multiple weeks. Running back Tyler Goodson also hurt a knee and is out for a stretch, while safety PJ Locke suffered his second concussion in two weeks and is out against the 49ers. George Pickens (hamstring), KaVontae Turpin (ribs), DeMarvion Overshown and Joey Porter Jr. (ankle) are in the rehab group, with their Sunday status still open.

Source: DallasCowboys.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

CeeDee Lamb piles up 195 yards in Indianapolis

Lamb caught 10 passes for 195 yards and a touchdown, even with a lost fumble on his ledger. Dak Prescott threw for 335 yards and two scores, and Williams added 101 yards on 20 carries. With Pickens inactive, Lamb carried the receiver room.

Source: NBC DFW ↗

4
Stat of the Day

508: Dallas outgained Indianapolis by 237 yards and barely won

The Cowboys piled up 508 yards to the Colts' 271, yet the game came down to the final minute. Dallas drew 14 penalties for 153 yards and lost two fumbles, one by Prescott that set up an Indianapolis touchdown. Clean those up and this offense wins by two scores.

Source: NBC DFW ↗

5
What's Next

San Francisco visits AT&T Stadium Sunday

Dallas hosts the 49ers on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 4:25 p.m. ET on Fox in Arlington. It opens a three-game homestand. Watch how the Cowboys fill the tight end spot with Ferguson out and whether Pickens returns from his hamstring injury.
