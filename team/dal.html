1
Biggest Story

Cowboys blow a 21-point lead and lose 39-38 on Thanksgiving

Dallas led 31-10 in the third quarter and lost to Philadelphia on a 59-yard Jake Elliott field goal with two seconds left. The Eagles scored the final 15 points of the game. The loss leaves the Cowboys chasing the division leading Giants.

Source: GSM Global News ↗

2
Injury Report

Quinnen Williams leaves a play hurt in the third quarter

The Eagles live blog logged Quinnen Williams as injured on a third quarter play against Philadelphia. It gave no diagnosis and no return status, so his availability for the Seattle trip is unknown. Watch the first practice report next week.

Source: PhiladelphiaEagles.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Javonte Williams scores three times in a loss

Williams carried 29 times for 101 yards and two touchdowns, then added a receiving touchdown. Dak Prescott went 19 of 28 for 291 yards, two touchdowns and an interception. CeeDee Lamb caught seven passes for 134 yards.

Source: GSM Global News ↗

4
Stat of the Day

38: points Dallas scored and still lost

The Cowboys scored 38 against the Eagles and lost by one. They also led by 21 points. Philadelphia's comeback from that deficit is the largest in Thanksgiving history.

Source: GSM Global News ↗

5
What's Next

Cowboys head to Seattle on Monday night, Dec. 7

Dallas plays at the Seahawks on Monday, Dec. 7 at 7:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. The Cowboys then take their bye in Week 14. They have a quick turnaround from a one-point loss, so watch how the defense closes games.
