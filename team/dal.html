NFC East · Saturday, November 14 · Week 10

Dallas Cowboys

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Cowboys escape Indianapolis and now face the 7-1 49ers

Dallas beat the Colts 27-23 when Javonte Williams punched in a one-yard touchdown with just over a minute left, then Caleb Downs picked off Daniel Jones to end it. The Cowboys sit at 4-5 and want back-to-back wins for the first time in a while. San Francisco comes to AT&T Stadium at 7-1 and has beaten Dallas four straight times.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Pickens clears the report, but the secondary is thin

George Pickens came off the injury report Friday and is expected to play after missing last week's game. Tight end Jake Ferguson and running back Tyler Goodson are out. Cornerbacks Joey Porter Jr. and DaRon Bland are both questionable, and safety Juanyeh Thomas is doubtful.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

3
Player Spotlight

CeeDee Lamb piles up 195 yards and a record

Lamb caught 10 passes for 195 yards and a touchdown against Indianapolis. The 10 catches pushed his career total to 641, the most by any player before turning 28. He also lost a late fumble, so the Cowboys want the yards without the mistakes this week.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

14: Penalties the Cowboys took in Indianapolis

Dallas drew 14 flags for 153 yards in the win over the Colts. Dak Prescott said the team must clean that up against a San Francisco defense that ranks sixth in points allowed at 19.5 per game. The Cowboys also have not yet beaten a team with a winning record this season.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

5
What's Next

49ers visit AT&T Stadium Sunday at 4:25 ET

Dallas hosts San Francisco on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 4:25 p.m. ET on FOX. Watch whether the Cowboys protect Prescott against Nick Bosa and DeForest Buckner, and whether they stop Christian McCaffrey in the passing game. Quinnen Williams has 4 sacks over his last four games.

Source: Last Word on Sports ↗
