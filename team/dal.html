Dallas Cowboys

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Cowboys steal one in Indianapolis, 27-23

Dallas blew a 17-7 halftime lead and still beat the Colts. Dak Prescott led a four-play, 65-yard touchdown drive that took 29 seconds, and Javonte Williams punched in the winning score from the 1. The Cowboys sit at 4-5 and host San Francisco next.

Source: DallasCowboys.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Locke out again, Ferguson and Goodson down for weeks

Safety P.J. Locke is out after what looked like another concussion. Tight end Jake Ferguson and running back Tyler Goodson both have knee injuries and should miss multiple weeks. George Pickens (hamstring) and Joey Porter Jr. moved from out to questionable, and Donovan Ezeiruaku has an ankle sprain with his status open. Statuses are from Monday's update, so check the final Friday designations.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

CeeDee Lamb piles up 195 yards in Indy

Lamb caught 10 passes for 195 yards and a touchdown against the Colts. He did it with Pickens ruled out 90 minutes before kickoff. Prescott finished 21 of 34 for 335 yards and two touchdowns.

Source: DallasCowboys.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

508: Cowboys total yards in the win over Indianapolis

Dallas gained 508 yards against the Colts and still needed a last-minute drive to win. The offense also took 14 penalties for 153 yards, a flag count that kept the Colts in it. The Cowboys have scored 230 points and allowed 230 through nine games.

Source: Football Nation USA ↗

5
What's Next

49ers at AT&T Stadium, Sunday on FOX

Dallas hosts San Francisco (7-1) on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 4:25 p.m. ET on FOX. The Niners lead the NFC West and average 6.2 yards per play. Watch Dallas on third down, where it has converted 45.3 percent, and the penalty count.
