NFC East · Saturday, November 7 · Week 9

Dallas Cowboys

3-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Cowboys blow a two-score lead and fall to Arizona in overtime

Dallas led 27-17 with about five minutes left and lost 33-30. Arizona scored touchdowns on four straight possessions after Gardner Minshew replaced Jacoby Brissett late in the third quarter. Chad Ryland kicked a 42-yard field goal in overtime to finish it, and the Cowboys went three-and-out on their extra possession.

Source: Dallas Cowboys ↗

2
Injury Report

Pickens and Porter Jr. questionable for Colts game

Wideout George Pickens (hamstring) and cornerback Joey Porter Jr. (ankle) are questionable for Sunday, along with linebacker DeMarvion Overshown, tight end Jake Ferguson and Luke Schoonmaker. Rookie Malachi Lawrence (ankle) and KaVontae Turpin (ribs) are out. Caleb Downs and P.J. Locke cleared concussion protocol.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Quinnen Williams piles up two more sacks against Arizona

Williams had two sacks in the loss, which gives him a team-high five this season. The Cowboys sacked Arizona five times and forced two turnovers, including a Shemar James interception. The defense held up until the Cardinals changed quarterbacks.

Source: Dallas Cowboys ↗

4
Stat of the Day

39.6: passer rating against Joey Porter Jr. in coverage

Porter has allowed 3 catches on 10 targets since Dallas acquired him after Week 4. He played 94 percent of Pittsburgh's snaps last year but only 53 percent in Dallas, as injuries have limited him. His ankle is the one to watch this week.

Source: Heavy.com ↗

5
What's Next

At Colts, Sunday 1:00 p.m. ET on FOX

Dallas visits Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis at 3-5, and a loss would drop them to 3-6. Watch whether the secondary gets Porter and Downs back together. The Cowboys lead the all-time series 11-6.
