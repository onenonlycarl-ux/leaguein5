Tampa Bay Buccaneers

3-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bowles says the Bucs have no margin left

Tampa Bay fell 31-21 at Detroit to drop to 3-7, its first loss by more than one score this season. Coach Todd Bowles told reporters the team is out of mulligans. Carolina now leads the division by two games, so Monday night carries real weight.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Mayfield practices fully, Winfield and Morrison sit

Quarterback Baker Mayfield (foot, right thumb) was listed as a full participant on Thursday's estimated report. Safety Antoine Winfield Jr. (ankle) and cornerback Benjamin Morrison (hamstring and back) did not practice. The final report is due Saturday.

Source: Heavy.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie Ted Hurst III scores twice in Detroit

Hurst caught five passes for 56 yards and two touchdowns against the Lions, including a 5-yard score with eight seconds left. He gave Mayfield a bright spot in a game with four sacks and a wiped-out 80-yard Bucky Irving touchdown.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

159.5: Tampa Bay's rushing yards per game, first in the NFL

The Bucs lead the league on the ground at 159.5 yards a game and rank second against the run at 82.1. Their passing offense ranks 32nd at 143.1. Irving has 893 rushing yards and needs 54 more to reach the franchise top 10.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗

5
What's Next

Vs. Carolina, Monday 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN

The Bucs host the 5-5 Panthers at Raymond James Stadium on Nov. 30. Tampa Bay won the Week 7 meeting 43-13 and has taken 11 of the last 13 in the series. A win would cut the division gap to one game.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗
