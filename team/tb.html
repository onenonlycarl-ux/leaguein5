NFC South · Saturday, November 7 · Week 9

Tampa Bay Buccaneers

3-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bucs beat Falcons 16-0 without completing a pass

Tampa Bay shut out Atlanta on a wet Sunday and ran for about 324 yards, a team single-game record. Baker Mayfield went 0 for 3 in his second game back from a thumb fracture. The defense produced four interceptions off Michael Penix Jr. The win is Tampa Bay's second in a row.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Diaby out, Egbuka questionable for Chicago

Outside linebacker Yaya Diaby is out with a knee injury after missing all three practices. Receiver Emeka Egbuka (foot) is questionable after full practices Thursday and Friday. Tackle Tristan Wirfs (toe) was limited Friday and carries no game designation. Mayfield practiced fully with his right thumb.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bucky Irving rushes for a career-high 170 yards

Irving carried 27 times for 170 yards and a 4-yard touchdown against Atlanta, a career high. He leads the team with 728 rushing yards and six touchdowns this season. Kenny Gainwell added 140 yards, including a 6-yard run that set the franchise single-game rushing record.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

0: pass completions in a win, a first since 1974

Tampa Bay is the first team to win without a completion since Buffalo on Sept. 29, 1974, according to the Chicago Sun-Times. The Bucs ran for 324 yards, a single-game team record. Kicker Chase McLaughlin made all three of his field goals to move to 19 for 19 this season.

Source: Chicago Sun-Times ↗

5
What's Next

Bucs at Bears, Sunday 8:20 PM ET on NBC

Tampa Bay (3-5) visits Chicago (6-2) at Soldier Field on Sunday Night Football, with Mike Tirico and Cris Collinsworth on the call. The Bucs lead the league in rushing offense and face Chicago's fourth-ranked run defense. Bears quarterback Caleb Williams carries a 104.6 passer rating, and Chicago added defensive lineman Jeffery Simmons.
