Carolina Panthers

3-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Panthers fire defensive coordinator Ejiro Evero

Carolina fired Evero on Oct. 30 after a 27-19 loss at Green Bay. Coach Dave Canales pointed to a collection of information over time and the need to build the defense's infrastructure. Defensive line coach Todd Wash is now interim coordinator and will call the plays. The defense ranked 22nd overall and last against the run.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Four Panthers questionable, Moton out for the year

Corey Bullock, Will Lee III, Damien Lewis and Jaelan Phillips are listed as questionable for Denver. Tackle Taylor Moton, cornerback Jaycee Horn and Jonathon Brooks are on injured reserve, and Ikem Ekwonu is on the PUP list. Bullock left the Green Bay game with a quad injury, and Monroe Freeling started in Moton's place at right tackle.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bryce Young absorbs six sacks behind a hurt line

Young went down six times at Green Bay and faced pressure on 48.9 percent of his dropbacks, per Next Gen Stats. Under pressure he completed 6 of 16 passes for 46 yards and an interception. He wore a brace on his left knee, called it fine, and said injuries are no excuse. He has taken 64 pressures through seven games, second in the league.

Source: ABC11 ↗

4
Stat of the Day

12: games of 200-plus rushing yards allowed since 2024

Evero's defenses allowed 12 games of 200 or more rushing yards since 2024, the most in the league. Chicago ran for 291 yards and six touchdowns in Week 1, and Tampa Bay ran for 258 yards against Carolina in Week 7. Wash gets his first game plan against Denver on Sunday.

Source: Atlanta Journal-Constitution (AP) ↗

5
What's Next

Panthers host Broncos, Sunday 1 PM ET on CBS

Carolina (3-4, first in the NFC South) hosts Denver (6-2) at Bank of America Stadium on Sunday at 1 PM ET on CBS. The Panthers have lost two straight and are 0-4 on the road. Watch the run defense under Wash against a Denver offense that leans on its defense to win games.

Source: Panthers.com ↗
