NFC South · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

Carolina Panthers

3-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Panthers and Saints meet for first place at 3-5

Carolina has lost three straight but sits tied atop the NFC South because every team in the division is under .500. The Panthers made no trades at the deadline, the second year in a row for GM Dan Morgan. Coach Dave Canales fired defensive coordinator Ejiro Evero on Oct. 30 and promoted Todd Wash. A win Sunday gives Carolina sole possession of first.

Source: The Ticket (Associated Press) ↗

2
Injury Report

Reinforcements back at practice, Okereke sits

Running back Jonathon Brooks (groin), cornerback Mike Jackson (groin), receiver Xavier Legette (knee) and guard Brady Christensen (hamstring) all practiced fully Thursday after opening their practice windows from injured reserve. Linebacker Bobby Okereke (foot) did not practice for a second day. Edge Jaelan Phillips (hamstring) and guard Damien Lewis (knee) were limited.

Source: New Orleans Saints (official injury report) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bryce Young throws for three scores in loss

Young went 28 of 39 for 276 yards and three touchdowns against Denver, with a 106.4 passer rating. Darren Waller caught eight passes for 90 yards and a score, and Tetairoa McMillan added a touchdown. Nik Bonitto's strip-sack with 1:24 left sealed the 26-21 loss.

Source: Panthers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

13: Panthers penalties against Denver for 119 yards

Carolina tied for the third most penalties in team history, and Denver earned six first downs by penalty, matching the team record for most allowed that way. Five of the defense's seven flags were for pass interference, with the top four cornerbacks out hurt.

Source: Panthers.com ↗

5
What's Next

Panthers at Saints on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on FOX

The game is at the Caesars Superdome, with first place in the division on the line. Carolina has to protect Young against a Saints front led by Chase Young, who has eight sacks, and Carl Granderson, who has 7.5. Watch how the secondary holds up with Will Lee III and Mike Jackson back at practice.

Source: New Orleans Saints ↗
