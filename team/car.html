Biggest Story

Panthers and Saints play for NFC South lead at 3-5

Carolina and New Orleans are both 3-5, so Sunday's winner takes sole possession of first place in the division. Carolina will try to protect Bryce Young, who has taken 26 sacks. Interim defensive coordinator Todd Wash calls his second game since Ejiro Evero was fired on Oct. 30.

Source: BVM Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Jaelan Phillips questionable as Okereke sits again

Linebacker Bobby Okereke (foot) is out for a second straight game. Outside linebacker Jaelan Phillips practiced fully Friday and is questionable after missing three weeks with a hamstring strain. Cornerback Will Lee III is also questionable, while Mike Jackson and Jonathon Brooks are doubtful.

Source: Panthers.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bryce Young faces a pass rush that has sacked 26 times

Young has been sacked 26 times this season, yet Carolina ranks fourth in the NFL in passing offense. He is 1-5 all time against New Orleans and 0-3 at the Superdome. Chase Young (8 sacks) and Carl Granderson (7.5) lead the Saints rush.

Source: BVM Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

13 of 14: Ryan Fitzgerald on field goals this season

Fitzgerald missed against Denver in Week 9 for his first failed field goal of the year. Coach Dave Canales said he trusts the kicker, and special teams coordinator Tracy Smith said the misses look alike, which makes them easier to fix. Fitzgerald has also missed five extra points.

Source: Panthers.com ↗

5
What's Next

Panthers visit Saints Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on FOX

Carolina (3-5) travels to the Caesars Superdome. New Orleans has won five of the last six meetings, including a 20-17 win last December on a field goal with two seconds left. Watch Young against the Saints pass rush and whether Phillips gets back on the field.
