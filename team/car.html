1
Biggest Story

Fitzgerald's field goal lifts Panthers past Ravens, 35-33

Ryan Fitzgerald hit a 37-yard field goal with 1:44 left, and Tyrel Dodson intercepted Jacoby Brissett's last heave with 33 seconds to go. Carolina is 5-5 and two games clear in the NFC South with seven left. It is the Panthers' first two-game division lead since the end of 2015.

Source: ESPN ↗

2
Injury Report

Coker clears concussion protocol; Horn still waits

Receiver Jalen Coker cleared the concussion protocol after a late hit against Baltimore, and Dave Canales said he is good to go. Cornerback Jaycee Horn has not opened his practice window yet. Tackle Brady Christensen has practiced for three weeks, so the team must activate him or move him to injured reserve.

Source: Panthers.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Chuba Hubbard runs for 120 yards and two scores

Hubbard carried 27 times for 120 yards and two touchdowns against the Ravens. He became the second Panther after Christian McCaffrey to reach 1,000 scrimmage yards and 10 touchdowns in the first 10 games of a season. He has topped 100 scrimmage yards in six games this year.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

14: game-winning drives for Bryce Young

Young led the 14th game-winning drive of his career against Baltimore. He threw for 254 yards and two touchdowns. Carolina has now won two straight to get back to .500.

Source: NFL.com ↗

What's Next

Panthers visit Tampa Bay on Monday Night Football

Carolina plays at Tampa Bay on Monday, Nov. 30 at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. The Bucs beat the Panthers 43-13 in Charlotte in Week 7, so Carolina has a score to settle and a division lead to protect.
