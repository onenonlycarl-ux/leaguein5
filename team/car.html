NFC South · Wednesday, November 25 · Week 12

Carolina Panthers

5-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Panthers outlast Ravens 35-33 and keep the division lead

Carolina has won two straight after a three-game skid and sits alone atop the NFC South at 5-5. Chuba Hubbard's third-quarter touchdown gave the Panthers the lead for good. Bryce Young threw for 254 yards and two touchdowns.

Source: AL.com via Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Okereke out of action, Horn progressing

Linebacker Bobby Okereke (foot) was inactive in Weeks 10 and 11 and has no designation yet. Cornerback Jaycee Horn is recovering from a quadriceps tear and is progressing well. Tackle Taylor Moton is out for the season with a fibula fracture, and Ikem Ekwonu is on the PUP list.

Source: DraftKings Network ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Chuba Hubbard is on a career-best rushing pace

Hubbard has 831 rushing yards and nine rushing touchdowns, sixth in the NFL in yards, and averages a career-high 83.1 yards per game at 4.4 yards per carry. He is about to face the Bucs, who allow just 82.1 rushing yards per game, second in the league.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

+3: Carolina's turnover margin in the win over Baltimore

The Panthers committed one turnover and took the ball away four times. That margin covered 421 yards allowed, including 157 on the ground. Carolina finished with 378 yards of offense and 147 rushing yards, and went 3 for 6 in the red zone.

Source: Football Nation USA ↗

5
What's Next

At Tampa Bay on Monday night, 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN

Carolina visits the 3-7 Buccaneers on Nov. 30 and can reach 6-5 with a win. Tampa Bay won the first meeting 43-13 in Week 7, and Baker Mayfield is 7-1 against Carolina in his career. The Panthers need Hubbard and the run game to answer that.

Source: Buccaneers.com ↗
