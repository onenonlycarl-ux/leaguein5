1
Biggest Story

Overturned pick at the 1 sinks Panthers

Carolina led Denver 14-13 late in the third quarter with the ball at the Broncos' 1 when a Bryce Young pass to Tommy Tremble was ruled an interception on review. Denver drove 80 yards for the lead and won 26-21. The Panthers have lost three straight, but all four NFC South teams have three wins, so they still share first place.

Source: Panthers.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Okereke sits out; Hubbard rests

Linebacker Bobby Okereke (foot) did not practice on Wednesday, and running back Chuba Hubbard sat out with a rest designation. Edge rusher Jaelan Phillips (hamstring) practiced fully, as did guard Damien Lewis (knee). The Panthers also leaned on two cornerbacks promoted from the practice squad on Sunday.

Source: Saints.com injury report ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Young throws three scores in the loss

Bryce Young completed 28 of 39 passes for 276 yards and three touchdowns, a 106.4 passer rating. Tight end Darren Waller caught eight passes for 90 yards and a score, both season highs.

Source: Panthers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

13: penalties for 119 yards vs. Denver

That total tied for the third most in franchise history. Many of the flags landed on the two cornerbacks elevated from the practice squad. Carolina still piled up 353 yards and went 3-for-3 on fourth down.

What's Next

Division showdown in the Superdome

Carolina visits New Orleans on Sunday, Nov. 15 at noon CT (1 p.m. ET) on Fox. Both teams are 3-5 and tied atop the NFC South. Watch the Panthers' pass protection and their penalty count.
