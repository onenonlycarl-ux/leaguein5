NFC North · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Minnesota Vikings

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bills beat Vikings 23-6 on Monday night

Buffalo handled Minnesota 23-6 at U.S. Bank Stadium, dropping the Vikings to 6-2. Carson Wentz started in place of the injured Kyler Murray and threw two interceptions. James Cook ran 31 times for 107 yards and a touchdown. Max Brosmer finished at quarterback.

Source: FOX Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Kyler Murray's hamstring is a week-to-week issue

Murray strained his hamstring on a read-option run in the fourth quarter at Detroit and sat out Monday. Kevin O'Connell said the team hopes he can return for the Nov. 15 game at Green Bay but will evaluate him week to week. The Vikings are not considering injured reserve. Receiver Jauan Jennings (calf) and running back Demond Claiborne (knee) were also ruled out for the Bills game.

Source: AP via Alaska's News Source ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Justin Jefferson held to three catches by Buffalo

Jefferson caught three passes for 58 yards against the Bills. With Wentz at quarterback this season he had averaged 6.4 catches and 89.1 yards over seven games, per the team's game preview. Minnesota's passing game managed 81 yards on Wentz's 8-of-25 night.

Source: TheScore ↗

4
Stat of the Day

81: Carson Wentz's passing yards on 25 attempts

Wentz completed 8 of 25 passes for 81 yards, no touchdowns and two interceptions against Buffalo. Brosmer went 6 of 7 for 58 yards in relief. The Vikings had entered the night with the league's top scoring defense at 13.3 points per game allowed.

Source: FOX Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Vikings at Packers with the NFC North in play

Minnesota travels to Lambeau Field on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 1 p.m. ET on FOX. The big question is who starts at quarterback: Murray if the hamstring allows, otherwise Wentz again. The Packers are 6-3.
