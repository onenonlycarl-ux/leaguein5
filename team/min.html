NFC North · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

Minnesota Vikings

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Vikings reacquire Kirk Cousins for a 2027 third-round pick

Minnesota sent a third-round pick to Las Vegas for Cousins on deadline day. Coach Kevin O'Connell would not promise a start Sunday and said the plan is to make sure Cousins feels comfortable in the offense. New general manager Nolan Teasley said the trade had been discussed for weeks and was not a reaction to Carson Wentz's bad night.

Source: Fox Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Murray still sidelined with a hamstring strain

Kyler Murray strained his hamstring on a read-option run at Detroit on Nov. 1 and missed the Buffalo game. O'Connell said the team wants to avoid making it worse, and Murray was not ready to practice this week. Wentz was checked for a lower leg injury on Monday night, and Max Brosmer finished the game.

Source: MPR News (AP) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jefferson draws 11 targets and catches three

Justin Jefferson caught three passes for 58 yards on 11 targets in the 23-6 loss to Buffalo. Wentz went 8 of 25 for 81 yards with three turnovers, and Minnesota gained 178 yards. Cousins was second in the league with 20 touchdown passes for Las Vegas this season.

Source: MPR News (AP) ↗

4
Stat of the Day

178: Total yards for Minnesota in the loss to Buffalo

The Vikings never crossed the Bills' 48-yard line in the first half and managed 178 yards on Monday night. Their defense still sacked Josh Allen four times. O'Connell said afterward, "Put tonight 100% on me."

Source: MPR News (AP) ↗

5
What's Next

At Green Bay, Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on Fox

The Vikings (6-2) visit the Packers (6-3) at Lambeau Field. Cousins is the likely starter, but O'Connell has not committed to it. Watch for how quickly the offense learns to protect him against a Green Bay pass rush.

Source: Fox Sports ↗
