Washington Commanders

3-5-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Commanders host Bengals on Monday Night Football

Washington is 3-5-1 and plays Cincinnati tonight at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. The Commanders beat the Giants 24-21 last Thursday on a late Jayden Daniels touchdown pass. Former Washington tackle Jonathan Allen returns to Northwest Stadium with the Bengals.

Source: Commanders.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Four Commanders listed questionable for Bengals

Running backs Jacory Croskey-Merritt (groin) and Rachaad White (heel), center Lucas Patrick (concussion) and cornerback Amik Robertson (ankle) were listed questionable. Croskey-Merritt, White and Patrick looked likely to play. Daniels (left elbow) practiced fully.

Source: Commanders.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jayden Daniels leads another late comeback

Daniels went 23 of 33 for 249 yards and three touchdowns against the Giants. Stefon Diggs caught the winner from 32 yards out with 57 seconds left, and Rasul Douglas sealed it with an interception. Washington is 5-0-1 in its last six against New York.

Source: Associated Press via News Tribune ↗

4
Stat of the Day

207: Daniels' interception-free streak ends

Daniels had thrown 207 straight passes without an interception before the Giants game. He then threw two in the 24-21 win. He still finished with three touchdown passes.

Source: Associated Press via News Tribune ↗

5
What's Next

Bengals visit Landover tonight on ESPN

Kickoff is 8:15 p.m. ET at Northwest Stadium. Cincinnati ranks first in the league in passing offense at 298.8 yards per game, and Washington ranks 31st in pass defense. Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase test a defense that gave up 293 passing yards to the Giants.
