Washington Commanders

3-5-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Daniels and Diggs rally Washington past the Giants

Jayden Daniels threw two fourth-quarter interceptions, then led a three-play, 71-yard drive that ended with a 32-yard touchdown to Stefon Diggs with 57 seconds left. Rasul Douglas then intercepted J.J. McCarthy to seal the 24-21 win. Washington is 3-5-1 and two games behind the Giants.

Source: ESPN ↗

2
Injury Report

Patrick and Yankoff out, Okonkwo returns

Center Lucas Patrick (concussion) and tight end Colson Yankoff (knee) were ruled out Thursday, and Nick Allegretti started at center. Tight end Chig Okonkwo left with a back injury in the second quarter and came back after halftime.

Source: Commanders.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Sonny Styles piles up 13 tackles in the win

The rookie linebacker made 13 tackles Thursday, including a third-down tackle for loss on McCarthy in the fourth quarter. He leads Washington with 80 tackles this season. His 19 percent pressure rate is the best among rookie defenders.

Source: Commanders.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

4 for 4: Ben Sinnott's night after one catch all year

Sinnott caught all four of his targets for 42 yards and a touchdown that gave Washington a 10-7 lead late in the first half. He had only one catch before Thursday. The defense has also held its last four opponents to 23 points or fewer.

Source: Commanders.com ↗

What's Next

Bengals visit Monday, Nov. 23 at 8:15 ET

Washington hosts Cincinnati on Monday night, Nov. 23, at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN. The Commanders get extra rest after Thursday's game. Watch whether the line holds up with Patrick's concussion status unsettled.
