Washington Commanders

3-5-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Daniels shakes off two picks, Diggs wins it with 57 seconds left

Jayden Daniels threw two fourth-quarter interceptions while Washington trailed 21-17, then hit Stefon Diggs for a 32-yard touchdown with 57 seconds left. The 24-21 win moved Washington to 3-5-1 and keeps their season alive. The Commanders hold the head-to-head edge on New York at 1-0-1, though their playoff odds sit near 19 percent.

Source: NFL.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Okonkwo leaves with back injury; McLaurin still out

Tight end Chig Okonkwo missed much of Thursday's game with a back injury. Terry McLaurin and Jacory Croskey-Merritt did not play.

Source: NFL.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Sonny Styles leads Washington with 13 tackles

Styles made 13 tackles, five of them solo, and one tackle for loss against the Giants. Frankie Luvu forced a Malik Nabers fumble that Jeremy Reaves recovered, the first fumble recovery of Reaves' career. Dan Quinn's challenge also overturned a Giants first down late and forced a punt.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

7 of 14: Washington's third-down conversions against the Giants

The Commanders converted seven of 14 third downs while holding New York to four of 12. Daniels had thrown no interceptions through eight games before the two late ones, and he is now 4-0-1 against the Giants in his career. Washington was flagged only four times for 17 yards.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

What's Next

Bengals on Monday night, Nov. 23, at Northwest Stadium

Washington gets extra rest before hosting Cincinnati on Monday, Nov. 23. Kickoff time and network were not stated in the sources reviewed. Watch whether the offense can keep converting on third down and protect Daniels.
