NFC East · Wednesday, November 25 · Week 12

Washington Commanders

4-5-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Commanders beat Bengals 22-20 on a field goal at the gun

Drew Stevens kicked the winner as time expired on Monday night. It was Washington's second straight win and its third straight primetime win, a first since 2007. The Commanders trail the Giants by two games in the division.

Source: ESPN ↗

2
Injury Report

Robertson sat out Monday as the reserve list stays long

Cornerback Amik Robertson (ankle) was inactive against Cincinnati. Terry McLaurin (torn patellar tendon), Jaylin Lane (ankle), Daron Payne (foot) and Javon Kinlaw (knee) are on reserve. Laremy Tunsil (triceps) is not expected back this regular season.

Source: DraftKings Network ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie Antonio Williams catches nine for 122

Williams had his biggest night as a pro, with nine receptions for 122 yards. Jayden Daniels threw both passes on the final drive to him, 22 and 42 yards. Daniels finished 23 of 35 for 289 yards and ran for 55 more.

Source: Commanders.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

6: straight scoring drives of 50-plus yards

Washington became the first team in 45 seasons to string together six straight scoring drives of 50 or more yards, per ESPN Research. The Commanders gained 428 net yards against Cincinnati. Five of the second-half scoring drives ended in field goals.

Source: Commanders.com ↗

What's Next

Commanders at Cardinals on Sunday, 4:25 ET on FOX

Washington plays at Arizona on Sunday, Nov. 29, at 4:25 p.m. ET on FOX. The Cardinals are 3-8. Tennessee, 1-9, follows at home.

Source: ESPN ↗
