AFC East · Wednesday, November 18 · Week 11

Miami Dolphins

1-8Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Dolphins cut Zane Gonzalez after a one-score loss

Miami lost 24-22 at Indianapolis and released kicker Zane Gonzalez on Monday after he missed two field goals and an extra point. Coach Jeff Hafley said the team has not decided whether to sign one of about six kickers who auditioned or hold more workouts. Daniel Carlson went 12 for 12 in his workout last week.

Source: Miami Herald via Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Willis on IR, defensive line thinned

Quarterback Malik Willis is on injured reserve with a core muscle injury and will miss at least four games, including Buffalo. Surgery is possible but not decided. DT Zeek Biggers hurt a knee and DT Jordan Phillips left with an arm in a sling. Zach Sieler is out for the season with a torn biceps, and center Aaron Brewer remains in concussion protocol.

Source: Miami Herald via Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie CB Chris Johnson takes one to the house

Johnson returned an interception 59 yards for a touchdown against the Colts. It was his second pick of the season. He also had a pass breakup and tackles for loss.

Source: Last Word on NFL ↗

4
Stat of the Day

1 for 3: Miami's kicker on field goals

Gonzalez made one of three field goals and one of two extra points in a two-point loss. Miami also failed a two-point try after a missed extra point and trailed by two at the end.

Source: Last Word on NFL ↗

What's Next

Miami heads to Buffalo as a big underdog

The Dolphins visit the 8-1 Bills on Sunday, Nov. 22, at 1 p.m. ET on CBS. Kyle McCord starts again at quarterback after his first career start, and Miami needs a kicker before kickoff.
