AFC East · Monday, November 16 · Week 10

Miami Dolphins

1-8Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Dolphins lose 24-22 to Colts and drop to 1-8

Miami never led in Kyle McCord's first career start. A strip-sack gave Indianapolis a 21-7 lead in the third quarter, and a late McCord touchdown run cut it to two. The Dolphins could not convert after that, and the Colts snapped a five-game losing streak.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Zach Sieler is out for the season with a biceps injury

The Dolphins lost defensive tackle Zach Sieler for the year. Zeek Biggers (knee) and Jordan Phillips (arm) also got hurt Sunday, which thins the interior further. Center Aaron Brewer missed the game with a concussion, and Andrew Meyer started in his place and played through a stinger.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Chop Robinson has four sacks in his last four games

Robinson picked up his fifth sack of the season against Indianapolis, plus five tackles and two quarterback hits. His pass rush win rate of about 36 percent leads the league. Miami's defense also held Daniel Jones to 108 passing yards and two interceptions.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

7: points Zane Gonzalez's three misses cost Miami

Gonzalez missed a 47-yard field goal, a 51-yarder and an extra point in a two-point loss. He went 1 of 3 on field goals and has missed kicks in three straight games. He is Miami's second kicker this season, after Riley Patterson was waived following Week 5.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Miami visits Buffalo on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on CBS

The Dolphins play at Highmark Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS. Watch the line of scrimmage, where Miami is down to thin tackle depth against a Bills offense that rushed well Sunday. The Dolphins then host the Jets in Week 12.
