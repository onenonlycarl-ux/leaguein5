AFC East · Saturday, November 7 · Week 9

Miami Dolphins

1-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Dolphins run game must replace De'Von Achane

Achane is on injured reserve and out for the season, so Ollie Gordon II becomes the lead back. The Dolphins fell to 1-6 after a 24-19 loss to New England on Nov. 1, when the ground game stalled.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

2
Injury Report

Backup backs and Willis practice with ailments

Running back Jaylen Wright was upgraded to limited Thursday after missing Wednesday with a quad injury. Kaytron Allen, Zach Sieler and Chop Robinson were also limited. Quarterback Malik Willis and rookie guard Kadyn Proctor, who cleared concussion protocol, practiced fully.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Malik Willis throws for 211 yards behind a leaky line

Willis completed 21 of 32 passes for 211 yards and two touchdowns against New England, including a 35-yard strike to Kevin Coleman Jr. late. He was sacked five times and threw one interception.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

74: Dolphins rushing yards against New England

Miami ran for 74 yards in Week 8, ending a run of games with at least 100. The team had 217 against Cincinnati in Week 5 and 156 against the Jets in Week 7.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

5
What's Next

Dolphins host Lions on Sunday at 1 p.m. on CBS

Miami hosts Detroit on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS. The Lions rank 32nd against the pass but ninth against the run, so watch whether Willis can attack through the air.

Source: NFL.com ↗
