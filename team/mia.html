AFC East · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

Miami Dolphins

1-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Kyle McCord makes first start after Willis goes to IR

Malik Willis landed on injured reserve with a groin injury and will miss four games. McCord, 24, starts Sunday at Indianapolis for a 1-7 Dolphins team. Willis can return for the Dec. 13 home game against Chicago.

Source: NFL.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Dolphins lose Willis, Sieler and center Brewer is in protocol

Willis is out at least four weeks. Defensive tackle Zach Sieler is done for the year with a torn biceps. Starting center Aaron Brewer is in the concussion protocol, which puts Miami's line at risk on McCord's debut. Running back De'Von Achane is also out for the season.

Source: WSVN 7News ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Kyle McCord brings big-college numbers to the NFL

McCord threw for 4,779 yards at Syracuse in 2024, an ACC single-season record that also led the FBS. He will be the 29th different Dolphins starter since Dan Marino retired. Offensive coordinator Bobby Slowik expects fewer deep runs from the quarterback with Willis out.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

46: A punter throws Miami's longest play of the day

In the 19-9 loss to Detroit, punter Bradley Pinion completed a 46-yard pass to running back Kaytron Allen. Detroit's Jack Fox also completed a pass, and RedZone reported it was the first game in NFL history in which both punters did.

Source: AtoZ Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Dolphins visit the Colts on Sunday at 1 p.m. on CBS

Miami is 1-7 and Indianapolis is 2-7 with five straight losses. The Colts have traded Quenton Nelson, DeForest Buckner, Keenan Allen and Josh Downs in the past two weeks. The Dolphins have run for 100 yards or more in five of their last seven games, and that run game now has to carry a first-time starter.
