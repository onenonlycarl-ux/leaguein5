Miami Dolphins

1
Biggest Story

Dolphins turn to Kyle McCord after Willis lands on IR

Miami put Malik Willis on injured reserve with a groin injury, and he will miss at least four games. McCord, in his second NFL season, starts at Indianapolis for a team that is 1-7.

Source: WPTV ↗

2
Injury Report

Sieler done for year, Brewer out vs Colts

Defensive tackle Zach Sieler tore his biceps against Detroit and is on injured reserve. Center Aaron Brewer (concussion) and receiver Jared Wayne are out, receiver Chris Bell (knee) is doubtful and cornerback Darrell Baker Jr. (knee) is questionable.

Source: CBS Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Caleb Douglas gives Miami a bright spot in the passing game

Douglas caught six of eight targets for 70 yards against Detroit. He has 296 receiving yards through five games.

Source: Football Nation ↗

4
Stat of the Day

0 for 3: Miami's red zone trips in Detroit

The Dolphins came up empty on all three red zone visits in the 19-9 loss. They also took 11 penalties, missed a field goal and rushed for under 100 yards for the second straight game.

Source: WPTV ↗

5
What's Next

At the Colts on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on CBS

Miami plays at Lucas Oil Stadium against a 2-7 Colts team trying to end a five-game home losing streak. The Dolphins will see how McCord handles his first start of the year behind a defense that held Detroit to 270 yards.
