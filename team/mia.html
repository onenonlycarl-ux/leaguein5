Miami Dolphins

1-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Kyle McCord gets first NFL start with Willis on IR

Miami placed Malik Willis on injured reserve with a groin injury, and he will miss at least Weeks 10 through 13. McCord, 24, arrived in an offseason trade with Green Bay and takes over a 1-7 team at Indianapolis. Backup Brady Cook will likely be elevated from the practice squad.

Source: NFL.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Aaron Brewer out with concussion, Jared Wayne out

Center Aaron Brewer (concussion) and receiver Jared Wayne (quad) are out for Sunday. Receiver Chris Bell (knee) is doubtful and cornerback Darrell Baker Jr. (knee) is questionable. Andrew Meyer will likely make his first start of the season at center.

Source: MiamiDolphins.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie Caleb Douglas becomes McCord's top target

Douglas caught six of eight targets for 70 yards in the loss to Detroit. He told reporters he and McCord have built strong chemistry on the scout team, and both say they are ready to compete Sunday.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

4
Stat of the Day

11: penalties for 87 yards cost Miami against Detroit

The Dolphins held the Lions to 270 yards and a 25 percent third-down rate in the 19-9 loss. They also took 11 penalties for 87 yards, which erased much of that defensive work.

Source: Football Nation ↗

5
What's Next

Dolphins visit the 2-7 Colts Sunday at 1 p.m. on CBS

Miami (1-7) plays at Lucas Oil Stadium against an Indianapolis team that has lost five straight. Watch McCord's first start, a patched offensive line and whether the Dolphins clean up the penalties.
