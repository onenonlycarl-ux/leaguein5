Philadelphia Eagles

3-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Joe Milton returns to Dallas as the Eagles' starter

Jalen Hurts is on injured reserve and out for the season, so Milton starts at Dallas today. The Cowboys left him on their practice squad and Philadelphia signed him. The Eagles are 3-7 after beating Pittsburgh 19-16 coming off their bye, ending a seven-game losing streak.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Eagles without Ebiketie and Jonathan Jones in Dallas

Outside linebacker Arnold Ebiketie (oblique) and cornerback Jonathan Jones (ankle) are out. Cornerback Quinyon Mitchell carries no designation after a limited Tuesday. An earlier report had ruled him out, so his status conflicts across sources.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Saquon Barkley has scored in four straight games

Barkley's four-game touchdown streak is a career high. He had a season-high 61 rushing yards before contact against Pittsburgh. He is the main weapon for a Philadelphia offense that managed 214 yards in that win.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

11: sacks for the Eagles over their last two games

Philadelphia also has 15 quarterback hits in that span. Jonathan Greenard is getting healthier and Nolan Smith Jr. is rising. Defensive coordinator Vic Fangio called Dak Prescott the hardest guy in the league to sack.

Source: ESPN ↗

What's Next

Eagles at Cowboys today, 4:30 PM ET on Fox

Philadelphia plays at Dallas on Thursday at 4:30 p.m. ET on Fox. Watch Milton against his old team and the pass rush against Prescott. After that the Eagles visit Arizona in Week 13.
