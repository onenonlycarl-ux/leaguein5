Philadelphia Eagles

2-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Eagles bench Tanner McKee for Joe Milton III

Nick Sirianni told McKee he was out a day after saying he would stay the starter. Milton, who joined the team less than three weeks ago, makes his first career start Sunday against the Giants. Jalen Hurts remains out with blood clots.

Source: NBC Sports Philadelphia ↗

2
Injury Report

Six Eagles out, McKee questionable with ribs

DeVonta Smith (hamstring), Dontayvion Wicks (ankle), Britain Covey (knee), Jonathan Jones (hamstring), Michael Carter (abdomen) and Jihaad Campbell (hamstring/quad) are all out against the Giants. McKee is questionable with a rib injury. Hurts has been on injured reserve since Oct. 17 and is expected to miss the season.

Source: Yahoo Sports (Giants Wire) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jalen Carter keeps wrecking games on a losing team

Carter had five tackles, a sack and a pass breakup in Week 8. Teammate Nolan Smith also recorded a sack, his third in four games. The defense has little else to show during a six-game skid.

Source: Giants.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

0-3: Eagles record in Tanner McKee's starts

McKee completed 63.2 percent of his passes for 638 yards, two touchdowns and two interceptions in losses to Carolina, Dallas and Washington. His passer rating was 76.6. DeVonta Smith, Wicks and Saquon Barkley all missed time in that stretch.

Source: NBC Sports Philadelphia ↗

5
What's Next

Home vs. Giants, Sunday 1:00 p.m. ET on FOX

Philadelphia hosts the division-leading Giants at Lincoln Financial Field with a six-game losing streak. Milton makes his first start. Watch how the offense functions without Smith and Wicks.

Source: Giants.com ↗
