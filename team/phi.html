Philadelphia Eagles

2-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Eagles try to stop a seven-game skid against Pittsburgh

Philadelphia is 2-7 after its bye, with quarterback Joe Milton making a second straight start and the offense ranked at the bottom of the league. The team traded Jordan Davis and cut Hollywood Brown, and talk of draft position is growing. Jalen Hurts has been out for the season since blood clots were found in his lungs.

Injury Report

Smith returns, Baun and Wicks sit out

DeVonta Smith practiced all week and is expected back from his hamstring injury. Linebacker Zack Baun (back) and receiver Dontayvion Wicks (ankle and foot) are out. Running back Will Shipley is questionable.

3
Player Spotlight

Joe Milton ran for 64 yards in his first start

Milton threw for 169 yards and led the team in rushing with 64 yards in his first NFL start. He also took seven sacks. He gets another chance against a Steelers defense that comes off a bad loss.

Stat of the Day

61: years since the Steelers last won in Philadelphia

The Eagles have won 11 straight home games against Pittsburgh. The streak gives a struggling team one thing to hold onto Sunday. Pittsburgh has won six of its first nine games.
