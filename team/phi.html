NFC East · Sunday, November 15 · Week 10

Philadelphia Eagles

2-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Eagles lose a seventh straight, longest skid since 2012

The Giants beat Philadelphia 19-11 at the Linc, dropping the Eagles to 2-7. Rookie Joe Milton III made his first start, threw for 169 yards with no touchdowns and was sacked seven times. Saquon Barkley scored the only touchdown and ran for 22 yards on 13 carries. The Eagles now use their Week 10 bye.

Source: FOX 29 Philadelphia ↗

2
Injury Report

Baun left with a back injury; Smith and Wicks out again

Linebacker Zack Baun left the Giants game in the third quarter with a back injury after recording two sacks. Receivers DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) missed a third straight game. Kicker Jake Elliott played through an ankle injury and hit a 45-yard field goal late.

Source: NBC10 Philadelphia ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jeremiah Trotter Jr. posts 10 tackles and a forced fumble

Trotter started in place of Jihaad Campbell and made 10 tackles, three tackles for loss, a QB hit and a forced fumble against the Giants. The linebacker group was the best part of a defense that held New York to 80 rushing yards. It was the first game this season the Eagles held an opponent under 100 on the ground.

Source: NBC10 Philadelphia ↗

4
Stat of the Day

7: sacks Joe Milton took in his first NFL start

Milton went down seven times for 41 yards against a Giants defense that had nine sacks coming in. He was 18 of 34 for 169 yards and led the team with 64 rushing yards. The offensive line drew an F from NBC10, and rookie right tackle Markel Bell continued to struggle.

Source: NBC10 Philadelphia ↗

5
What's Next

Bye this week, then Steelers on Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS

The Eagles are off in Week 10. They host Pittsburgh at Lincoln Financial Field on Sunday, Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. on CBS. The Steelers are 6-2 and have won four straight, including a 27-6 win over Cleveland. The Eagles then play at Dallas on Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, at 4:30 p.m. ET on FOX.

Source: PhiladelphiaEagles.com ↗
