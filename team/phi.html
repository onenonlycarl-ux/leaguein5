NFC East · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

Philadelphia Eagles

2-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Eagles enter the bye with seven straight losses

Philadelphia is 2-7, and its last win came Sept. 20 at Tennessee. It is the longest skid since an eight-game slide in 2012. Playoff odds sit at 0.2 percent, and Nick Sirianni did not rule out staff changes during the bye.

Source: AP via Lancaster Online ↗

2
Injury Report

Hurts stays on IR as backs and receivers keep falling

Jalen Hurts went on injured reserve Oct. 17 with blood clots in his lungs and is expected to miss the rest of the season. Saquon Barkley has missed time with several injuries, and Dontayvion Wicks missed games with an ankle injury. Tank Bigsby, Will Shipley, Carson Steele and Dameon Pierce have all been lost at running back this year. The team is off this week.

Source: Yahoo Sports (NBC Sports Philadelphia) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jalen Carter has 5 sacks and 4 forced fumbles

The defensive tackle has 5 sacks, 4 forced fumbles, 7 tackles for loss and 25 tackles through nine games. Linebacker Zack Baun leads the team with 65 tackles and has 5 pass breakups. Carter has been solid, though not consistently elite, according to one midterm review.

Source: Yahoo Sports (NBC Sports Philadelphia) ↗

4
Stat of the Day

126-40: Eagles' second-half scoring deficit this season

Opponents have outscored Philadelphia 126-40 after halftime. The Eagles have not topped 341 total yards in any game, and Barkley averages 42.3 rushing yards per game. The offense scores 16.4 points per game, 30th in the NFL.

Source: AP via Lancaster Online ↗

5
What's Next

Bye week, then Steelers on Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. on CBS

Philadelphia is off in Week 10. It returns home Sunday, Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS against Pittsburgh (6-2). Watch the quarterback spot, where Tanner McKee and Joe Milton III have both started since Hurts went down.

Source: PhiladelphiaEagles.com ↗
