NFC East · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Philadelphia Eagles

2-7Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Seven straight losses leave Sirianni weighing staff changes

The Eagles fell 19-11 to the Giants and head into the bye at 2-7, their seventh straight loss. Coach Nick Sirianni did not rule out coaching staff changes and said he has not done a good enough job on offense. That unit ranks 30th in the league at 16.4 points per game in play caller Sean Mannion's first season.

Source: 6abc ↗

2
Injury Report

Baun's back is the one injury note from Sunday

Linebacker Zack Baun hurt his back on the third-quarter play that sent Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy to the locker room, according to the Giants' game story. Philadelphia has not updated his status. PhillyVoice reported no Eagles injuries from the game, and Baun led the defense with two sacks. The bye gives the team time to sort it out.

Source: Giants.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Joe Milton runs the offense but takes a beating

Milton, who has been with the team about three weeks, replaced Tanner McKee at quarterback. He went 18 of 34 for 169 yards while the Giants sacked him seven times. The offense gained 220 yards and scored one touchdown.

Source: Giants.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

16.4: Eagles points per game, 30th in the NFL

Philadelphia's offense also ranks 28th in efficiency, and second-half adjustments have been a repeated problem. The bye gives Sirianni and Mannion two weeks to find a fix before Pittsburgh.

Source: 6abc ↗

5
What's Next

Bye in Week 10, then the Steelers on Nov. 22

The Eagles are off this week. They return Sunday, Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS at Lincoln Financial Field against Pittsburgh, which is 6-2. The NFL flexed the game out of the 4:25 p.m. window.

Source: NBC Sports Philadelphia ↗
