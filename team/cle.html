Cleveland Browns

5-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Saints beat Browns 23-22 in overtime after failed 2-point try

Cleveland dropped to 5-4 after Tyler Shough's New Orleans team scored first in overtime. Harold Fannin Jr. answered with a 39-yard touchdown catch, and the Browns went for two and the win, but Shedeur Sanders was pressured, rolled right and threw incomplete. Andre Szmyt's 51-yard field goal had forced overtime.

Source: Chicago Sun-Times ↗

2
Injury Report

Watson cleared and set to start against Texans

Deshaun Watson cleared the concussion protocol Saturday but was inactive against the Saints, and the Browns plan to start him Sunday against his former team, per ESPN's Jeremy Fowler. Todd Monken praised Sanders but would not discuss the quarterback job beyond that.

Source: TSN ↗

3
Player Spotlight

KC Concepcion scores first NFL touchdown on a 64-yard catch

The rookie receiver caught four passes for 85 yards, including the 64-yard score late in the fourth quarter that put Cleveland ahead. He left in the third quarter with an injury and returned at the start of the fourth.

Source: Browns.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

2: 60-yard completions from Sanders, double Watson's total

Sanders threw two completions of 60 or more yards against New Orleans, twice as many as Watson had in his first eight starts. He finished 19 of 31 for 297 yards and two touchdowns in his first start of the year. Watson entered Week 9 ranked 28th of 30 qualifying quarterbacks in QBR.

Source: TSN ↗

5
What's Next

Browns host Texans on Sunday with Watson facing his old team

Cleveland plays Houston at home on Sunday, Nov. 15, at 1 p.m. ET on Fox. Watch Watson against the team that traded him, and whether the deep shots Sanders hit return to the offense.
