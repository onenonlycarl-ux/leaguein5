AFC North · Saturday, November 14 · Week 10

Cleveland Browns

5-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Browns keep Deshaun Watson as the starter over Shedeur Sanders

Coach Todd Monken named Watson the starter for Sunday after Sanders led a comeback in a 23-22 overtime loss to the Saints. Monken said he planned all along to go back to Watson, who is 5-3 in eight starts. Sanders had not taken first-team reps in about 10 weeks.

Source: Cleveland.com via Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Denzel Boston questionable with toe injury, Hickman out

Rookie receiver Denzel Boston is questionable with a toe injury, though Monken said he looked good in Friday's practice. Safety Ronnie Hickman and fullback Michael Burton are out. Rookie Emmanuel McNeil-Warren will start next to Grant Delpit.

Source: Cleveland.com via Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Denzel Boston leads Browns with 507 receiving yards

Boston has 32 catches for 507 yards and is second on the team with three touchdown catches. He caught a 60-yard pass from Sanders against the Saints. His toe will decide whether he plays Sunday.

Source: Cleveland.com via Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

33: sacks taken by Watson, most in the NFL

Watson has completed 65.5 percent of his passes for 1,806 yards, 7 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. His sack total leads the league. Houston ties Minnesota for the NFL lead with 29.0 sacks, so pressure is the matchup to watch.

Source: Cleveland.com via Yahoo Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Browns host Texans on Sunday afternoon

Cleveland hosts Houston Sunday, Nov. 15 at 1 p.m. ET on FOX. The Texans have won three of four after an 0-4 start. It is Watson's second start against his former team.

Source: NFL.com ↗
