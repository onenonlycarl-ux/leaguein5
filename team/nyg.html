Biggest Story

Giants enter Week 9 as the NFC East's best team

At 4-2-1, New York climbed from 17th to 14th in Fox Sports' power rankings. The writer credits the running game and the coaching staff. The Giants now play a banged-up Eagles team in Philadelphia.

Source: Fox Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Skattebo doubtful, Nabers practices ahead of Eagles

Running back Cam Skattebo (hamstring) missed practice for a third straight day and is doubtful, though John Harbaugh left the door slightly open. Receiver Charlie Jones (hand) is questionable. Malik Nabers said he practiced Friday and no Giant is ruled out.

Source: Yahoo Sports (Giants Wire) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Nabers named NFC Offensive Player of the Month

Nabers caught 36 passes for 509 yards and two touchdowns in four October games. He led the NFL in receiving yards through the month and ran off four straight 100-yard games. He is the only player this season with 500 yards and 35 catches in a four-game span.

Source: Giants.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

2013: the last time the Giants won in Philadelphia

New York last won at the Eagles on October 27, 2013. The teams split their 2025 series, and the Eagles won the most recent meeting 38-20. J.J. McCarthy is chasing a third straight game with 240 passing yards and a 90 rating.

Source: Giants.com ↗

What's Next

At Eagles, Sunday 1:00 p.m. ET on FOX

The Giants visit Lincoln Financial Field with a chance to stretch their division lead. Philadelphia starts third-string quarterback Joe Milton. Watch how New York's run game fares if Skattebo sits.
