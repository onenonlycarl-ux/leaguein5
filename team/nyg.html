NFC East · Saturday, November 14 · Week 10

New York Giants

5-3-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Giants blow a late lead to Washington on Thursday night

New York led 21-17 midway through the fourth quarter before Jayden Daniels drove Washington 71 yards in three plays and hit Stefon Diggs for the winning touchdown with 57 seconds left. J.J. McCarthy threw for a career-high 293 yards, but Rasul Douglas intercepted his last pass. The Giants still lead the NFC East at 5-3-1.

Source: ABC News (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Safety Jason Pinnock leaves with a back injury

Pinnock left early in the third quarter Thursday with a back injury, and the game coverage gave no update on his status. Linebacker Malik Harrison carried a questionable tag for the game with a groin issue. The Giants have 10 days before they host Jacksonville.

Source: ABC News (AP) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Isaiah Likely turns in nine catches for 127 yards

Likely caught nine passes for 127 yards and the 66-yard touchdown that put New York ahead in the fourth quarter. Cam Skattebo added 78 yards and two touchdowns on 18 carries. Neither could keep the Giants from losing in the closing minute.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

399: Giants yards in a loss they outgained

New York outgained Washington 399 to 340 on Thursday but lost the turnover battle with two giveaways. A 33-yard pass interference call on Deonte Banks set up the winning drive. A delay of game on fourth-and-1 and a missed field goal also hurt.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Jaguars visit MetLife Nov. 22 at 4:25 ET

The Giants host Jacksonville on Sunday, Nov. 22. The NFL moved the game from 1 p.m. to 4:25 p.m. ET on CBS. New York needs to tighten its late-game clock management before the Jaguars arrive.

Source: WMTR ↗
