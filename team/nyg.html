NFC East · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

New York Giants

5-3-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Giants lose on Diggs' 32-yard touchdown with 57 seconds left

New York led 21-17 after a 66-yard Isaiah Likely touchdown, then watched Jayden Daniels drive Washington down the field for the winner. J.J. McCarthy threw for 293 yards but an interception with 25 seconds left sealed it. The Giants drop to 5-3-1 and keep a two-game lead in the NFC East.

Source: GMEN HQ ↗

2
Injury Report

McCarthy and Skattebo play through hip and hamstring issues

McCarthy hurt his hip against the Eagles when Zack Baun hit him, and Cam Skattebo missed that game with a hamstring injury. Both were limited early in the week and both played Thursday. Linebacker Malik Harrison (groin) was the only Giant on the final injury report, listed questionable.

Source: Giants.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Isaiah Likely goes for 127 yards on nine catches

Likely caught nine passes for 127 yards and a touchdown against Washington, including the 66-yard score that gave New York the lead. Skattebo ran 18 times for 78 yards and two touchdowns and added 44 receiving yards.

Source: DAZN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

0-4-1: Giants' record against Jayden Daniels

Daniels has beaten the Giants three times and tied them once in four starts. The teams also played to a 27-27 overtime tie in Week 5. The Giants still hold a two-game division lead, but Washington owns the tiebreaker.

Source: ESPN ↗

5
What's Next

Jaguars at MetLife, Sunday Nov. 22 on CBS

The Giants host Jacksonville (6-2) on Sunday, Nov. 22 at 4:25 p.m. ET on CBS. The league moved the game from 1 p.m. Watch how New York bounces back from a loss it led late, and whether the defense holds up against a team with a better record.

Source: Giants.com ↗
