New York Giants

5-2-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Giants beat Eagles 19-11 and lead the NFC East

New York went to Philadelphia and won 19-11 behind a season-high seven sacks and four field goals from rookie kicker Dominic Zvada. The defense held the Eagles to 220 yards and forced five three-and-outs. The Giants sit at 5-2-1 with the division lead and a Thursday night game at home.

Source: Giants.com ↗

2
Injury Report

McCarthy's hip pointer looks manageable for Thursday

J.J. McCarthy left the Eagles game with a hip pointer after a third-quarter hit, then returned to finish 12 of 19 for 127 yards. Coach John Harbaugh said Monday that McCarthy looks like he will play and that he feels fairly good about it. McCarthy said a lot of guys are banged up after the physical game.

Source: AP via Your Valley ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Arvell Reese turns Philadelphia into his playground

The rookie linebacker finished with 12 tackles, three sacks and a forced fumble. His strip-sack of Joe Milton took 2.29 seconds, the fastest sack in the NFL this season by Next Gen Stats. Micah McFadden recovered the fumble and added nine tackles and a sack in place of the injured Tremaine Edmunds.

Source: Giants.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

7: sacks of Joe Milton, a Giants season high

The Giants hit Milton seven times, held the Eagles to 5-of-18 on third down and pinned them inside the 20 five times with Jordan Stout punts. Milton finished 18 of 34 for 169 yards. Saquon Barkley and Elijah Mitchell combined for just 28 rushing yards for Philadelphia.

Source: Giants.com ↗

5
What's Next

Washington visits MetLife tonight on Prime Video

The Giants host the Commanders tonight, Thursday, Nov. 12, at 8:15 p.m. ET on Prime Video at MetLife Stadium. New York is 5-2-1 and Washington is 2-5-1. Watch McCarthy's hip and whether the pass rush keeps up its pace on a short week.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗
