New England Patriots

5-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Packers hang 45 on Patriots in 45-27 blowout at Gillette

Green Bay outgained New England 166-13 in the first quarter and scored 17 points in the third. Jordan Love threw for 372 yards and three touchdowns, and Drake Maye threw three first-half interceptions. The Patriots sit at 5-4, second in the AFC East, and would hold the No. 7 seed.

Source: Boston.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Stevenson, Campbell and Ben Brown all left with knee injuries

Rhamondre Stevenson's MRI results were still pending Monday, and Will Campbell and Ben Brown also hurt knees against Green Bay. Receiver A.J. Brown, who has missed six games, may not make the trip to Germany, and Mike Vrabel said there was no decision yet. The team is considering activating Greg Van Roten from injured reserve.

Source: Boston Globe ↗

3
Player Spotlight

DeMario Douglas posts a career-high 102 receiving yards

Douglas caught five passes for his second 100-yard game. Mack Hollins returned from injured reserve to start and scored on a 22-yard catch, and Corey Kiner ran for his first NFL touchdown while leading the team with 55 rushing yards.

Source: Patriots.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

6: straight games with three touchdowns for Drake Maye

Maye threw three touchdown passes and 299 yards against Green Bay, extending a run that is the second longest in team history behind Tom Brady in 2007. He also passed Jim Plunkett for fifth on the franchise list with 64 career touchdown passes.

Source: Patriots.com ↗

What's Next

Patriots face the Lions in Munich on Sunday at 9:30 a.m. ET on Fox

The Week 10 game is at Allianz Arena, so the team leaves Wednesday, a day early, with a walkthrough Thursday and practice Friday. Watch the secondary, which gave up 335 yards to Green Bay's two top receivers.
