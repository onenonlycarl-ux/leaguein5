New England Patriots

5-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Maye's broken fibula puts DeVito in charge

Drake Maye fractured his fibula in the loss to Detroit in Munich. Coach Mike Vrabel said Tommy DeVito is the starter, and the team has not ruled out adding another quarterback. Only DeVito and rookie Behren Morton are available on the roster.

Source: Boston.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Maye expected out at least six weeks

NFL Media reports Maye will miss at least six weeks. The Patriots kept him on the active roster rather than injured reserve, and coach Mike Vrabel called the situation fluid. Backup Tommy DeVito and rookie Behren Morton are the only quarterbacks available.

Source: Boston.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Elijah Ponder leads the New England pass rush

Ponder has 8 tackles for loss and 7.5 sacks. New England needs that pressure against a Chargers line facing an offense that has been sacked an NFL-high 39 times.

Source: CBS Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

32.3: points the Patriots allow per game over four weeks

New England's defense ranks 30th in EPA per play and has given up 32.3 points a game over its last four. The Patriots also hold a minus-10 turnover differential, tied for 31st.

Source: Patriots.com ↗

What's Next

Patriots at Chargers, Sunday 4:05 p.m. ET

New England plays at SoFi Stadium on CBS. Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert is in the concussion protocol, so DJ Uiagalelei could make his first NFL start. Buffalo visits Foxborough on Dec. 6.
