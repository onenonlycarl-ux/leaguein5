AFC East · Friday, November 20 · Week 11

New England Patriots

5-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Maye out indefinitely as Patriots weigh surgery

An AP source says quarterback Drake Maye has a right ankle injury that will keep him out indefinitely, and the team is still deciding whether he needs surgery. Coach Mike Vrabel does not expect Maye back when the team returns from its bye. New England has lost two straight and three of its last four.

Source: AP via Spectrum News 13 ↗

2
Injury Report

Maye's ankle reportedly fractured, team has not confirmed

NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported Maye fractured his ankle on a first-quarter sack by Aidan Hutchinson in Munich. Vrabel called it a low leg injury and has not confirmed a diagnosis. Maye stayed in Germany for testing and will have more exams back in the U.S. Backup Tommy DeVito and practice squad rookie Behren Morton are the only other quarterbacks on the roster.

Source: NBC Sports Boston ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Corey Kiner leads a backfield that needs a boost

Kiner carried 10 times against Detroit and averages 4.5 yards on 60 carries this year. Rhamondre Stevenson is at 3.7 yards a carry, and TreVeyon Henderson is sidelined after six games. With DeVito likely under center, New England needs Kiner and Stevenson to carry more of the offense.

Source: ClutchPoints ↗

4
Stat of the Day

151: Patriots total yards in Munich, a season low

New England gained 151 yards in the 35-10 loss to the Lions, its lowest total of the season. DeVito went 12 of 24 for 81 yards with a late touchdown to Lan Larison and an interception. The run game added only 61 yards, and DeVito had 19 of them.

Source: AP via Spectrum News 13 ↗

5
What's Next

Bye this week, then the Chargers Nov. 29 at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS

The Patriots are off Sunday and return against the 1-8 Chargers on Nov. 29. The league moved that game out of Sunday night to the 4:05 p.m. ET window on CBS. Watch the quarterback decision: Vrabel has said he is comfortable with DeVito.

Source: Boston.com ↗
