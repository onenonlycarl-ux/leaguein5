AFC East · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

New England Patriots

5-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Patriots land in Munich after a 45-27 loss to Green Bay

New England fell to 5-4 after allowing 45 points, the most of Mike Vrabel's tenure. Drake Maye threw three interceptions and now leads the NFL with 11. The Patriots face the 6-2 Lions in Munich on Sunday, and a loss would make them 5-5.

Source: Patriots.com ↗

2
Injury Report

A.J. Brown practices again, Stevenson works off to the side

Brown, who missed two games with an ankle and knee issue, took part in his second straight practice in Munich. Running back Rhamondre Stevenson (knee) did not join stretching but worked on his own. Center Ben Brown (knee) is out, cornerback Carlton Davis III stayed home, and guard Walter Rouse is in the concussion protocol. The Friday report will say more.

Source: Patriots.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

DeMario Douglas catches 5 for 102 against Green Bay

Douglas led the offense in a rough game with five catches and 102 yards. He played 42 snaps. With Brown back at practice, he said it feels good to have that leader back.

Source: Patriots.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

-4: Patriots lose the turnover battle by four

New England forced no turnovers while Green Bay took the ball away four times. Cornerback Christian Gonzalez gave up 10 catches for 187 yards and a touchdown in coverage, a career high. The Patriots entered the week 11th in DVOA.

Source: Patriots.com ↗

5
What's Next

Patriots face the Lions Sunday at 9:30 a.m. on FOX

The game is at FC Bayern's stadium in Munich, the last of the NFL's European games this year. Detroit averages 27.8 points per game. After Sunday the Patriots hit their bye, then meet Buffalo, Minnesota, Kansas City and Denver. Watch the makeshift offensive line against the Lions' pass rush.

Source: Patriots.com ↗
