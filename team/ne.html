AFC East · Saturday, November 28 · Week 12

New England Patriots

5-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Patriots go west with DeVito as Chargers lose Herbert

Tommy DeVito will start with Drake Maye out with a fractured right fibula. The Chargers are 1-9 and Justin Herbert is in concussion protocol, so DJ Uiagalelei is likely to make his first start since 2024 at Florida State. New England has allowed 80 points over its last two games.

Source: The Boston Globe ↗

2
Injury Report

Brown, Davis, Doubs out; Barmore questionable

The Patriots ruled out Maye, receiver A.J. Brown (hamstring), cornerback Carlton Davis (knee) and receiver Romeo Doubs. Defensive lineman Christian Barmore (knee) and guard Alijah Vera-Tucker (hamstring) are questionable. Coach Mike Vrabel is still deciding between Ben Brown and Matt Pryor at right guard.

Source: Boston.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

DeMario Douglas becomes the top target with Brown out

Douglas leads the Patriots with 49 targets, 32 catches, 438 yards and four touchdowns. Brown has caught 8 of 22 targets for 102 yards since coming back from his ankle injury. Douglas will be DeVito's main option on Sunday.

Source: The Boston Globe ↗

4
Stat of the Day

16.8: points per game for the Chargers, 30th in the NFL

Los Angeles also ranks 26th in total offense at 288.1 yards per game. Its one win came 23-20 over Baltimore in Week 10. Omarion Hampton has run for 194 yards on 36 carries over the last two games.

Source: The Boston Globe ↗

5
What's Next

Patriots at Chargers, Sunday at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS

New England plays in Inglewood against a one-win team, then hosts Buffalo on Dec. 6 in a game with AFC East stakes. Watch how DeVito handles the pass rush without Brown on the outside.

Source: The Boston Globe ↗
