Biggest Story

Patriots rally from 10 down to beat Miami 24-19

New England trailed 13-3 at halftime and scored all three of its touchdowns after the break. The win moved the Patriots to 5-3 and gave them four wins in their last five games.

Source: ESPN ↗

2
Injury Report

Henry questionable, Brown and Henderson out

Tight end Hunter Henry (foot) is questionable after leaving the Miami game. Receiver A.J. Brown and running back TreVeyon Henderson are listed out. Receiver Mack Hollins (calf) is eligible to return from injured reserve.

Source: Patriots.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Drake Maye accounts for three touchdowns again

Maye went 16 of 20 for 199 yards with two touchdown passes and a rushing score against Miami. All three came in the second half. He has 17 touchdowns and two interceptions over his last five games.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

15: Patriots giveaways, third-most in the NFL

New England has turned it over 15 times this season. Maye had seven turnovers in his first three games and only three in the five since. Green Bay's defense has 15 takeaways, second in the league.

Source: NBC Sports Boston ↗

What's Next

Packers visit Gillette on Sunday at 4:25 p.m. on Fox

New England hosts Green Bay (5-3) on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 4:25 p.m. ET on Fox. It is the first meeting since 2022. Watch Christian Gonzalez against Packers deep threat Christian Watson.
