Kansas City Chiefs

1
Biggest Story

Chiefs and Bills meet with AFC seeding on the line

Kansas City (8-2) plays at Buffalo (9-1) tonight, and the Bills lead the Chiefs by one game in the standings. The result could shape home-field advantage in the AFC playoffs. Josh Allen is 5-1 against Kansas City in the regular season and 0-4 in the postseason.

Source: KMBC ↗

2
Injury Report

Walker is out, Mahomes and Rice are cleared

Running back Kenneth Walker III will miss tonight's game with a groin injury. Patrick Mahomes (knee, right elbow) and receiver Rashee Rice (knee) came off the injury report Wednesday and are set to play. Tight end Noah Gray is questionable.

Source: Footballguys ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Emmett Johnson keeps starting in Walker's place

Rookie Emmett Johnson, a fifth-round pick from Nebraska, has opened back-to-back games with at least 22 carries and 101 rushing yards. He has 314 scrimmage yards while filling in. Offensive coordinator Eric Bieniemy said Walker remains the starter when healthy, but Johnson gets the job again tonight.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

13: Mahomes' touchdown passes in November, with no picks

Patrick Mahomes has thrown 13 touchdowns this month without an interception and has four picks all season. The Chiefs pass rush has 11 sacks over the last three games. Buffalo's Allen has nine interceptions, so the turnover margin is worth watching.

Source: KMBC ↗

What's Next

At Buffalo tonight, 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC

Kansas City plays the Bills on Thursday night at Highmark Stadium, kickoff 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. Buffalo runs the ball better than anyone, second in the league at 146.9 yards per game with 21 rushing touchdowns. The Chiefs own the league's top red zone defense, allowing touchdowns on 36 percent of trips.
