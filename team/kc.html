AFC West · Saturday, November 21 · Week 11

Kansas City Chiefs

7-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Mahomes throws five touchdowns as Chiefs beat Falcons 35-13

Kansas City moved to 7-2 and tied Denver for first in the AFC West. Patrick Mahomes went 22 of 35 for 348 yards and five scores. Josh Downs, acquired from Indianapolis earlier in the week, caught an 18-yard touchdown in his first game as a Chief.

Source: KCTV5 ↗

2
Injury Report

Trey Smith, Kenneth Walker and Jaylon Moore out vs. Cardinals

Guard Trey Smith (concussion), running back Kenneth Walker (groin) and tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) did not practice Friday and are out. Defensive linemen Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) and Peter Woods (neck) are questionable after limited work. Travis Kelce and Mahomes practiced fully.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie Emmett Johnson piles up 171 yards in first start

Johnson started in place of the injured Kenneth Walker and ran 22 times for 101 yards. He added six catches for 70 yards and two touchdowns, and forced 10 missed tackles. He now has 519 scrimmage yards, second among rookies.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

5: games with 30-plus points in nine tries this season

Kansas City has scored 30 or more points five times through nine games, after doing it three times in all of last season. The Chiefs rank second in total offense and third in scoring defense, and only Chicago and Jacksonville also sit in the top 10 in all four major team categories.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

What's Next

Cardinals visit Arrowhead Sunday at 1 PM ET on CBS

The 3-7 Cardinals come to Arrowhead Stadium Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on CBS, also streaming on Paramount+. Kansas City is trying to hold a share of the division lead with Denver. Watch how the Chiefs' offensive line holds up without Trey Smith and Jaylon Moore.
