AFC West · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Kansas City Chiefs

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Chiefs rout Jets 35-12 to end a two-game skid

Kansas City scored a season-high 35 points and shook off a slow start. Kenneth Walker III scored three touchdowns, two on Patrick Mahomes passes, as the Chiefs improved to 6-2. Denver beat them 28-24 on Nov. 1.

Source: KCTV5 (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Mahomes day to day with elbow; Worthy, Moore also hurting

Andy Reid said Mahomes is day to day after David Bailey sacked him and hit the throwing elbow late in the first half. Reid called the news more positive than negative and described it as a soreness issue. Receiver Xavier Worthy (hamstring) and right tackle Jaylen Moore (ankle) are also day to day.

Source: KSHB ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Walker leads the league with 11 touchdowns

Kenneth Walker III ran for 102 yards and a score and finished with 143 yards from scrimmage against New York. The three-touchdown game was his third of his career. He now leads the NFL with 11 total touchdowns through eight games.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

5: sacks by the Chiefs defense against New York

Chris Jones and George Karlaftis each had multiple sacks, and Karlaftis added three tackles for loss. The Jets gained just 46 yards in the second half. On offense, Kansas City went 4-for-4 in the red zone.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

5
What's Next

At Atlanta on Sunday, 1 p.m. ET on CBS

The Chiefs visit the 3-6 Falcons at Mercedes-Benz Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 15. The big question is Mahomes' elbow, and how many of Worthy and Moore are ready.
