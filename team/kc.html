Kansas City Chiefs

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Chiefs snap two-game skid with 35-12 win over Jets

Kansas City ended a two-game losing streak by beating the Jets 35-12 at Arrowhead. Patrick Mahomes threw for 242 yards and two touchdowns after a late first-half scare, and the defense piled up a season-high five sacks. The win moved the Chiefs to 6-2, half a game behind Denver.

Source: KSHB 41 ↗

2
Injury Report

Mahomes practices fully after elbow scare

Mahomes hurt his throwing elbow against the Jets, and an MRI showed no major damage. Friday's report lists him as a full participant with a knee and right elbow designation and no game status. Offensive tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) is out, and running back Kenneth Walker (groin) is questionable. Wide receiver Xavier Worthy, who left the Jets game with a hamstring injury, practiced fully.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Kenneth Walker scores three times against the Jets

Walker ran 18 times for 102 yards and a touchdown, then caught four passes for 41 yards and two more scores. He finished with 143 scrimmage yards and 1,044 for the season, the first Chief over 1,000 since Isiah Pacheco in 2023. It was his fifth multi-touchdown game of the year, the most in the NFL.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

9,360: Mahomes and Kelce set the QB-to-TE yards record

Mahomes and Travis Kelce now hold the NFL record for passing yards from a quarterback to a tight end, at 9,360. Kelce led the Chiefs with 72 receiving yards against the Jets and added a one-yard rushing touchdown. Fifty-six of those yards came after the catch.

Source: KSHB 41 ↗

5
What's Next

Chiefs visit the 3-6 Falcons on Sunday

Kansas City plays at Atlanta on Sunday, Nov. 15, at 1 p.m. ET on CBS. Mahomes said after practice this week that he has no doubts about playing. Watch the pass rush, which hit Geno Smith on half his dropbacks last week, against a Falcons team that is 3-6.
