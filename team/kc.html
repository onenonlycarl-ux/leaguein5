AFC West · Wednesday, November 25 · Week 12

Kansas City Chiefs

8-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Mahomes is playing through elbow and knee issues

Patrick Mahomes has thrown for 672 yards and eight touchdowns over his last two games despite elbow and knee problems. He said last week he has not had the burst he wants. The Chiefs (8-2) head to Buffalo on Thanksgiving night.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Walker limited Tuesday, Moore unlikely to return

Running back Kenneth Walker III practiced on a limited basis Tuesday after missing time with groin soreness. Andy Reid said Walker feels good but gave no commitment on his status. Tackle Jaylon Moore has missed two weeks with an ankle injury and looks unlikely to play Thursday.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie Emmett Johnson keeps producing in Walker's place

Johnson, a fifth-round pick from Nebraska, has 45 carries for 206 yards and a touchdown in his two starts. He also has 10 catches for 108 yards and two scores. He is the first player since 1994 with 100 rushing yards and 30 receiving yards in each of his first two starts.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

672: Mahomes passing yards over the last two games

Mahomes has thrown eight touchdowns in that stretch, in wins over Atlanta and Arizona. Against the Cardinals he went 27 of 39 for 324 yards and three touchdowns with no interceptions. It was his first back-to-back game this season with three or more touchdowns and no picks.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Chiefs at Bills on Thanksgiving night

Kansas City (8-2) plays at Buffalo (9-1) on Thursday, Nov. 26, at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC, with a stream on Peacock. Mahomes is 3-2 in road games this season. Watch how the Chiefs run the ball against the Bills, who have the fourth-best run defense by yards allowed.

Source: NBC Sports ↗
