AFC West · Thursday, November 19 · Week 11

Kansas City Chiefs

7-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Chiefs look for a fifth home win against a reeling Cardinals team

Kansas City hosts Arizona at 7-2 and trails Denver only on tiebreakers. The Cardinals are 3-7, start rookie Carson Beck again after Gardner Minshew's season-ending elbow injury, and have been outscored 51-9 over two games. Andy Reid said the Chiefs are still annoyed about missing the playoffs last year.

Source: KC's The Ticket ↗

2
Injury Report

Smith, Moore and Walker sit out Wednesday's practice

Right guard Trey Smith (concussion), right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) and running back Kenneth Walker III (groin) did not practice. Nick Bolton (shin) and Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) were limited. Rookie lineman Peter Woods and running back Jaydn Ott returned to full work, and final game designations come Friday.

Source: A to Z Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Mahomes plays through an elbow issue and throws for 348

Patrick Mahomes threw for 348 yards and five touchdowns against Atlanta, his first five-touchdown game since 2022. He did it while carrying an elbow injury from the week before. Reid said Mahomes is staying in the pocket when he should and moving when he needs to.

Source: CBS Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

17.3: points per game the Chiefs defense allows, third in the NFL

Kansas City also ranks eighth in total defense at 314.8 yards per game. The offense has scored 30 or more points five times this season. That mix has made the Chiefs a serious threat in the AFC.

Source: KC's The Ticket ↗

5
What's Next

Cardinals visit Arrowhead on Sunday at 1 PM ET on CBS

Arizona has not gotten a touchdown pass from its starting quarterback in three straight games. Watch the Chiefs offensive line without Smith and Moore if they do not clear. A short week follows, with a Thanksgiving night game at Buffalo.
