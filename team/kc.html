Kansas City Chiefs

8-3Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Tyler Bass kicks Bills past Chiefs as time expires

Buffalo won on a 57-yard Bass field goal at the gun, 13-10, in a rainy Thanksgiving night game. Patrick Mahomes threw for 174 yards and no touchdowns. A tipped punt gave the Bills a short field, and Kansas City managed five three-and-outs.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Kenneth Walker out a third straight game with groin strain

ESPN reports Walker has a grade-two groin strain from the Nov. 8 game against the Jets, and the Chiefs held him out on the short week. Guard Trey Smith is expected back, while tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) was out.

Source: ESPN ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Emmett Johnson carries the load again in Walker's place

The rookie started and ran 17 times for 72 yards and a second-quarter touchdown against Buffalo. Travis Kelce added four catches for 71 yards and passed 1,300 career receptions.

Source: ClutchPoints via Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

8 of 16: Butker's field goals from 40 yards or longer

Harrison Butker missed a 46-yarder wide right with about six minutes left, with the Chiefs trailing. He now has eight misses this season and a field goal rate just over 65 percent.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Chiefs at Rams on Thursday night, Dec. 3

Kansas City plays at SoFi Stadium on Prime Video, 8:15 p.m. ET, on another short week. Andy Reid said after the loss he will make no decision on Butker yet, so watch the kicker spot.

Source: CBS Sports ↗
