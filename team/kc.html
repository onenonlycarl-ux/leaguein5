Kansas City Chiefs

6-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Chiefs trade for Colts receiver Josh Downs

Kansas City sent 2027 fourth- and fifth-round picks to Indianapolis for Downs and a 2027 seventh-round pick. The deal came at Tuesday's trade deadline and deepens a receiver room that already includes Rashee Rice and Xavier Worthy.

Source: NFL.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Moore and Woods missed practice again before Atlanta

Right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) and defensive tackle Peter Woods (neck) did not practice Wednesday or Thursday. Running back Jaydn Ott (hamstring) was limited Thursday. Patrick Mahomes, Travis Kelce, Rashee Rice and Xavier Worthy all practiced fully.

Source: A to Z Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Kenneth Walker III reaches 1,000 scrimmage yards

Walker scored three touchdowns and gained 143 scrimmage yards in the 35-12 win over the Jets. He has 1,044 scrimmage yards and 11 scrimmage touchdowns, tied with Jahmyr Gibbs for the NFL lead. He is the first Chiefs player past 1,000 yards since Isiah Pacheco in 2023.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

63%: Harrison Butker's field goal rate trails the league

Butker has missed a kick in every game but Week 1, and that rate is the worst among kickers on a roster. Andy Reid will keep him for Atlanta even though Kansas City signed Justin Tucker to the practice squad.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Chiefs visit the Falcons in Week 10

Kansas City plays at Atlanta on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS at Mercedes-Benz Stadium. The Falcons are 3-6. Watch the offensive line if Moore stays out and the interior defensive line, which is down to three healthy tackles.

Source: SI ↗
