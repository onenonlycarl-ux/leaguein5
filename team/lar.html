NFC West · Thursday, November 12 · Week 10

Los Angeles Rams

5-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Rams beat Washington 20-13 for a third straight win

Los Angeles improved to 5-4 on the road. Puka Nacua caught 14 passes for 150 yards and a touchdown, Matthew Stafford hit him for the go-ahead score early in the fourth quarter, and Byron Young recovered a late fumble to seal it.

Source: TheRams.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Nacua to IR with knee ligament sprains and turf toe

An MRI showed a sprained MCL and PCL in Nacua's right knee plus turf toe. He is expected to miss at least a month. Davante Adams is out with a hamstring injury, guard Steve Avila is out significantly with a hamstring, and cornerback Trent McDuffie missed his third game of the season.

Source: KIRO 7 (AP) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Garrett posts 3 sacks in return from knee surgery

Myles Garrett came back after seven missed games and finished with three sacks, a forced fumble, three quarterback hits and three tackles for loss against Washington. His strip sack in the second quarter gave the Rams the ball back.

Source: TheRams.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

0-for-9: Rams on third down before the late third quarter

Los Angeles failed on its first nine third-down tries against Washington and converted for the first time with 2:48 left in the third quarter. The offense still scored 20 points behind Nacua's 19 targets.

Source: TheRams.com ↗

5
What's Next

At Arizona Sunday, with Nacua and Adams out

The Rams visit the Cardinals on Sunday, Nov. 15, at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS. Los Angeles must find receiver production without Nacua and Adams, who combined for 100 of the team's 120 wideout catches this season.

Source: DIRECTV ↗
