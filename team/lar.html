1
Biggest Story

Rams crush Cardinals 34-6 for a fourth straight win

Los Angeles trailed 6-3 late in the second quarter, then scored two touchdowns in the last 1:06 of the half and pulled away. The 6-4 Rams head into the bye week with their winning streak intact, even without Puka Nacua and Davante Adams.

Source: ABC15 Arizona ↗

2
Injury Report

Nacua out a good while with MCL and PCL strains

Coach Sean McVay said Nacua has MCL and PCL strains plus turf toe and will probably go on injured reserve. The bye week does not count toward the four-game minimum. Davante Adams (hamstring) and guard Steve Avila (hamstring) are also hurt.

Source: NBC Los Angeles ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Stafford spreads it around for three touchdowns

Matthew Stafford went 18 of 26 for 249 yards and three touchdowns against Arizona. The scores went to Xavier Smith, Tyler Higbee and Davis Allen, and Blake Corum added a rushing touchdown.

Source: Rams.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

9: different Rams caught a pass against Arizona

No Rams receiver had more than three catches in the win. With Nacua, Adams and tight end Terrance Ferguson out, Stafford leaned on Tutu Atwell, CJ Daniels and Konata Mumpfield, among others.

Source: Rams.com ↗

5
What's Next

Bye in Week 11, then Packers on Thanksgiving Eve

The Rams host Green Bay at SoFi Stadium on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 8 p.m. ET on Netflix. Both teams are 6-4, and the Rams will have to show they can keep the offense humming without their top two receivers.

Source: Rams.com ↗
