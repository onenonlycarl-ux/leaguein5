Los Angeles Rams

6-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Puka Nacua out after MCL surgery and a three-game suspension

Nacua had surgery on an MCL injury last week, and Sean McVay said he will miss a couple of months. The NFL also suspended him three games for a personal conduct policy violation, and he is eligible for reinstatement Dec. 14. The Rams are 6-4 on a four-game win streak.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Avila out, Curl in doubt, Kyren Williams limited

In the first injury report of Week 12, guard Steve Avila (hamstring) did not participate and will not play, and safety Kam Curl (toe) is in doubt. Center Coleman Shelton (hamstring) is out for several weeks. Running back Kyren Williams (toe) was limited, and Kobie Turner (groin) was a full participant and is expected to play.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Blake Corum runs for 100 yards without the top two receivers

Corum rushed for 100 yards and scored from a yard out in the 34-6 win over Arizona. Matthew Stafford threw for 249 yards and three touchdowns, to Tyler Higbee, Xavier Smith and Davis Allen. Nacua and Davante Adams were both out.

Source: AZFamily ↗

4
Stat of the Day

1:06: all it took the Rams to flip a 6-3 deficit into 17-6

Los Angeles trailed Arizona 6-3 late in the second quarter, then scored twice in the final 1:06 of the half to lead 17-6 at the break. The Rams finished with a 34-6 win, their fourth in a row. Arizona managed 123 total yards.

Source: AZFamily ↗

5
What's Next

Rams host Packers on Thanksgiving Eve, 8 PM ET on Netflix

Los Angeles hosts Green Bay on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at SoFi Stadium, with an 8 PM ET kickoff on Netflix. Both teams are 6-4. Packers pass rusher Micah Parsons has been medically cleared to make his season debut, so the Rams' offensive line faces a test.

Source: NFL.com ↗
