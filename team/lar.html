Los Angeles Rams

7-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Rams beat Packers 26-13 and move to 7-4

Los Angeles ran for more than 240 yards on Wednesday night at SoFi Stadium and pushed Green Bay toward the edge of the playoff race. Kyren Williams scored in the second quarter, and the defense forced a fumble and two interceptions. The win gives them a one-game lead in the standings, and the head-to-head tiebreaker makes it effectively two over Green Bay.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

2
Injury Report

Line depth thins; Nacua reported out for the season

Guard Steve Avila (hamstring) and tight end Terrance Ferguson (ankle, staying on injured reserve) were ruled out for the Packers game. Center Coleman Shelton (hamstring) is expected to miss four to six weeks. Defensive end Kobie Turner came off injured reserve and lineman Justin Dedich returned to practice. NFL.com reports receiver Puka Nacua had knee surgery and is likely done for the year, though earlier coverage said he stayed off injured reserve.

Source: Los Angeles Rams ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Matthew Stafford finds a rhythm as targets thin out

Through 10 games Stafford has 2,623 passing yards, 19 touchdowns and 10 interceptions, per NFL.com's quarterback index. The index ranks him 14th and says he needs to stay as clean as he was against Arizona in Week 10 with the receiver injuries. He hit Konata Mumpfield for 20 yards on third-and-8 during the Packers game's key second-half touchdown drive.

Source: NFL.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

184: Blake Corum's career-high rushing yards

Corum carried 32 times for 184 yards and a touchdown against Green Bay. It was the most rushing yards by a Rams player since Todd Gurley ran for 208 in 2018. The team piled up a season-high rushing total.

Source: ESPN ↗

5
What's Next

Chiefs come to SoFi Thursday night, Dec. 3

The Rams host Kansas City on Thursday, Dec. 3 at 8:15 p.m. ET on Amazon Prime Video, per the original schedule, which the NFL could still flex. Los Angeles then visits the 49ers in Week 14. Watch whether the ground game holds up against a Chiefs defense that will know it is coming.
