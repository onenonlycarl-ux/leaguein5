Biggest Story

Titans trade safety Hooker to Buffalo for a 2027 second

Tennessee sent Amani Hooker and a 2027 fifth-round pick to the Bills for a 2027 second-round pick. Hooker had five interceptions this season and 17 for his career. The Titans now hold two first-round and two second-round picks in 2027.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

Injury Report

Five Titans out, Spears questionable vs Jaguars

Receiver Carnell Tate (shoulder), guard Peter Skoronski (hamstring), linebackers Anthony Hill Jr. (neck) and Cody Barton (concussion), and punter Tommy Townsend are out. Running back Tyjae Spears (knee) practiced fully and is questionable. Cedric Gray is expected to play the full game at linebacker.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Pollard rides back to back 100 yard games

Tony Pollard has 595 rushing yards and six touchdowns this season. He ran for 117 yards and two scores in the overtime loss at Cincinnati, his second straight 100 yard game. He has five touchdowns over the last two games.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

Stat of the Day

86-3: Titans second quarter deficit through eight games

Opponents have outscored Tennessee 86-3 in the second quarter. The Titans have outscored teams 47-27 in the first quarter and 70-47 in the fourth. They have scored 65.3 percent of their points in the second half or overtime.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

What's Next

Home against Jacksonville, Sunday 1:00 PM ET on FOX

The Titans host the Jaguars today at 1:00 PM ET on FOX, coming off their bye. Tennessee has no home win this season, and its only win came at Indianapolis on Oct. 18. The rematch is Nov. 29 in Jacksonville.
