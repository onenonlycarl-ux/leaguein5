1
Biggest Story

Cowboys score on first six drives in 51-30 rout of Titans

Dallas scored a touchdown on each of its first six possessions and Dak Prescott threw for 325 yards and four touchdowns. Tennessee dropped to 1-9 and clinched its fifth straight losing season, its longest such run since the franchise left Houston.

Source: ESPN ↗

2
Injury Report

Titans lose Flott, Moore and more in a thin Dallas game

Safety Tony Adams barely played because of illness, cornerback Cor'Dale Flott left with a neck injury and left tackle Dan Moore Jr. left with a knee injury. Safety Terrell Burgess also hurt a knee. Guards Peter Skoronski and Fernando Carmona Jr. did not play.

Source: ESPN ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Cam Ward goes 21 of 30 for 248 yards and two scores

Ward hit Carnell Tate on a career-long 59-yard touchdown and added a rushing score. He lost a fumble on a fourth-quarter strip sack and took five sacks behind a banged-up line. Tennessee has scored at least 23 points in five straight games.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

4
Stat of the Day

3: straight games the Titans allowed 40 or more points

Tennessee gave up 51 to Dallas after allowing 40 to Jacksonville the week before. The Sports Illustrated team site says it is the second time in franchise history and the first since 2004.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

What's Next

Titans visit the Jaguars on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on Fox

Tennessee travels to Jacksonville on Nov. 29. The Titans need answers on the offensive line and in the secondary after a week of injuries, and they get a Jaguars team that wants to bounce back from its own loss.
