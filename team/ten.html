Tennessee Titans

1-8Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Titans lean into Cam Ward's audition at 1-8

Tennessee is 1-8 after a 40-23 loss to Jacksonville and has lost three straight. Coach Robert Saleh said the team must give Ward a real chance to prove he is the franchise quarterback. A loss to Dallas followed by a trip to Jacksonville would clinch a fifth straight losing season.

Source: AP via ABC News ↗

2
Injury Report

Carnell Tate and Anthony Hill Jr. questionable at Dallas

Guard Fernando Carmona Jr. (ankle) is out. Wide receiver Carnell Tate (shoulder), linebacker Anthony Hill Jr. (neck), safety Tony Adams (concussion) and cornerback Micah Robinson (hip) are questionable, though all practiced fully on Friday. One report listed running back Tyjae Spears on injured reserve while the team report had him questionable, so his status is unclear.

Source: Tennessee Titans ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Cam Ward: 28 of 39, 231 yards, 2 TDs against Jacksonville

Ward also led the Titans with 39 rushing yards in last week's loss. Over his last two games he has 535 passing yards, four touchdowns and one interception, plus 80 rushing yards. His record as a starter is 4-22.

Source: Tennessee Titans ↗

4
Stat of the Day

459: yards allowed by Titans, third game in a row at that mark

Jacksonville gained 459 yards and converted 7 of 14 third downs against Tennessee. It was the third straight game the Titans allowed at least 459 yards. Tennessee is 20-57 since 2022.

Source: AP via ABC News ↗

5
What's Next

Titans at Cowboys today, 1 p.m. ET on FOX

Tennessee (1-8) plays at AT&T Stadium against Dallas (4-6), kickoff at noon Central on FOX. It is the Titans' first trip to Dallas since 2018. Dak Prescott has 2,724 passing yards and 21 touchdowns, and Dallas ranks third in the NFL in passing yards per game. The game had not started in the sources checked.

Source: Tennessee Titans ↗
