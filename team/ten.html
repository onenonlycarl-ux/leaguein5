Biggest Story

Titans send Hooker to Buffalo for a second-round pick

Safety Amani Hooker went to the Bills with five interceptions, tied for the league lead. Tennessee had already traded Jeffery Simmons to Chicago, and the Hooker deal continues the rebuild by adding more draft capital.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Tate and Skoronski still out of practice

Rookie receiver Carnell Tate (shoulder), guard Peter Skoronski (hamstring), linebackers Cody Barton (concussion) and Anthony Hill Jr. (neck) did not practice Wednesday or Thursday. Running back Tyjae Spears (knee) moved up to limited on Thursday.

Source: Tennessee Titans ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Cam Ward posts a first 300-yard game

In the overtime loss at Cincinnati, Ward threw for a career-high 304 yards and two touchdowns on 26 of 40 passing. He also ran for a career-high 41 yards.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

25:37: Tennessee's time of possession, last in the NFL

The Titans hold the ball for 25 minutes, 37 seconds per game, the lowest average in the league. They score 18.0 points per game, 28th, against Jacksonville's 28.6.

Source: Football Nation ↗
