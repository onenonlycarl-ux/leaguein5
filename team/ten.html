Biggest Story

Cam Ward makes his case as the Titans' long-term quarterback

Ward threw for at least 230 yards and two touchdowns in three straight games, a first for a Titans passer since Marcus Mariota in 2016. Coach Robert Saleh said Ward is showing he can lead the franchise. Tennessee lost 51-30 at Dallas on Sunday.

Injury Report

Titans line thin: Skoronski on IR, Moore hurt knee

Left guard Peter Skoronski is on injured reserve and rookie right guard Fernando Carmona likely stays out. Left tackle Dan Moore Jr. missed Wednesday practice with a knee injury, and James Hudson III would start if Moore cannot go.

Player Spotlight

Carnell Tate scores on a 59-yard catch and run in Dallas

Tate turned a short catch into a 59-yard touchdown, running 51 yards after the catch. He returns this week after missing the first Jacksonville game. Ward calls on him as a deep threat.

Stat of the Day

109-16: Tennessee's second-quarter scoring margin against it

Opponents have outscored the Titans 109-16 in the second quarter this season. Tennessee has the worst first-half scoring margin in the NFL at minus 87.
