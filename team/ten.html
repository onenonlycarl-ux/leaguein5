1
Biggest Story

Cam Ward posts first 300-yard game in overtime loss to Cincinnati

Ward went 26 of 40 for 304 yards and two touchdowns in the 43-36 overtime loss on Nov. 1. He also ran for 41 yards and threw a two-point conversion. The Titans, coming off a Week 9 bye, are 1-7 and host Jacksonville.

Injury Report

Skoronski, Tate and two linebackers out against Jacksonville

Tennessee ruled out guard Peter Skoronski (hamstring), receiver Carnell Tate (shoulder), linebackers Cody Barton and Anthony Hill Jr., and punter Tommy Townsend. Running back Tyjae Spears (knee) is questionable after a full Friday practice.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Tony Pollard runs for 117 yards and two scores

Pollard carried 21 times for 117 yards and two touchdowns against the Bengals, his second straight 100-yard game. He added 20 receiving yards. He also scored on a 40-yard run in the first quarter.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

36: points scored at Cincinnati, a Titans high since 2021

Tennessee scored 36 points against the Bengals, its most since October 2021. Safety Amani Hooker intercepted Joe Burrow three times, and Jacob Martin had three sacks. The Titans still lost after Burrow tied it with 1:17 left and won in overtime.

Source: TennesseeTitans.com ↗

What's Next

Titans host Jaguars Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on FOX

Tennessee hosts Jacksonville at Nissan Stadium on Nov. 15 in Music City uniforms. The Jaguars are 6-2 and double-digit favorites. The rematch comes Nov. 29 at EverBank Stadium.
