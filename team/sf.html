NFC West · Saturday, November 7 · Week 9

San Francisco 49ers

6-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bosa and McCaffrey cleared to face the Raiders

Nick Bosa returns after missing five games with a calf injury. He went from limited work Wednesday and Thursday to a full practice Friday, and Kyle Shanahan said he is cleared to go. Christian McCaffrey, who strained a hamstring in Week 7, also practiced fully Friday with no game designation.

Source: 49ers.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Evans, Samuel and Kirk out, leaving San Francisco thin at receiver

Mike Evans (Achilles), Deebo Samuel (hamstring) and Christian Kirk (knee) are all out, which takes three of the six receivers on the 53-man roster. Demarcus Robinson is questionable and likely to be activated from injured reserve after five missed games. KhaDarel Hodge could be elevated from the practice squad.

Source: NBC Sports Bay Area ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Purdy aims to clean up after a two-interception loss

Brock Purdy went 27 of 40 for 288 yards, one touchdown and two interceptions in the Week 7 loss at Atlanta. The second pick, thrown on third-and-9 with under three minutes left, ended a possible go-ahead drive. San Francisco had turned the ball over only six times in its first six games.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

4-0: Atlanta's turnover edge in the 49ers' only loss

The Falcons took the ball away four times and gave it away none in their 29-20 win. Atlanta scored 13 points off those turnovers. Shanahan said four giveaways with zero takeaways makes it very hard to win.

Source: ESPN ↗

5
What's Next

Raiders visit Levi's Stadium Sunday at 4:25 PM ET on FOX

The 49ers host Las Vegas on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 4:25 PM ET on FOX after a Week 8 bye. The Raiders are 4-4 and coming off a 38-33 win over the Jets. Watch the 49ers' receiver depth and the return of Bosa.

Source: Football Nation ↗
