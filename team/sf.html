San Francisco 49ers

7-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

49ers load up at the deadline with Allen and Buckner

San Francisco traded for receiver Keenan Allen and acquired defensive tackle DeForest Buckner from Indianapolis. Allen caught four passes for 26 yards in his first game back against the Colts. The 49ers sit at 7-1, tied for the best record in the league.

Source: Dallas Cowboys ↗

2
Injury Report

Mike Evans is out, Trent Williams is questionable

Evans is out with an Achilles injury. Left tackle Trent Williams (shoulder), linebacker Dre Greenlaw (knee) and four cornerbacks, Renardo Green, Nate Hobbs, Deommodore Lenoir and Upton Stout, are all questionable. Deebo Samuel has no designation and Kyle Shanahan says he is cleared.

Source: NFL.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Christian McCaffrey keeps scoring in bunches

McCaffrey ran 18 times for 68 yards and a touchdown and caught a touchdown pass in the win over the Raiders. He has scored a rushing touchdown in seven straight games, a 49ers single-season record. He has nine rushing scores this year.

Source: 49ers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

190: Rushing yards the 49ers piled up against Las Vegas

San Francisco ran 43 times, a season high in yards and its most carries since 2022. Shanahan teams are unbeaten when they run 40 or more times. The defense held the Raiders to 24 rushing yards.

Source: 49ers.com ↗

5
What's Next

49ers visit the Cowboys Sunday at 4:25 p.m. ET on FOX

The game is at AT&T Stadium in Arlington. Watch the thin cornerback group against CeeDee Lamb, who has the second-most catches in the league. Dallas is 4-5.
