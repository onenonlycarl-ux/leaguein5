NFC West · Tuesday, November 17 · Week 11

San Francisco 49ers

8-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

49ers outlast Cowboys 46-40 in a shootout

San Francisco gained 584 yards, its most in a game in 14 years, and stayed on top of the NFC at 8-1. The game was Sunday, Nov. 15, not Monday. Kyle Shanahan still grumbled about five sacks allowed and two pre-snap penalties. The Vikings, owners of the NFL's top defense, are next.

Source: myMotherLode / AP ↗

2
Injury Report

Green day to day; Evans, Hobbs, West nearing returns

Cornerback Renardo Green (back) left the Dallas game early and is day to day. Receiver Mike Evans (Achilles tendinitis) may practice this week after missing three games. Cornerback Nate Hobbs (adductor) and defensive tackle C.J. West (knee) are practicing and could come off injured reserve.

Source: myMotherLode / AP ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Christian McCaffrey piles up 223 yards and 3 TDs

McCaffrey caught four passes for 161 yards, including touchdowns of 74 and 66 yards, and ran 13 times for 62 yards and a fourth-quarter score. The 74-yarder was the longest receiving touchdown of his career. He now holds the 49ers record with 21 receiving touchdowns by a running back or fullback.

Source: 49ers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

389: Brock Purdy's passing yards in Dallas

Purdy went 22 of 28 for 389 yards and three touchdowns, per the Fox Sports box score. That box score lists the team's passing total as 370, so the two figures do not match. He also had 135 passing yards on scramble plays, including the 74-yard throw to McCaffrey.

Source: Fox Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Vikings in Mexico City, Sunday at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC

San Francisco plays Minnesota on Sunday, Nov. 22 at Estadio Azteca, a 5:20 p.m. PT kickoff on NBC's Sunday Night Football. The 49ers will practice in Colorado Springs first. Watch their top-two offense against a Vikings defense that took Green Bay for eight sacks.

Source: NBC Sports Bay Area ↗
