San Francisco 49ers

7-1Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

49ers force five second-half turnovers, rout Raiders 38-17

San Francisco bounced back from its Week 7 loss to move to 7-1. Jaden Dugger returned an interception 35 yards for a touchdown on the first play of the second half. It was the 49ers' first five-takeaway half since 2006.

Source: ABC7 (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Rookie Halton out for season with broken ankle

Defensive tackle Gracen Halton broke his left ankle late in the win and leaves after 15 tackles and two sacks in eight games. Cornerbacks Renardo Green (back) and Deommodore Lenoir (foot) also left the game, and Kyle Shanahan said more testing is coming. Nate Hobbs could return from IR against Dallas.

Source: TSN ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Purdy reaches 100 touchdown passes in 57 games

Brock Purdy threw two touchdown passes, including a 37-yarder to Jordan Watkins, and ran for another score. He became the 10th quarterback since the merger to reach 100 career touchdown passes in his first 57 games.

Source: ABC7 (AP) ↗

4
Stat of the Day

6: straight games Las Vegas has allowed 30 or more points

The Raiders tied an NFL record by giving up at least 30 points for a sixth straight game. San Francisco added to the pile with 38, and Fred Warner forced one fumble and recovered another.

Source: ABC7 (AP) ↗
