Las Vegas Raiders

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Raiders name Fernando Mendoza their starting quarterback

General manager John Spytek made the call Wednesday, a day after Las Vegas traded Kirk Cousins to Minnesota for a 2027 third-round pick. The No. 1 overall pick makes his first NFL start Sunday against Seattle. Spytek said Mendoza passed every test.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Raiders list four players on the Week 10 report

Defensive tackle Thomas Booker (ankle) and guard Trey Zuhn (concussion) were limited both Wednesday and Thursday. Defensive tackle Folorunso Fatukasi (shoulder, elbow) moved from limited to full. Tight end Ian Thomas (ankle) practiced fully both days.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Brock Bowers keeps setting tight end marks

Bowers caught seven passes for 111 yards and a touchdown in the 38-17 loss at San Francisco. He has 658 receiving yards and six touchdowns, both best among tight ends. He is also the fastest tight end to 2,500 career receiving yards, doing it in 36 games.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

2.9: Raiders' yards per carry is the lowest in the NFL

Las Vegas ran 15 times for 24 yards against the 49ers and has not had a 100-yard rushing game in six weeks. The defense also gives up a league-high 151.2 rushing yards per game, so Mendoza gets little help from the ground game on either side.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

5
What's Next

Raiders host Seahawks and their former offensive coordinator

Las Vegas hosts Seattle on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 4:05 p.m. ET (1:05 p.m. PT) on CBS at Allegiant Stadium. Seahawks QB Sam Darnold did not practice Wednesday or Thursday with ribs and quadriceps injuries. Raiders coach Klint Kubiak ran Seattle's offense for its Super Bowl title run.

Source: Raiders.com ↗
