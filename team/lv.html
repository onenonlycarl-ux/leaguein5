Las Vegas Raiders

4-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Five second-half takeaways sink Raiders, 38-17

San Francisco forced five turnovers after halftime and pulled away from Las Vegas. Kirk Cousins threw three interceptions, including a 35-yard pick-six by Jaden Dugger, and Michael Mayer lost a fumble at the goal line. The Raiders fell to 4-5.

Source: Fox 5 Vegas (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Zuhn leaves with a concussion

Offensive lineman Trey Zuhn left in the second half with a concussion. He will need to clear the protocol this week before he can face Seattle. The recap did not list any other Raiders injuries.

Source: Fox 5 Vegas (AP) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Bowers piles up 111 yards but fumbles

Brock Bowers caught seven passes for 111 yards and a 21-yard touchdown from Cousins. He also lost a fumble on a screen pass. It was a productive day for the tight end that still hurt in the turnover count.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

3: Cousins interceptions, one for a touchdown

Cousins threw three picks, with Dugger returning the first for a score after a bobbled snap and an underthrow. Renardo Green took another away in the end zone in the fourth quarter. Cousins also ran in a 1-yard touchdown, his second straight week with a rushing score.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

5
What's Next

Seahawks visit Sunday, 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS

Las Vegas hosts Seattle on Sunday, Nov. 15. The Raiders need to clean up ball security after giving it away five times in the second half in Santa Clara. Watch the offensive line if Zuhn cannot go.
