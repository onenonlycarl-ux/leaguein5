Injury Report

Bowers limited again, two defensive tackles sit out

Tight end Brock Bowers (ankle) was limited Wednesday and Thursday after missing the Denver game. Defensive tackles Adam Butler (concussion) and Tim Keenan (knee) did not practice either day. Tonka Hemingway (stinger) went from limited to out of practice Thursday.
