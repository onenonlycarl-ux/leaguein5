Las Vegas Raiders

4-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Mendoza gets a second straight test against a top defense

In his first start against Seattle, Fernando Mendoza went 11 of 13 for 90 yards with a 10-yard rushing touchdown in the first half. The offense then cooled, gained no positive yardage in the third quarter, and Mendoza took six sacks in the 21-13 loss. Denver's pass rush ranks in the top 10 in sacks and pressures.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

2
Injury Report

Zuhn, Hemingway and Gay miss Thursday's practice

Right guard Trey Zuhn (illness), defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway (stinger) and kicker Matt Gay (hip) did not practice Thursday. Tight end Brock Bowers (ankle and quad) went from no practice Wednesday to limited Thursday. Defensive tackle Folorunso Fatukasi (toe) also sat out Thursday. No game designations are out yet.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Crosby sits half a sack from 75 for his career

Maxx Crosby needs 0.5 sacks to reach 75.0 for his career, a mark the Raiders' game preview ties to undrafted or late-round players. He lines up against a Denver front with 26 sacks. Running back Ashton Jeanty is also one receiving touchdown from a franchise milestone for backs over a 27-game span.

Source: Raiders.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

70.7: rushing yards per game, the Raiders' weak spot

Las Vegas throws for 243.5 yards per game but runs for just 70.7, per FOX Sports team stats. The Raiders score 25.1 points per game and allow 28.9, so the passing game carries the offense.

Source: FOX Sports ↗

What's Next

Raiders visit Denver on Sunday at 4:25 PM ET on CBS

Las Vegas (4-6) faces the 7-2 Broncos at Empower Field. Denver won the last two meetings, 10-7 and 24-17. Watch Bowers' ankle and whether the Raiders can protect Mendoza.
