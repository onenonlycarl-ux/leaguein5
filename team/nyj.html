AFC East · Monday, November 16 · Week 10

New York Jets

2-8Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Jets fall 31-12 to the Bills as Geno Smith leaves hurt

Smith took a hit before halftime and was checked for a head injury. He came back for a drive, then left in the third quarter with an ankle injury. Rookie Cade Klubnik took over and made his NFL debut. The Jets dropped to 2-8 with their fifth straight loss, and the offense produced only four Jason Sanders field goals.

Source: CBS News New York ↗

2
Injury Report

Smith (ankle), Cooper (ankle) and Parham (knee) all got hurt

Smith was ruled out after his ankle injury and finished 13 of 21 for 148 yards. Rookie receiver Omar Cooper Jr. hurt an ankle on the first offensive play, and left guard Dylan Parham hurt a knee early in the second quarter. Aaron Glenn will pick a starter for Sunday based on how Smith's ankle responds.

Source: Spectrum News ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Garrett Wilson catches 7 passes for 106 yards

Wilson had a 33-yard catch in the first half and a 29-yarder in the second, and both set up field goals. It was his second 100-yard game of the year. He also owned a fourth-quarter hook-and-lateral that Buffalo recovered before James Cook ran 74 yards for a touchdown. Wilson said he flubbed it.

Source: NewYorkJets.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

9: straight quarters without a Jets offensive touchdown

New York's four scores against Buffalo were all Sanders field goals, so the drought has reached nine quarters. The Jets also went 0 for 2 in the red zone. Klubnik finished 11 of 20 for 137 yards with one interception and ran three times for 26 yards.

Source: Spectrum News ↗

5
What's Next

Jets visit the winless Chargers Sunday at 4:05 p.m. ET on Fox

New York plays at Los Angeles on Sunday, Nov. 22 at 4:05 p.m. ET on Fox. The Chargers are 0-8, so this is a winnable game for a team on a five-game skid. The quarterback is the thing to watch, with Klubnik a possible first-time starter if Smith cannot go.

Source: NFL.com ↗
