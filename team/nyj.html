Biggest Story

Jets fall to Raiders 38-33 for third straight loss

The Jets dropped to 2-6 after giving up a 95-yard kickoff return touchdown and failing to force a turnover. Geno Smith threw an interception on the game's final play. Las Vegas went 4 for 4 in the red zone.

Injury Report

Wilson and Wallace out; Hall and Bailey no designation

Receiver Garrett Wilson (concussion) and linebacker Trevin Wallace (groin) are out against the Chiefs. Breece Hall (quad) and David Bailey (groin) practiced fully Friday with no designation. Edge Joseph Ossai, safety VJ Payne, lineman Harrison Phillips and corner Nahshon Wright are questionable.

Player Spotlight

Braelon Allen runs for 147 yards in Hall's absence

Allen carried 21 times for 147 yards and a touchdown against Las Vegas while Hall sat out a fourth straight game. He could share carries with Hall now that Hall is practicing in full.

Stat of the Day

23: sacks taken by Geno Smith through eight games

Smith has been sacked 23 times this season but has still played well, according to the Jets' beat coverage. Kansas City's pass rush posted a season-low 6 percent pressure rate in Week 8, which gives the line a chance to hold up.

What's Next
