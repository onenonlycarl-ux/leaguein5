1
Biggest Story

Jets end five-game skid with 27-24 win in L.A.

New York scored on its first five possessions and held on to beat the Chargers. Geno Smith threw three touchdown passes, and Breece Hall ran 20 times for 86 yards. The Jets now try to avoid a season sweep by Miami.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Williams, Cooper, Wallace questionable for Miami

Isaiah Williams, Omar Cooper Jr. and Trevin Wallace are listed as questionable. Williams and Cooper sat out the Chargers game, and Sterling Shepard filled in from the practice squad to catch two touchdowns.

Source: Sports Illustrated ↗

3
Player Spotlight

David Bailey is closing in on the rookie sack record

The No. 2 overall pick has 9.5 sacks, third in the league and half a sack short of the Jets rookie record. He is the first player since 1982 with at least 9.5 sacks and six forced fumbles through 10 career games.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

9 of 11: Jets third-down conversions against L.A.

New York converted nine of 11 third downs in the win over the Chargers. Smith went 17 of 21 for 176 yards with no turnovers, and the Jets ran the ball well behind Hall and Braelon Allen, who had 75 yards.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

What's Next

Jets at Dolphins, Sunday 1 p.m. ET on CBS

The Jets play at Hard Rock Stadium and want to avoid a sweep after losing the first meeting 28-25 on Oct. 25. They have a bye in Week 13. Watch whether Frank Reich keeps calling plays from the sideline.
