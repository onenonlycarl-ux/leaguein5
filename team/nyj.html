1
Biggest Story

Jets limp into Buffalo game on a four-game skid

New York is 2-7 after a 35-12 loss at Kansas City. The Jets host the 7-1 Bills on Sunday, their last home game before a two-game road trip and a bye. Andre Cisco went to Detroit for a 2029 sixth-round pick, the team's only deadline trade.

Source: amNY ↗

2
Injury Report

Garrett Wilson still limited in concussion protocol

Wilson practiced on a limited basis Wednesday and Thursday after missing the Chiefs game. Coach Aaron Glenn said he is very hopeful Wilson plays. Defensive lineman Harrison Phillips (ankle) and linebacker Trevin Wallace (groin) did not practice Thursday. Receiver Omar Cooper Jr. (ankle) was limited. One earlier report called Wilson out, so watch for the official designation.

Source: Jets.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie David Bailey wrecks the Chiefs' protection

The rookie edge rusher had two sacks, two forced fumbles and nine tackles in the loss at Kansas City, the one bright spot on the day. Through eight career games he has 40 pressures along with those two sacks and two forced fumbles.

Source: amNY ↗

4
Stat of the Day

5: Jets have scored on their opening drive five straight games

That is the longest active streak in the NFL, according to the team. The fast starts have not turned into wins during a four-game losing streak. Geno Smith ranks fifth among qualified quarterbacks with a 67.5 completion percentage.

Source: Jets.com ↗

What's Next

Jets host the Bills Sunday at 1 p.m. on CBS

The game is at MetLife Stadium. Buffalo leads the NFL in yards and points per game, and Glenn says everything starts with Josh Allen. Watch whether Breece Hall, back from a five-game absence, can keep the ball away from that offense.
