Biggest Story

Aaron Glenn feels the heat as Jets slide to 2-7

The Jets have lost four straight after a 35-12 defeat to Kansas City, and Glenn is 5-21 in his season and a half as head coach. Speculation about his job is growing. Glenn said the team feels it is on the cusp of breaking through.

Source: AP via The Fan 107.5 ↗

2
Injury Report

Garrett Wilson questionable, Trevin Wallace out

Linebacker Trevin Wallace (groin) is out. Eight Jets are questionable, including receivers Garrett Wilson (concussion), Adonai Mitchell (knee) and Omar Cooper Jr. (ankle), plus edge Joseph Ossai (ankle) and lineman Harrison Phillips (ankle). Wilson, Cooper and Ossai were full participants Friday.

Source: NewYorkJets.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rookie David Bailey sets the pace with 7.5 sacks

The No. 2 overall pick had two sacks and two forced fumbles on Patrick Mahomes in the loss to Kansas City. Bailey shares the NFL lead with five forced fumbles. Bills coach Joe Brady called him already a star.

Source: NewYorkJets.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

13: Jets lead the NFL in forced fumbles

The Jets defense has forced 13 fumbles, the most in the league. The offense has scored on its opening possession in five straight games, but five of seven losses came by a combined 25 points.

Source: NewYorkJets.com ↗

What's Next

Jets host the 7-1 Bills Sunday at 1 p.m. on CBS

The game is at MetLife Stadium. Geno Smith needs a rebound after throwing for 165 yards and no touchdowns and taking five sacks against Kansas City. New York also has to slow Buffalo's run game and stop settling for red zone field goals.
