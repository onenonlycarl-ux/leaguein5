Pittsburgh Steelers

6-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Steelers release Aaron Rodgers after interview

Pittsburgh benched Rodgers this week, then released him Wednesday after he criticized coach Mike McCarthy on The Pat McAfee Show. Rodgers said he is not going back to Pittsburgh. Any team could claim him until 4 p.m. ET Thursday, and he becomes a free agent if nobody does.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

No new injury news as quarterback change dominates

None of the articles I read listed a new Steelers injury designation this week. Rodgers' absence was a coaching decision, not an injury, according to ESPN's sources. The official Friday injury report should confirm who is available against Denver.

Source: ESPN ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Mason Rudolph takes over against Denver

Rudolph, 31, has 20 career starts and a 9-10-1 record, and he is 1-5 in his last six starts dating to 2024. This season he has played 19 snaps and completed 1 of 4 passes for 2 yards. He has never faced Denver or a defense coached by Vance Joseph.

Source: NFL.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

257: yards lost to sacks by Rodgers, most in the NFL

Rodgers took 31 sacks this season, already more than the 29 he took last year, and completed 60.9 percent of his passes. ESPN's numbers also put his off-target rate at 18.9 percent, 28th among 30 qualified passers. The Steelers lost 19-16 to Philadelphia in Week 11.

Source: ESPN ↗

5
What's Next

Broncos at Acrisure Stadium, Friday 3 p.m. ET on Prime Video

Pittsburgh plays Denver on Black Friday in a game that matters for AFC seeding. The Broncos are chasing a seventh straight win. Watch how Rudolph handles a Vance Joseph defense and whether the running game carries the offense.

Source: NFL.com ↗
