Biggest Story

Steelers fall 38-18 to Bengals, lose division lead

Pittsburgh punted on its first three drives and had about 40 yards of offense at halftime. It did not record a first down until its third possession. The Bengals now hold first place and have beaten the Steelers twice this season.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

McCormick carted off with ankle injury

Left guard Mason McCormick left with a right ankle injury and an air cast, and Spencer Anderson replaced him. McCormick had played every offensive snap this season. Pat Freiermuth called it a really big loss, and the team has given no timeline.

Source: Yardbarker ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Rodgers starts 0 for 8 in worst opening of career

Aaron Rodgers missed his first eight throws, the worst start of his career, and was pressured on 46.9 percent of his dropbacks. He completed four passes for 25 yards in the first half. DK Metcalf had two catches for 54 yards on six targets.

Source: NFL.com ↗

Stat of the Day
