Pittsburgh Steelers

6-3Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bengals bury Steelers 38-18, ending Pittsburgh's four-game streak

Pittsburgh fell behind 17-0 and 24-3 at halftime on Sunday night in Cincinnati. The loss dropped the Steelers to 6-3 and out of first place in the AFC North. The offense did not record a first down until well into the second quarter.

Source: Local 12 ↗

2
Injury Report

Six Steelers questionable for Eagles, Herbig missed practice

Linebackers Patrick Queen (hand) and Nick Herbig (hip, ribs), safety DeShon Elliott (knee), receivers Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson, and guard Gennings Dunker are all questionable. Herbig did not practice Friday, while the others went full. Left guard Mason McCormick hurt his ankle against the Bengals.

Source: Steelers.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Payton Wilson leads Steelers with 17 tackles at Cincinnati

Linebacker Payton Wilson made 17 tackles against the Bengals, and Patrick Queen added 14. Jalen Ramsey forced a fumble in a game where neither side lost the ball.

Source: Steelers.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

21: completions for Burrow before Rodgers had one

Joe Burrow completed 21 passes before Aaron Rodgers recorded his first, which matched the most by one team before its opponent's first since play-by-play data began in 1978. Rodgers finished 15 of 29 for 143 yards and two touchdowns.

Source: Local 12 ↗

What's Next

Steelers at Eagles on Sunday afternoon on CBS

Pittsburgh plays at 2-7 Philadelphia on Sunday, Nov. 22, at 1 p.m. ET on CBS at Lincoln Financial Field. The Steelers need a response after Sunday night's loss. Watch the status of Herbig and Queen on defense.
