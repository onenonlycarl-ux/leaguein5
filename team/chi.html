Chicago Bears

1
Biggest Story

Williams says he has moved on from the four-pick game

Caleb Williams threw four interceptions in the 27-24 win over Tampa Bay, two of them in scoring range. He called one throw a dumb decision and says he has put it behind him. The Bears are 7-2 and tied atop the NFC North, and six of their last eight opponents are current playoff teams.

Source: Chicago Sun-Times ↗

2
Injury Report

Gordon, Burden and Monangai all trending toward Saints game

Kyler Gordon (left arm) sat out Monday's practice, but the paper says it is too early to worry about Sunday. Luther Burden practiced Monday after a concussion, and Kyle Monangai (toe) practiced and is expected back for the first time since Oct. 4. Tyrique Stevenson's thumb did not keep him off the field.

Source: Chicago Sun-Times ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Booker has a career-high five sacks and counting

Austin Booker has five sacks and six tackles for loss, both career highs. Fox 32 lists him and Devin Bush as the defenders who have improved most as the season has gone on. The defense bailed out the offense against the Buccaneers.

Source: FOX 32 Chicago ↗

4
Stat of the Day

6 of 8: Bears face playoff teams in six of their last eight

The Sun-Times calls it the NFL's toughest remaining schedule by opponent winning percentage. That run includes a Thanksgiving game at Detroit. Caleb Williams enters it with a 68.7 completion percentage, third in the league.

Source: Chicago Sun-Times ↗

What's Next

Saints visit Soldier Field Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on FOX

The Bears host New Orleans (3-6), which the Sun-Times says has one of the league's worst defenses. Williams has a sore hamstring but has been cleared. Watch whether he avoids the turnovers that nearly cost Chicago against Tampa Bay.
