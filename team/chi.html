Biggest Story

Simmons makes a loud Bears debut in 34-17 rout of Seattle

The Bears sent two first-round picks and a fourth-rounder to Tennessee for defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons, then watched him get a sack in his first game. Seattle gained just 3.0 yards per play over the 48 snaps Simmons played. Chicago has won three straight and sits at 6-2.

Injury Report

Bears list two non-participants ahead of Tampa Bay

Linebacker D'Marco Jackson (ankle) and running back Kyle Monangai (thumb and toe) did not practice Thursday. Lineman Theo Benedet (shoulder), defensive tackle Grady Jarrett (groin) and defensive back Malik Muhammad (toe) were limited. Tackle Braxton Jones is out for the season.

Source: Bear Goggles On ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Caleb Williams opens with a 17-play touchdown drive in Seattle

Williams completed 70.6 percent of his passes for 247 yards against the Seahawks. He went 10 of 15 for 109 yards and a touchdown on play-action. His opening march covered 80 yards in 17 plays, the longest opening drive for Chicago since 2007.

Source: ESPN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

10: D'Andre Swift's rushing touchdowns lead the NFL

Swift ran 24 times for 57 yards and three touchdowns against Seattle. That pushed him to a league-high 10 rushing scores this season. The Bears also lead the league with 17 rushing touchdowns as a team.

Source: ESPN ↗

What's Next

Bears host Buccaneers on Sunday Night Football

Chicago plays Tampa Bay (3-5) on Sunday at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC at Soldier Field. Watch how the Bears defense handles deep shots, since it has allowed 17 pass plays of 30 or more yards, the most in the league. The Bears rank second in sacks per pass attempt.
