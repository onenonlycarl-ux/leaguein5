Bears and Lions meet at 8-2 with the NFC North lead at stake

Injury Report

Gordon, Hicks and Johnson out for Thanksgiving

Cornerback Kyler Gordon (shoulder), safety Elijah Hicks (concussion) and running back Roschon Johnson (ankle) are ruled out. Hicks and Johnson both play key special teams roles. Cornerback Tyrique Stevenson (groin) and linebacker D'Marco Jackson (elbow) are questionable.

Player Spotlight

Jeffery Simmons turns the trade into a defensive reset

The defensive tackle came from Tennessee on Oct. 29 and already has 5.0 sacks, tied for the team lead. Three of them came against the Saints. He now plays his first Thanksgiving game as a pro.

Stat of the Day

5: straight games the Bears have not trailed, their first such run since 1985

Caleb Williams has not lost a game he started and finished this season. Chicago also tied a franchise record by scoring in all 20 quarters during the streak. The Bears did lose some of their edge last week, when a 16-0 lead over the Saints shrank to a 21-13 final.
