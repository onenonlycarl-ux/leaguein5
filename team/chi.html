1
Biggest Story

Bears head to Detroit with first place on the line

The Bears have won five straight and sit tied with the Lions at 8-2 atop the NFC North. The winner on Thursday takes sole possession of first. Caleb Williams is 0-4 against Detroit in his career, and the Bears are 0-2 in division play this season.

Source: 104.5 The Zone ↗

2
Injury Report

Kyler Gordon sits out practice with a shoulder injury

The cornerback did not practice Monday or Tuesday and has no game status yet. He has been inactive since Week 11. Tight end Cole Kmet and tackle Braxton Jones are done for the season with knee injuries, and safety Coby Bryant is rehabbing from knee surgery.

Source: DraftKings Network ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jeffery Simmons has changed the Bears defense

Simmons has nine sacks on the season, fourth most in the NFL, and five of them came in his first three games after the trade from Tennessee. Montez Sweat has added two sacks and seven quarterback hits over the last three games.

Source: BVM Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

142.4: Bears rushing yards per game, third in the NFL

Detroit coach Dan Campbell pointed to the Chicago ground game as a problem to solve. The Bears also rank eighth in scoring at 26.2 points per game. Campbell named Caleb Williams, Luther Burden III, Colston Loveland and D'Andre Swift as the players to contain.

Source: Detroit Lions ↗

What's Next

At Lions on Thanksgiving: 1 p.m. ET on CBS

The Bears play at Ford Field on Thursday, Nov. 26, with Jim Nantz and J.J. Watt on the call. Watch whether the Lions can pressure Williams after getting only 10 pressures outside their four sacks of Baker Mayfield last week.
