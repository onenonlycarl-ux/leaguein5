Chicago Bears

9-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bears steal Thanksgiving in Detroit and take over the NFC North

Chicago trailed for most of the afternoon, then Caleb Williams found Rome Odunze for a 6-yard touchdown with three seconds left to beat Detroit 39-35. The win moved the Bears to 9-2 and into sole possession of first place in the division.

Source: Sky Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Loveland leaves with a knee injury, Kmet already out

Rookie tight end Colston Loveland hurt his knee in the first quarter and did not return. The Bears were already without Cole Kmet, who is out for the season with his own knee injury, so the tight end room is thin heading into December.

Source: NFL.com ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Williams throws for 345 yards and two scores in Detroit

Caleb Williams went 30 of 44 for 345 yards and two touchdowns without an interception, and he added 15 yards on three carries. Odunze caught six passes for 87 yards and two touchdowns, including the winner. D'Andre Swift had seven catches for 97 yards.

Source: Bleacher Report ↗

4
Stat of the Day

39: points the Bears scored in the win at Detroit

Chicago put up 39 at Ford Field and still needed every one of them, because Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 205 yards and three touchdowns for the Lions. The Bears are 1-2 in the division and 7-2 in the conference.

Source: Bleacher Report ↗

5
What's Next

Jaguars visit Soldier Field on Dec. 6

Chicago hosts Jacksonville on Sunday, Dec. 6 at 1 p.m. ET. Watch the tight end snaps without Loveland and Kmet. The Bears meet the Lions again at Soldier Field on Jan. 3.
