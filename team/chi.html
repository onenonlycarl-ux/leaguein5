Chicago Bears

7-2Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Bears beat Tampa Bay on a last-second field goal

Chicago turned the ball over five times and still beat the Buccaneers 27-24 at Soldier Field. The Bears led 17-3 at the half, then watched Tampa Bay tie it late. Caleb Williams hit Colston Loveland for 38 yards on the final drive, and Cairo Santos kicked a 38-yard field goal as time ran out. It was Chicago's fourth straight win.

Source: AP via Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

2
Injury Report

Burden in concussion protocol, Stevenson hurt

Receiver Luther Burden III left Sunday night with a concussion and is in the league protocol. The bye week gives him time to clear it. AP also reported cornerback Tyrique Stevenson hurt his thumb. Running back Kyle Monangai and defensive back Malik Muhammad are expected back after the break.

Source: FOX 32 Chicago ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Jaylon Johnson returns an interception 67 yards for a score

The Bears cornerback took a Baker Mayfield pass back 67 yards for a touchdown, the second pick-six of his career. Johnson's score helped Chicago build its early lead on a night when the offense gave the ball away five times. Mayfield threw two interceptions in the game.

Source: AP via Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

4
Stat of the Day

5: turnovers in a Bears win, the first such win since 2006

Chicago gave the ball away five times, four of them Williams interceptions, and still won. The AP says it is the Bears' first victory with five or more turnovers since 2006. Williams finished 22 of 37 for 279 yards and two touchdowns. Ben Johnson said it is hard to win that way, and the team found a way anyway.

Source: AP via Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

5
What's Next

Bye week, then the Saints at Soldier Field

Chicago is off in Week 10 after three straight primetime games. The Bears host New Orleans on Sunday, Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET on FOX. Watch whether the offense cleans up the turnovers and whether Burden clears the protocol.
