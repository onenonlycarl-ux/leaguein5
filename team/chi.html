Chicago Bears

1
Biggest Story

Bears put a four-game win streak on the line against the Saints

Chicago (7-2) comes out of its bye with four straight wins, including a road win in Seattle. New Orleans (3-6) lost 20-17 to Carolina last week and allows 26.2 points per game. The Bears host the Saints on Sunday at Soldier Field.

Source: SI ↗

2
Injury Report

Odeyingbo stays on IR as Burden clears the concussion protocol

Receiver Luther Burden III has exited the concussion protocol, though Ben Johnson said his Week 11 status is still to be determined. Running back Kyle Monangai (toe) and cornerback Malik Muhammad II are on track to play. Cornerback Kyler Gordon (shoulder) is day-to-day, and defensive lineman Dayo Odeyingbo will not be activated from injured reserve this week.

Source: Fox 32 Chicago ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Williams says four-interception game will not make him cautious

Caleb Williams threw a career-high four interceptions in the 27-24 win over Tampa Bay on Nov. 8, then led the drive for Cairo Santos' 38-yard winning field goal. Tampa Bay blitzed him on 66 percent of his dropbacks. Williams said he got greedy on one goal-line throw and that he will keep attacking.

Source: TSN ↗

4
Stat of the Day

26.2: points per game the Saints give up

New Orleans also allows 231 passing yards per game. Williams went 22 of 37 for 279 yards, two touchdowns and four interceptions in his last start. Against the Saints last season he went 15 of 26 for 172 yards with one interception.

Source: SI ↗

What's Next

Saints visit Soldier Field Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on FOX

Chicago (7-2) hosts New Orleans (3-6) in Week 11. The Saints list center Erik McCoy and tackle Taliese Fuaga as doubtful. Watch whether the Bears protect Williams against pressure without extra blockers.
