Biggest Story

Bears sit at 7-2 but lose the division lead on their bye

Chicago did not play in Week 10 and watched Detroit and Minnesota both win to pull level at 7-2. The Bears now sit third in the NFC North and sixth in the conference. They return Sunday at home against New Orleans, with the NFL's toughest remaining schedule ahead.

Injury Report

Monangai still working back from a Week 5 injury

Running back Kyle Monangai has missed games since Week 5. He practiced on a limited basis in Week 9 and it remains unclear whether he plays against New Orleans. Receiver Zavion Thomas is likely to sit a game or two after a Week 9 penalty.

Source: Athlon Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Swift leads the league with 10 rushing touchdowns

D'Andre Swift carries the NFL's third-best rushing attack into the second half. Luther Burden III has 49 catches for 644 yards and four touchdowns, and he and Rome Odunze are both on pace for 1,000 yards.

Stat of the Day

10: points Chicago is favored by against the Saints

The Bears opened as 10-point favorites over New Orleans, their first line of eight points or more since 2020. The Saints are 3-6, but the Bears have allowed the most pass plays of 40 yards or more in the league.

What's Next

Saints visit Soldier Field on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET
