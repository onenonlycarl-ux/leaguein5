Seattle Seahawks

4-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Darnold out against Arizona, Lock to start

Sam Darnold will miss Sunday's game with a quadriceps injury that Mike Macdonald called week to week. He hurt it on Seattle's last drive of the 34-17 Monday night loss to Chicago, after playing through a rib injury. Drew Lock starts and Jalen Milroe is the backup.

Source: KOMO News ↗

2
Injury Report

Seven Seahawks out, including Cross, Nelson and Williams

Seattle ruled out quarterback Sam Darnold, tackle Charles Cross (ankle), guard Quenton Nelson (knee), receiver Rashid Shaheed (knee), defensive tackles Leonard Williams (stinger) and Brandon Pili (back), and linebacker Uchenna Nwosu (ankle). Linebacker Connor O'Toole (foot) is questionable. The offensive line loses two starters.

Source: Arizona Cardinals ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Drew Lock steps in with a clean touchdown record

Lock has four touchdowns and no interceptions this season. In Week 2 he beat Arizona 31-7, completing 19 of 26 passes for 235 yards and three touchdowns. He has spent the last two games as the emergency third quarterback because of forearm and elbow injuries.

Source: KOMO News ↗

4
Stat of the Day

985: Jaxon Smith-Njigba's receiving yards through eight games

Smith-Njigba had six catches for 118 yards and a touchdown against Chicago. His 985 yards lead the NFL by 259 and trail only Tyreek Hill's 1,014 in 2023 for the most through eight games in the Super Bowl era. It was his seventh game this season with 100 or more receiving yards.

Source: Seahawks.com ↗

What's Next

Cardinals visit Lumen Field Sunday at 4:25 PM ET on FOX

Seattle hosts Arizona on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 4:25 PM ET on FOX. The Seahawks are 4-4 and need a bounce back after Monday's 34-17 loss. Watch how Lock and a thinned offensive line handle Arizona's pass rush.
