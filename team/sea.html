Seattle Seahawks

5-4Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Lock and Barner lead Seattle past Arizona 17-3

Drew Lock, starting for the injured Sam Darnold, went 19 of 28 for 175 yards and a touchdown. Tight end A.J. Barner scored twice, once on a catch and once on a run, and Seattle improved to 5-4 with a season sweep of the Cardinals.

Source: FOX 13 Seattle (AP) ↗

2
Injury Report

Darnold out again, Lock managing oblique and quad

Mike Macdonald said Lock will start Sunday in Las Vegas. Darnold, who has a cracked rib and a quad injury, is week to week and may return after the Week 11 bye. Left guard Quenton Nelson had a knee procedure and will miss the Raiders game.

Source: ABC7 (AP) ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Smith-Njigba tops 1,000 yards through nine games

Jaxon Smith-Njigba caught seven passes for 51 yards against Arizona and now has 1,036 receiving yards. He is the third Seahawk with three straight 1,000-yard seasons, joining Tyler Lockett and Steve Largent.

Source: Seahawks.com ↗

4
Stat of the Day

11: straight wins over Arizona

Seattle has beaten the Cardinals 11 times in a row, the longest streak against one opponent in team history. The defense held Arizona to 198 yards and 3 points, and Derick Hall had a career-high three tackles for loss.

Source: Seahawks.com ↗

5
What's Next

At Las Vegas Sunday, then the bye

Seattle plays the Raiders on Sunday, Nov. 15, at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS at Allegiant Stadium, then has its Week 11 bye. Watch whether Lock's oblique and the missing offensive line pieces hold up.

Source: DIRECTV ↗
