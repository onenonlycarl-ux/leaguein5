Biggest Story

Texans beat Colts 26-3, now eye a Ravens team in free fall

Houston is 4-6 and sits three games behind Jacksonville in the AFC South. C.J. Stroud went 21 of 27 for 194 yards and three touchdowns in the win over Indianapolis. Baltimore arrives at 4-7 after six straight losses.

Injury Report

Hunter, Boutte and Rutledge sit out practice again

Danielle Hunter (hamstring), Kayshon Boutte (hip), Brevin Jordan (finger and foot) and Keylan Rutledge (neck and shoulder) did not practice Wednesday or Thursday. Sheldon Rankins (ankle) was limited Thursday. Marvin Harrison Jr. (hamstring) went full after a limited Wednesday.

Source: Houston Texans ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Will Anderson Jr. leads the league in sacks

Anderson tops the NFL sack chart, and Houston's defense ranks third in the league in sacks as a team. He gets a Baltimore offense that has allowed 27 sacks this season.

Source: Toro Times ↗

Stat of the Day

4: C.J. Stroud's interceptions all season

Stroud has thrown four interceptions this year, and Houston has the fewest giveaways in the NFL. Last year against Baltimore he went 23 of 27 for 244 yards and four touchdowns in a 44-10 Texans win.
