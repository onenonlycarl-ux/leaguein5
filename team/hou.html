Houston Texans

2-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Texans return from bye at 2-5 with a winless Chargers team next

Houston sits 2-5 after its Week 8 bye and heads to SoFi Stadium to face the Chargers, the league's only winless team at 0-7. The Texans use the trip to show they fixed the tackling, penalty and communication problems that left them among the worst in the league at allowing explosive plays.

Source: Football Nation USA ↗

2
Injury Report

Noel done for the year, Dell back in the receiver mix

Receiver Jaylin Noel went on injured reserve with a toe injury and will miss the rest of the season. Tank Dell returned just before the bye and should pick up some of that workload. The last official pre-bye report listed Reed Blankenship, Blake Fisher, David Montgomery and Jalen Pitre as out, so check Friday's report for Week 9 designations.

Source: Houston Press ↗

3
Player Spotlight

C.J. Stroud protects the ball while taking hits

Stroud has completed 160 of 247 passes for 1,867 yards, 11 touchdowns and one interception, good for a 100.7 rating. He has also absorbed 15 sacks, so keeping him upright on early downs is the first job against Los Angeles.

Source: Football Nation USA ↗

4
Stat of the Day

27: Straight Chargers drives without a touchdown

The Chargers have gone 27 consecutive offensive possessions without a touchdown, and quarterback Justin Herbert has thrown 10 interceptions in seven games. Houston owns a +3 turnover margin, while Los Angeles sits at -6, which gives the Texans a chance to win the field position battle.

Source: Football Nation USA ↗

5
What's Next

At Chargers, Sunday 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS

Houston plays at the 0-7 Chargers at SoFi Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 8. Watch how the Texans use Dell, Marvin Harrison Jr. and Nico Collins together, and whether the offense leans less on its six-lineman packages.
