AFC South · Friday, November 13 · Week 10

Houston Texans

3-5Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Texans go to Cleveland with the wind at their backs

Houston opened 0-4 and has won three of its last four, the latest a 44-22 romp over the Chargers after the bye. Now the Texans visit a Browns team that is 3-0 at home and starts former Houston quarterback Deshaun Watson. Houston is a 4.5-point favorite.

Source: TSN (Canadian Press) ↗

2
Injury Report

Montgomery still sidelined as Texans open the week

Running back David Montgomery (groin) did not practice Wednesday and has not played since Week 5. Linebacker Azeez Al-Shaair, tight end Brevin Jordan and guard Keylan Rutledge also sat out. Tank Dell, Marvin Harrison Jr. and safety Reed Blankenship (concussion) were limited.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Boutte and Dell supply the deep shots in the rout

Kayshon Boutte set a career high with 125 receiving yards and two touchdowns against the Chargers, including a 63-yard score in the fourth quarter. Tank Dell returned from a knee injury that cost him almost two years and caught four passes for 105 yards. C.J. Stroud threw five touchdown passes in the win.

Source: AP via Yahoo Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

9.5: Will Anderson Jr.'s league-best sack total

Anderson leads the NFL with 9.5 sacks, and he faces a Browns line that has given up a league-high 38. Houston ranks fifth in the league with 21 team sacks.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

What's Next

At Browns, Sunday 1 p.m. ET on FOX

Houston plays at Huntington Bank Field against a Browns team that is 5-4 overall. Watch the Texans pass rush against Cleveland's protection and whether Watson, back from a concussion, can stay upright.
