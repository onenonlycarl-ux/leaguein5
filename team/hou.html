AFC South · Wednesday, November 25 · Week 12

Houston Texans

4-6Today's 5 stories
1
Biggest Story

Texans bury Colts 26-3 on Thursday night

Houston beat Indianapolis 26-3 on Thursday night at home and moved to 4-6 after a Week 10 loss at Cleveland. C.J. Stroud threw three touchdown passes, and the ground game gave him room to work. The win kept the Texans in the AFC playoff picture.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

2
Injury Report

Harrison and Hunter hamstring strains look short term

Marvin Harrison Jr. and Danielle Hunter both sat out the Colts game with hamstring problems. Hunter has now missed two straight. Aaron Wilson reports both could return for the Ravens game. Running back Woody Marks missed Week 11 with a concussion.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

3
Player Spotlight

Montgomery posts his first 100-yard game as a Texan

David Montgomery carried 26 times for 107 yards against Indianapolis and added 17 receiving yards. It was his first 100-yard day since joining Houston. He cleared a groin issue before the game, though his workload may shrink once Marks returns.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

4
Stat of the Day

21 of 27: Stroud stays sharp and adds 27 rushing yards

Stroud completed 21 of 27 passes for 194 yards, three touchdowns and one interception against the Colts. He also ran for 27 yards. Nico Collins and Kayshon Boutte each caught a touchdown pass.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

5
What's Next

Ravens visit Houston on Sunday

Houston hosts Baltimore on Sunday, Nov. 29 at 1 p.m. ET on CBS. Watch the hamstring updates on Harrison and Hunter, and whether Montgomery keeps the lead role. Baltimore is 4-7.

Source: NBC Sports ↗
