What everyone's talking about

Biggest Story

Cousins starts for the Vikings five days after the deadline trade

Minnesota sent for Kirk Cousins on deadline day and Kevin O'Connell named him the starter at Lambeau today. Carson Wentz went 8 of 25 for 81 yards with three turnovers in Monday's loss to Buffalo, and Kyler Murray is out with a hamstring injury. Las Vegas gave the job to rookie Fernando Mendoza.

Source: NFL.com ↗

Injury Watch

Mike Evans' Achilles injury moves Keenan Allen into the 49ers lineup

Evans is sidelined, so Allen, a trade deadline pickup, is expected to play at Dallas today. Left tackle Trent Williams also sat out Friday practice with a shoulder injury and carries no formal designation. Brock Purdy says Allen already knows the plays and formations.

Source: Yahoo Sports (ClutchPoints) ↗

Player Spotlight

C.J. Stroud throws five touchdowns in Houston's rout of the Chargers

Stroud went 21 of 35 for 384 yards, five touchdowns and one interception in the 44-22 win. Houston piled up 485 total yards, and Boutte caught two scores for 125 yards. The Texans now chase a third win in four games at Cleveland.

Source: FOX 26 Houston ↗

Stat of the Day

322: Josh Allen's career touchdowns tie Peyton Manning

Allen threw for 234 yards and a 25-yard score to Keleki Latu in Monday's 23-6 win over Minnesota. His 322 regular-season touchdowns, 232 passing, 88 rushing and 2 receiving, match Manning for the most through a player's first 10 years. The 7-1 Bills visit the Jets today.

Source: Associated Press via KTTC ↗

What's Next

Steelers visit the Bengals on Sunday night with the AFC North lead at stake

Both teams enter 6-2, and kickoff is 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. Cincinnati has won four straight, and Pittsburgh took the first meeting 30-27 on Sept. 27. Watch the Bengals' run defense against Jaylen Warren.

Source: Fox Sports ↗
