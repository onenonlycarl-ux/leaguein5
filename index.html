What everyone's talking about

Biggest Story

Ravens trade for Jacoby Brissett after Lamar Jackson goes down

Baltimore sent Arizona a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 seventh, and got a 2027 fifth back. The deal follows a four-game losing streak and injuries to both Jackson and Tyler Huntley. The Ravens have not said whether Brissett starts.

Source: The Sports Encounter ↗

Injury Watch

Deshaun Watson stays in the concussion protocol, Sanders starts

Watson reported symptoms Monday night after a helmet hit from Steelers safety Jaquan Brisker. He practiced on a limited basis Friday but has not been cleared and is not expected to play. Shedeur Sanders takes the Week 9 start for Cleveland.

Source: NFL.com ↗

Player Spotlight

Myles Garrett expected to make his Rams debut at Washington

Garrett played Week 1 in Australia, then had knee surgery and went on injured reserve. His practice window opened this week, and Sean McVay expects him to play Sunday barring setbacks.

Source: TheRams.com ↗

Stat of the Day

0: pass completions in a Buccaneers win, a first since 1974

Tampa Bay beat Atlanta 16-0 without a completion, the first team to win that way since Buffalo on Sept. 29, 1974. The Bucs ran for 324 yards, a single-game team record, and kicker Chase McLaughlin is 19 for 19 on field goals.

Source: Chicago Sun-Times ↗

What's Next

Packers visit Patriots on Sunday at 4:25 p.m. ET

Green Bay plays at Gillette Stadium on Fox against New England (5-3). Drake Maye has accounted for at least three touchdowns in five straight games, and A.J. Brown is out for the Patriots.

Source: ESPN ↗
