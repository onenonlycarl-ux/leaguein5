What everyone's talking about

Biggest Story

Chargers beat the Ravens 23-20 for their first win of the season

Los Angeles beat Baltimore on Monday night after eight straight losses. The Chargers scored on their first drive, never trailed and played their first turnover-free game of the year. Jim Harbaugh said he wants to win every week.

Source: ESPN ↗

Injury Watch

Ravens lose two linemen as Lamar Jackson stays on injured reserve

Jackson is out with a thumb injury, so Jacoby Brissett remains the starter. Right guard Vega Ioane (shoulder) and defensive tackle Nnamdi Madubuike (hamstring) left Monday's game and did not return. Baltimore visits Carolina on Sunday.

Source: Baltimore Ravens ↗

Player Spotlight

Ja'Marr Chase catches 11 passes for 231 yards against Pittsburgh

Chase torched the Steelers with 11 receptions and 231 yards in the Bengals' 38-18 win. He now sits 122 yards short of a sixth straight 1,000-yard season, and Cincinnati plays Washington on Monday night.

Source: ESPN ↗

Stat of the Day

+125: Jaguars own the NFL's best point differential

Jacksonville also leads the league with a +9 turnover margin, and Trevor Lawrence has 21 touchdown passes against 5 interceptions. A 30-point game at the Giants today would give the Jaguars a franchise-record fifth in a row.

Source: TSN ↗

What's Next

Packers visit the Rams on Netflix the night before Thanksgiving

Green Bay is on a bye this week, then plays at SoFi Stadium on Wednesday at 8 p.m. ET. It is only the second Wednesday regular-season game in franchise history. Watch Micah Parsons' status.

Source: Packers.com ↗
