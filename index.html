What everyone's talking about

Biggest Story

Bills smother Vikings 23-6 to reach 7-1

Buffalo held the ball for 42:32 on Monday night and forced three turnovers. Minnesota went 0-for-7 on third down. The Bills have won four straight and visit the Jets on Sunday.

Source: Buffalo Bills ↗

Injury Watch

Mahomes day to day with a sore throwing elbow

Andy Reid said David Bailey's hit late in the first half left Mahomes sore and called the news more positive than negative. Receiver Xavier Worthy (hamstring) and tackle Jaylen Moore (ankle) are also day to day. Kansas City visits Atlanta on Sunday.

Source: KSHB ↗

Player Spotlight

Jordan Love throws for 372 and accounts for four scores

Love threw three touchdowns and ran for another in Green Bay's 45-27 win at New England. Christian Watson (171 yards) and Matthew Golden (164) each topped 150 yards and scored. The Packers have won five of their last six.

Source: Associated Press ↗

Stat of the Day

5: turnovers in a Bears win, the first since 2006

Chicago gave the ball away five times and still beat Tampa Bay 27-24 on a last-second Cairo Santos field goal. It was the Bears' fourth straight win. They are on bye this week.

Source: AP via Atlanta Journal-Constitution ↗

What's Next

Panthers visit Saints with first place in the NFC South up for grabs

Both teams are 3-5 and every club in the division has three wins. Carolina plays at New Orleans on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on Fox. The winner takes sole possession of first.

Source: WLOX ↗
