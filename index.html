What everyone's talking about

Biggest Story

Patriots expect to be without Drake Maye the rest of the season

Safety Kevin Byard told WEEI that Maye is probably done for the year, and coach Mike Vrabel does not expect him back after the bye. Adam Schefter reported the team fears a fractured ankle, though New England has not confirmed a diagnosis.

Source: Boston.com ↗

Injury Watch

Puka Nacua stays on injured reserve until mid-December

Nacua sprained his MCL and PCL and has turf toe, and the Rams moved him to injured reserve Nov. 12. Coach Sean McVay said the timeline has not changed. He becomes eligible to return for Week 15 against the Cowboys.

Source: NBC Sports ↗

Player Spotlight

James Cook leads the AFC with 971 rushing yards

Cook has nine rushing touchdowns and tops every AFC back in yards. He now meets a Dolphins front missing Zach Sieler, Jordan Phillips and Zeek Biggers.

Source: 921 The Ticket ↗

Stat of the Day

1,207: Jaxon Smith-Njigba's receiving yards lead the NFL

Smith-Njigba also leads the league with 79 catches and 10 touchdowns with seven games left. No other receiver has reached 1,000 yards, and CeeDee Lamb is next at 976.

Source: Seattle Sports ↗

What's Next

Vikings and 49ers meet in Mexico City on Sunday night

Minnesota (7-2) visits San Francisco (8-1) at Estadio Banorte on Sunday, Nov. 22, at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. Watch Kirk Cousins against Raheem Morris's disguised coverages and how both teams handle the altitude.

Source: KSTP ↗
