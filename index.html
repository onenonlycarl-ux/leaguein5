What everyone's talking about

Biggest Story

Colts ship Keenan Allen and Josh Downs to contenders

Indianapolis sent Allen to San Francisco and Downs to Kansas City on Tuesday and took back picks in the fourth, fifth and sixth rounds of the 2027 draft. With Alec Pierce on injured reserve, the Colts are thin at receiver heading into Sunday's game against Miami.

Source: Colts.com ↗

Injury Watch

Mahomes practices fully after elbow scare

An MRI showed no major damage to the throwing elbow Mahomes hurt against the Jets. He was a full participant Friday with no game status. Running back Kenneth Walker is questionable with a groin injury and tackle Jaylon Moore is out.

Source: Chiefs.com ↗

Player Spotlight

CeeDee Lamb catches 10 for 195 and a record

Lamb scored once against Indianapolis. His 10 catches lifted his career total to 641, the most by any player before turning 28. He also lost a late fumble, so Dallas wants the yards without the mistakes against San Francisco.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

Stat of the Day

16: straight one-score wins for the Broncos, an NFL record

Denver has won 16 consecutive regular-season games decided by one score, the longest such streak in league history. The latest came against Carolina, when the Broncos took the lead in the fourth quarter. They are 7-2 and on their bye.

Source: DenverBroncos.com ↗

What's Next

Patriots and Lions kick off in Munich at 9:30 a.m. ET

New England plays the 6-2 Lions on FOX at FC Bayern Munich Stadium. Detroit's offense averages 27.8 points, fifth in the league, against a Patriots pass defense ranked 25th. Watch Jared Goff and Jahmyr Gibbs.

Source: Patriots.com ↗
