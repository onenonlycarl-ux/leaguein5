What everyone's talking about

Biggest Story

Micah Parsons is cleared to play the Rams, 346 days after his ACL tear

The Packers activated Parsons on Tuesday, and he can play tonight on Thanksgiving Eve. Green Bay is 6-4 and last in the NFC North. Parsons had 12.5 sacks last season in 14 games.

Source: CBS Sports ↗

Injury Watch

Mahomes plays through elbow and knee problems before Buffalo

Patrick Mahomes has thrown for 672 yards and eight touchdowns over his last two games despite the injuries. He said last week he has not had the burst he wants. The Chiefs are 8-2 and visit Buffalo on Thanksgiving night.

Source: Yahoo Sports ↗

Player Spotlight

Dak Prescott throws for 325 yards and four touchdowns against Tennessee

Prescott also scored on a red-zone scramble. CeeDee Lamb caught 11 of 12 targets for 132 yards, and George Pickens added seven catches for 102 yards. The Cowboys host the Eagles on Thanksgiving.

Source: ESPN ↗

Stat of the Day

6: straight touchdown drives to open the Cowboys' win over Tennessee

Dallas scored a touchdown on each of its first six possessions Sunday, the longest such start in the franchise's play-by-play era. The Cowboys reached the end zone seven times in all.

Source: CBS Sports ↗

What's Next

Bears at Lions on Thanksgiving with first place in the NFC North on the line

Both teams are 8-2, and the winner takes sole possession of first. Kickoff is Thursday at 1 p.m. ET on CBS at Ford Field. Caleb Williams is 0-4 against Detroit in his career.

Source: 104.5 The Zone ↗
